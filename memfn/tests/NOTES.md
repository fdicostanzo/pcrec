## The part selector (R-39, lane r39)

`run_g2.sh --quick --part K/N` runs part K of N of the quick tier; `g2_parts.py gather` judges the N logs. Requested for `[CLOUD-SUITE]`: `test-memfn-g2` was the longest `make test` section (1,212 s over 3 CI runs) and one section cannot be split across runners.

- UNIT: a block of `run_g2.sh` that runs, judges and reports alone. Today (g2_units.tsv): `gcc` (the foreground answer run), `asan`, `w1w3` (the sampled build, W1 x10, W3), `w2-1..16` (one mutation each: generator, build, mutants run), `k1` (libc record + both K1 builds), `simd-facts`/`simd-answers`/`simd-paths`/`simd-plants-a`/`simd-plants-b` (r39b: `run_g2_simd.py --arms ARM`; each arm renders the space and runs the fact checks quietly, only `facts` counts them), `win`, `any`, `rows` (the MISMATCH-only and STRIDE-only generators, the plain-library control, section 4b's (f)/(g) and controls). The generator stage (the PRELUDE) runs in every part and is no unit.
- RULE: units sorted by declared weight (heaviest first, table order on ties) are dealt to the part with the least weight so far (lowest number on ties). No run-time state. Weights are seconds from the table's `weight_source` column; a wrong weight costs balance, never coverage. Re-weight from the `G2UNIT name seconds` lines of a split run.
- MANIFEST: a part prints `G2UNIT name seconds` when a unit's judge ran (a unit that died earlier is missing, not counted); `G2_UNITS_OUT=FILE` writes the same lines from an unsplit run (no stdout change). `gather` checks every expected unit appears in exactly one part, and that each part ran what the rule deals it.
- COUNTS over the population: section 4b's (a) REACH_DROPPED 0, (b) every row chosen, (c) distinct rows >= `FLOOR_ROWS`, (d) every process printed REACH lines, (e) `G2_ROW_FLOORS` (`row_floors.tsv`) are sums over every G2 process, so a part defers them: it prints `G2PART rowchosen-prelude|other TABLE ROW N`, `reach_procs`, `reach_dropped`, `floor_rows` and `gather` judges. The prelude generator's REACH lines are in every part and counted once (its lines must agree across parts: the determinism check). (f)/(g) and the controls are per-unit facts (the `rows` unit) and stay in the part. Every other floor in `run_g2.sh` judges a unit's own output and holds inside it.
- TOTALS: `gather` prints `checks passed/failed` as one run would: sum over parts, minus (N-1) x the replicated prelude and section-5 tallies (`G2PART prelude_passed`, `replicated_failed`), plus its own (a)-(e).
- `--units LIST` narrows the population to a slice (a diagnostic; judged like a part, (b)/(c)/(e) not judged). `G2_JOBS=N` sets the build parallelism (default: online CPUs).
- Plants: `g2_parts_selftest.sh` gathers clean fixtures green (N = 1..4) and red under a missing unit, a duplicate, an extra, a misdealt unit, a row one under its floor, an absent row, a zero row, REACH_DROPPED, a no-REACH process, a red part, a prelude that differs.
- Re-weight (r39b): `python3 -I memfn/tests/g2_parts.py reweight --table memfn/tests/g2_units.tsv --source 'TEXT' gather.log [part logs]` rewrites the weight and source of every unit the log measured (`G2UNIT` lines or gather's `units a:1s` lines); `predict --table T 4 8` prints the dealt parts' summed weights. Weights are job 561's walls on the 16-core dev box, a 4-core runner's ratios may differ; re-measure from a CI gather.log.
- Limit: `gcc` is one single-threaded driver run (weight 350 of ~1,100): N >= 3 cannot shrink the longest part below it. Splitting it needs the driver's coverage cells and `*_min` census lines made mergeable (raw counters, not the printed minima) — not done.
