# /close — find a session-reset opportunity

Frank wants the session reset. Your job is to find the right moment for it and get
there. HOW to close is not in this skill. The close procedure belongs to the manager
skill you are running (for pcrec: `pcrec-manager` §6; for the kit:
`pcrec-memfn-manager`'s own close section). Follow that procedure as written. Add
nothing to it here.

## What to do

1. **Read Frank's arguments first.** Anything he adds after `/close` OVERRIDES the
   default below: urgency ("now", "house on fire"), a deadline, or any other
   modification. Do what he says, at the speed he says.
2. **With no modifier, wait for the next natural opportunity.** That is the point
   where closing loses the least: a lane delivery accepted, a merge done, a run's
   verdict read, a ruling recorded. Do not start new work. Do not take on work that
   would push the opportunity further out.
3. **Before acting, tell Frank in one or two lines** what you are waiting for (or
   that you are closing now), so he knows when the reset will be safe.
4. **Then run your manager skill's close procedure.** When it is done, say so plainly.
   Frank does the shutdown himself.
