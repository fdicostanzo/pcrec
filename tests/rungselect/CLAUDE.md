# tests/rungselect — the [ENG-BREP] REVERSE-DETERMINISTIC rung's validation

`docs/design/engine_m4.md` §2.5 and `docs/design/eng_brep_design.md` §3 are the
design; `docs/design/rungselect_impl/rungselect_design.md` is the emitted-shape
sketch; `src/opt/revdet.c` is the analysis; `src/gen/emit_vm.c` is what acts on
it. This directory is the evidence that it changes no answer.

**Not a module directory.** The rung is an emission strategy over the base tier,
not a regex feature, so no block here carries a `features` directive and there
is no module gate to open. It gets its own directory for the reason
`tests/possessify/` has one: what it needs to assert is not expressible as
`.rxt` expectations.

## The three checks see three different things, and none replaces another

- **`rungselect.rxt`** pins what each pattern MATCHES. It is structurally BLIND
  to the rung itself: a quantifier emitted as one body copy plus a backward walk
  and the same quantifier replicated `n` times match identically, which is the
  entire claim. Every expectation was produced by BOTH oracles (python3 `re` and
  libpcre2) and agreed — 0 disagreements over the whole file.
- **`run_rungdiff.sh`** — the row's PRIMARY instrument (§5.1). Same pattern
  compiled twice, once with the rung and once with `-fno-revdet`, both artifacts
  linked into ONE translation unit, and every subject compared at every start
  position on the span, EVERY capture slot and the FAILURE SURFACE. Denying the
  rung drops the quantifier to the frames rung, which for a bounded repeat is
  LITERAL REPLICATION — not an approximation of `{m,n}` but `{m,n}` unrolled,
  which is what ships today — so the denied build is the semantic ground truth
  and a disagreement is a bug by construction. It carries its own NON-VACUITY
  control: a sweep in which no pattern took the rung compared identical
  artifacts and measured nothing, and says so as a failure.
- **`run_rungselect_tests.sh`** — that the rung was SELECTED where the stamp
  says, NOWHERE when denied (D47.3's do-or-die, asserted against the artifact
  and never against the flag having been passed), that every corpus pattern the
  rung does not apply to emits BYTE-IDENTICAL C, and that the promised size and
  capacity wins are real. Nothing else in the tree asserts any of these.

## Files

- **`rungselect.rxt`** — GENERATED by
  `docs/design/rungselect_impl/make_corpus.py`, never hand-edited. One block per
  distinct emitted shape (m == 0, m > 0, m == n, unbounded, greedy, lazy,
  possessified, not) plus one per DECLINE REASON, plus the mixed artifacts.
  Every pattern is capture-bearing, which is not decoration: a capture-free
  pattern routes to the DFA under the default engine choice and never reaches
  `src/gen/emit_vm.c`, so a rung would be structurally invisible to it. That is
  the defect the possessification lane measured on its own first corpus (33 of
  38 patterns DFA-routed, three carrying the feature under test), and `.rxt`
  blocks carry no engine flag, so the pattern text has to force the VM itself.
- **`patterns.txt`** — GENERATED by
  `docs/design/rungselect_impl/gen_patterns.py`, the differential's designed
  population: bodies × counts × follows × BOTH preferences. The generator's
  `--full` mode widens every axis for a deeper sweep run by hand; the committed
  narrow set keeps at least one member of every distinct emitted shape, which is
  the property that matters, and `--full` adds redundancy within those shapes
  rather than new ones.
- **`run_rungdiff.sh`**, **`run_rungselect_tests.sh`** — the two suites, wired
  into `make test` as `make test-rungselect` and into the `make ubsan`/`make
  asan` both-axes batteries. Every generated-code compile goes through `gen_cc`
  and every matcher run through `gen_run` (`tests/lib/gen_timeout.sh`,
  `WATCHDOG_SECTION=rungdiff`/`rungselect`).

  `run_rungdiff.sh` REUSES `tests/possessify/possdiff_driver.c` rather than
  keeping a second copy. The comparison is identical for every member of
  D47.3's deny family — two artifacts of one pattern must agree on span, every
  slot and the failure surface — and only the words in the divergence report
  differ, which come in through `-DDIFF_A_LABEL`/`-DDIFF_B_LABEL`.

## The count ceiling is 64, and that is a property of the GROUND TRUTH

§5.1 says to keep the differential's counts below the replication knee and
suggests N ≤ 256. On this branch the binding constraint is tighter and it is
`PCREC_MAX_VM_REPEAT_COPIES = 64`, because the DENIED build is the one that
replicates. A pattern whose `{m,n}` exceeds that compiles on the rung and is
REFUSED on the ground truth, and `run_rungdiff.sh` reports that as a FAILURE
rather than skipping it — a ground truth that cannot be built is exactly the
blindness §5.1 discloses, and it should be visible rather than quietly stepped
over. Above the cap, `rungselect.rxt` and the oracles are what check the rung.

## What the corpus-wide byte-identity gate is really asserting

Not "nothing changed" — a great deal changes, and that is the point. The gate is
that the change is confined to the quantifiers the rung actually selects:
patterns with no rung-selected quantifier must emit the SAME BYTES with the rung
on and off, so the artifacts of everything the analysis declines are the
artifacts of today. That is what makes the rung safe to ship, held as a gate
rather than promised, and it is the §5.4 technique the possessification lane
used one rung up.

## The nested fixed-count quantifier, and why it is spelled with an x and a y

The rung admits exactly one kind of nested quantifier — an EXACT count — and it
is the only shape that exercises the backward emitter's own replication arm, so
both the corpus and the differential carry it deliberately. It is spelled
`(?:x((a)|b){2}y){0,3}z`, with the repeat in the MIDDLE of the body, and that is
measured rather than stylistic: the Glushkov construction models `{2}` as a LOOP
(it links last to first whenever `rmax > 1`), so a fixed repeat at either END of
a body gives that end's positions a back edge and prefix-freeness fails in that
direction. `(?:(?:a|b){2}c){0,3}d` therefore declines, and it declines as a
MODELLING artifact rather than a real ambiguity — `c(a|b)(a|b)` is fixed-length
and perfectly decodable. Recorded in the design file's residual list as
something a sharper construction would recover.

## The lesson this directory inherits rather than re-pays

**A subject generator can silently measure nothing** (D47.6, and
`tests/possessify/CLAUDE.md`'s account of paying for it twice). Subjects here
are built FROM THE PATTERN'S OWN CHARACTERS. The one place this suite's
generator deliberately differs from the possessification suite's is the RUN
LENGTH: it goes to twelve where that one stops at eight, because this rung's
family carries counts up to eight over bodies up to two bytes wide, and n+1
iterations of a two-byte body is eighteen subject bytes. A sweep that stops at
eight cannot reach the upper boundary of its own family — the same failure mode
as a missing alphabet character, one axis over.
