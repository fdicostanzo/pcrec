### EXAMPLES-FLAGS-1 — CONFIRMED — the "as text" spelling `(?^ims-subset:...)` is not the ruled rule: it does not reset `U`, and a text paste breaks the inserted literal's own parse
- Location: R.1.14 HOLE-READING (1), the [REV 29 ...] bracket, draft line 1926 ("\"as text\" now means the inserted literal spelt `(?^ims-subset:...)` at the hole ... so the `x` cases above no longer arise").
- Severity: doc (the sentence) + design (the dropped rev 28.1 reading is still needed). Effort: small (one sentence: "the inserted literal is PARSED on its own, then placed as a group with the inherited flags; `(?^...)` is a model only where the paste is context-free"). Blast radius: R.1.14 (1), R.1.15 header, §R.8 flags-scope row, every later reader who builds the spelling from the sentence.
- Repro (libpcre2 10.46 via pcre2h, `probe1.py`, `probe2.py` in this dir):
  (a) `U`: `(?U)x(?^:a+)` SEARCHED `xaaa` gives `xa`@0; the ruled answer (U not inherited, draft's own run) is `xaaa`@0, which needs `(?U)x(?-U:(?^:a+))` (the MODEL's `spell()` does this; the DRAFT's sentence does not say it). `(?^)` resets i m n s x xx (and `r`, see -2), never `U`.
  (b) a WRITTEN `(?x)` inside the inserted literal with a trailing comment: `/x${/(?x)a #c/}/` as text `x(?^:(?x)a #c)` is a PCRE COMPILE ERROR ("missing closing parenthesis"; also at depth 2: `(?i)x(?^i:y(?^i:(?x)a #c))`), while the literal alone `/(?x)a #c/` is legal. "A flag it writes for itself wins" (RULED) makes this exactly the rev 28.1 `#` case the bracket says "no longer arise[s]". Its own parse `(?i)x(?:(?x)a )`: WHOLE `XA` HOLDS / SEARCHED `z XA` `XA`@2 / FIND-ALL `xa XA` [`xa`@0, `XA`@3].
  (c) an unterminated `\Q` (legal PCRE: `/a\Qb/` WHOLE `ab` HOLDS): pasted `x(?^:a\Qb)` is a COMPILE ERROR.
  (d) `(?R)` in the inserted literal (self-recursion is "plain PCRE inside the literal", §5.4 line 6336, §6 line 6575): own-literal reading `x(?:(?<r>a(?&r)?b))` vs the paste `x(?^:a(?R)?b)` differ on 2 / 8 / 8 of 1,093 texts (`xab`, length <= 6); `/x${/a(?R)?b/}/` WHOLE `xaabb`: own HOLDS, paste FAILS (it recurses the OUTER pattern, so `xaxabb` HOLDS). A numbered call `(?1)` likewise binds the outer group 1 (`(z)-(?^:(a)(?1))` WHOLE `z-az` HOLDS, `z-aa` FAILS). The draft names `\k<h>` and `\1` under "its own group numbering"; `(?R)`, `(?N)`, `(?&name)` are unnamed.
- Expected: one sentence that the inserted literal is parsed alone (comments, `\Q`, its group numbers and recursion are its own) and inherits only `i m s`; `(?^ims:...)` plus `(?-U:...)` plus renumbering is then a MODEL of that, never "the" text. Rev 28.1's reading ("parsed on its own, its comment ending at the literal's end") should be KEPT, not retired.
