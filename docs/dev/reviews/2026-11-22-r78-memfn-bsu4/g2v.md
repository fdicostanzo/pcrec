### G2V-4 nit (maintenance; effort small) -- duplication: the two new drivers copy each other's guard/fault scaffold; the generator copies the family's sink/arena/buf

### G2V-5 nit (coverage; effort trivial) -- the "every member, cycled" fill degenerates when the member count is a multiple of 7
g2_vspan.py:289/295 (and the driver's :131) place byte i = fill[(i*7 + fseed) % nf]; with nf = len(memb) and 7 | nf the window cycles through nf/7 members only. `\w` has 63 members: a "cycled" `\w` window holds 9 of them. Coverage only (the sweeps still put every byte at 4 positions). Use a stride coprime to nf (e.g. 1, or the next coprime).
