### EXAMPLES-1 — prefix_in places S exactly as quoted does, yet the convention exempts it as a "set argument"; its piece says nothing, and the model's census never counts it
CONFIRMED. Severity: design (a convention exemption that contradicts the convention's own definition of a spot). Effort: small (either give prefix_in's body the list or state in both pieces why a set argument in a hole is not a spot). Blast radius: lib/convention/argument-labels, lib/sets/prefix-in, lib/modifiers/quoted; model PLACES.
- The convention (library.md [lib/convention/argument-labels]): "The spot is each place a body lays its argument into its pattern" and "'Places' leaves out ...: an argument used only as a filter (the right side of `&` in a body) or under a complement ..., the set arguments (`between`, `prefix_in`, the S of `length` and `month_numbered`), the natives with no body."
- prefix_in's body (library.md [lib/sets/prefix-in]): "`prefix_in(n, S)` accepts a text whose first n characters are a member of S: `/${S & /(?s:.){${n}}/}(?s:.*)/`." S is laid into a pattern hole, as the LEFT side of an `&` (not "the right side of `&`", not under a complement).
- quoted's body (library.md [lib/modifiers/quoted]) has the same shape, `/"${[(=) = S] & /[^"]*/}"/`, and its piece says: "S is the pattern side of the `&` (placed), so its labels pass on; the filter `/[^"]*/` is the body's own."
- Same shape, opposite treatment. libpcre2 confirms S's groups are set in that position: `(?:(?<a>ab)c|xy)(?s:.*)` (prefix_in(3, /(?<a>ab)c|xy/) lowered) on `abcz` matches with a = `ab`; so `prefix_in(3, /(?<a>ab)c|xy/) & (prefix_in.a: "ab")` is legal by the spot definition and a compile error by the exemption.
- "unless its piece says otherwise": the prefix_in piece says nothing about labels at all (nor does between's). The exception lives only in the convention.
- The evidence does not see it: predspot_model.py PLACES builds its census from `BODY_RX`/`BODY_RX2`; prefix_in's sentence ("accepts a text whose ...: `...`") matches neither, so the census (predspot_model.out lines 22-57: 34 pairs) has no prefix_in row, and `SET_ARGS = {("between",), ("prefix_in",)}` is declared but never reached. "a parameter placed alone in a hole: none" is therefore not evidence for prefix_in (a population nobody counts).
- Fix direction: prefix_in's S gets `[(=) = S]` (and the exemption drops it), or both pieces state the reason a set argument laid in a hole is not a spot (then quoted needs the same reason against it).
