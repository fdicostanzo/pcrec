### READY-B-12 — §2.2's round-trip projection omits a cross-hook TEXT relation the gate reads: `miss` equal to `n` (CONFIRMED; design/doc; effort XS; blocks 3.0b's seam text and row AD's fixture)

## Verdict
3.0b: **not ready**, decided by **READY-B-4**. The kit agreement must compare "every field ... built from what the define stored, never through reply_of or the plan function". At a678d0a4e `site_rec` stores nothing the decision group could be built from, and on E-func (the offset-skip define) it stores no plan at all. So a builder must either add a record the design does not list or take a shortcut the design forbids. That is a design choice the kit manager has to rule; a lane cannot settle it. Once it is ruled (a listed `site_rec` decision record plus a field map, or a stated narrowing of the memcmp with its control named), 3.0b becomes **ready with listed text fixes**. **READY-B-1** comes first among those fixes (the purity reach population patterns and plant files for the Table E/B/S rows). The others are -3, -5, -6, -7, -9, -11 and -12, which change sabotage rows, the seam text or the D27 cell's inputs, then -2, -8 and -10, which are records and counts.
