## Findings (consolidated)

### A. Refusals through a lent channel are counted but the count is never read; four places claim "allocates OR refuses" (LIFE-1; design/doc; CONFIRMED by reading)
A refusal through an outer Ctx goes `pcrec_ctx_fail(pcrec_err_chan(cx), ...)`, which counts the use and then longjmps to `cx->jb`, the OUTER setjmp (compile.c:2593). That happens before `walk_part`'s `pcrec_ctx_unlend` (compound.c), so the inner compile strands exactly as K163 did, and `lent` is left set on the outer Ctx. The count therefore covers the ALLOCATING half of the class only. In arena.c and sb.c, 5 refusal-only channel sites are invisible (`reg_fail`, `pcrec_scope_calloc` overflow, `sb_grow`'s abort_over, `sb_simd_fail`, `pcrec_sb_adopt`), plus the 16 direct `sb->cx` refusals (enc.c 5, vm_stmt.c 11). Today's population is 0: no outer Arena, StrBuf or Ctx crosses the part seam (LIFE-4's struct check). The overclaim appears in four places: the design's FIX SHAPE, the design's Discipline row "K163 class", the K163 "Fixed" paragraph, and internal.h's `Ctx.lent` comment.
- RULED: **FIX-NOW** (doc narrowing only; done in lane f4fix). Narrow the four claim sites to "allocates", and name the refusal-only half as covered by the member's allocating companion and by the seam rule (B5). Option for the manager to rule: LIFE-1's ~5-line general form. At the top of compile_driver's setjmp arm, a lent Ctx means a refusal crossed a lent window: diagnose it as a named internal error and clear `lent`. This turns every refusal-shaped member from silent into named. It adds one src reader of `lent`, beyond D-7's "arena.c only" reader line, so the reader line and the scanner rule move with it. RULING: the handler-top check at compile_driver's setjmp arm is NOT taken.

## Lessons for the guide (ACCEPTED; added to docs/dev/guide/critics.md)
- A counted-use detector is a NORMAL-RUN detector. When a fix counts uses of a channel to refuse later, every refusal-only path through that channel longjmps before the count is read. A check-adequacy or lifetime lens asks "which uses are seen only on the failing run?" (r110 A).
- A control whose population is 0 by construction after the fix needs a plant that makes it SWEEP (the member back AND the fix's own refusal neutered). A plant that only restores the member is caught by the refusal first and never reaches the control (r110 B2: S3653 vs V2).
- Every unconditional "count here" line in a counted class is its own site; a plant per class is not a plant per line (r110 B1).
