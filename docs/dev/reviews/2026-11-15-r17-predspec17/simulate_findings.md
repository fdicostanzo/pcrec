## Findings

### SIMULATE-1: the strict derived forms `P & !lex_le2(s, lo)` / `P & !lex_ge2(s, hi)` invert on an UNSET label: one match is both "strictly above" and "strictly below" the same bound
CONFIRMED. Severity: answer (a stated derived form gives a wrong answer by the draft's own rules). Effort: small (a sentence restricting the derived forms to labels that are always set, or deriving strict forms without `!`, e.g. a strict-last-entry body). Blast radius: §8 TUPLE COMPARISONS "THE FAMILY IS SMALL ON PURPOSE" (rev17 l.2360-2362), caveat (5) (l.2382-2383), changelog rev 16 (A) "strict comparisons and equality are derived by `!` and `&`".
Rules used: §3.5 (array of macros = any one entry), §2.1 typing (`!M` filters as "neither"), §4.2 "A comparison on an UNSET label is false (its `!` true)". With `P = /(?<h>\d{1,2})(?::(?<m>\d\d))?/` (minutes optional) and lo = hi = [9, 30], subject `9` (m unset): `lex_le2(s,[9,30])` = h<9 (false) | h==9 & m<=30 (m unset: false) = false, so `P & !lex_le2` ACCEPTS `9` as strictly above 9:30; likewise `lex_ge2(s,[9,30])` is false so `P & !lex_ge2` ACCEPTS `9` as strictly below 9:30. Caveat (5) says an unset label "makes its conditions false ..., so the compare rejects that match": under `!` it ACCEPTS it. Repro: `$R/simulate/run_b.py` (draft-text emulation `$R/simulate/emu.py`, python `re`): prints `strictly above AND strictly below 9:30: ['9']`. The evidence's (D) table cannot see this: every author pattern in predspec17_check.py (HM, PAST, HMS, VERS) sets every label in every whole match, so the "above/below 0 differ" rows hold only for always-set labels; the text states the derived forms without that condition. (The same inversion applies to caveat (4)'s non-digit label text: `!lex_le2` accepts it.)

### SIMULATE-2: caveat (5) "an UNSET label ... makes its conditions false, so the compare rejects that match" is false for the plain compare too: an unset LATER field is never consulted when an earlier field decides
CONFIRMED. Severity: doc (the bodies are right; the caveat's sentence about them is wrong). Effort: one sentence ("rejects that match when the compare reaches the unset label's entry, i.e. when every earlier field equals the bound"). Blast radius: §8 caveat (5), rev17 l.2382-2383.
Repro: same P as SIMULATE-1 (`/(?<h>\d{1,2})(?::(?<m>\d\d))?/`), `lex_between2([(h:), (m:)], [9, 30], [11, 0])` on subject `10` (m unset): lex_ge2 entry 1 `h > 9` is true, lex_le2 entry 1 `h < 11` is true, m never read: ACCEPT. `$R/simulate/run_b.py` prints `lex_between2 [9,30]..[11,0] accepts with m UNSET: ['10']`. Whether accepting `10` is the desired answer is the author's business; the caveat as written promises a rejection the bodies do not give, and `label.unset` is then the only signal.

### SIMULATE-3: the wrong-length COMPILE ERROR (caveat (2)) is not what the bodies give by the draft's rules, and table (W)'s "refusal" is the emulator's own `raise`, not evidence
CONFIRMED (text + script). Severity: design (a claimed error with no mechanism; the evidence row is a control sharing a source with what it controls). Effort: small to state (name the mechanism: an `@assert` on the arrays' sizes in the body, or a rule that a `lex_*N` macro checks its argument sizes) and drop "table (W) shows the refusal". Blast radius: §8 caveat (2) l.2377-2378; "plain macros" claim (§0 rulings table l.375).
By the text: the six are PLAIN MACROS whose bodies index `s[k]`, `lo[k]`; §3.2 tool 1: "Out of range = entry absent: tests on it are false". So `lex_ge3([(h:), (m:)], [9, 0, 0])` is, by the rules, `h>9 | h==9 & m>0 | (h==9 & m==0 & <absent: false>)` = strictly above 9:00, silently, with NO error (emulated: `9:00` rejected, `9:01` accepted, `$R/simulate/run_b.py`); and `lex_ge2` given a 3-entry projection simply ignores entry 3. A too-short BOUND (`lex_ge2(s, [9])`) puts an absent entry as a comparison atom (`padded(>=lo[2])`), which no section defines. The analogy cited ("as `each`'s zip of unequal arrays, §5.3") is a rule of the `each` primitive, which these bodies do not call. In predspec17_check.py the refusal is `bound()`'s own `if len(proj) != len(b): raise ValueError(...)` (l.~95), so (W)'s "refused: wrong-length array: 2 vs 3 (a compile error)" can only ever print; it tests the emulator, not the draft.

### SIMULATE-4: bounds written the way times are written, `[09, 00]`, are a silent look-alike of `[9, 0]` under §2.4's "unless the literal has them": `9:30` drops out of 9:00..10:00

Repro: `$R/simulate/run_c.py`: `/(?<h>\d{1,2}):(?<m>\d\d)/ & lex_between2([(h:), (m:)], [09, 00], [10, 00])`: `9:30` and `9:00` REJECT (h=`9`: `padded(==09)` = `0*09` needs the two-digit `09`; `padded(>09)` = `0*(10..99)`), `09:30` accepts; with `[9, 0]` all four accept.
