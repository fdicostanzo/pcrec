## Headline

**As written, the bake-off cannot be trusted to decide the question.** It is a strong design in its
bones (held-out population, prereg, a salted split, cross-fit oracle, planted-failure controls, honest
early stops), but the round found, and the verifier held:

1. **Five ways to reach a WRONG verdict** (answer level): option (a) is defined as J and is also a
   crowned candidate (B1); the oracle would be timed on the bench's REPEATED loop, the regime the
   design itself says flatters the walk and flips decisions (B2); the early stop "PRICING DOES NOT
   PAY" leans on a blind-spot control that is vacuous without the kit lever, which F7 recommends
   proceeding without (B3); the verdict table reads a site-count tie before the mean, so a 1.8x loss
   on 5 % of sites is a TIE (B4); the census lever L-ROW, "no wrongness risk", lands rows the code
   calls undeniable for soundness and the give-up contract (B5).
2. **The verdict table is mis-ordered against the design's own prediction**: the outcome §9.1
   predicts (priced options agree, MN worse) lands on INCONCLUSIVE or UNRESOLVABLE, never on the
   intended "NUMBERS TIE, architecture decides"; and at H-open ~110 with the 15-site floor, the
   (a′)-vs-(d) pair is UNDERPOWERED by construction (B4).
3. **Several controls cannot go red for the reason they exist, or go red on honest data**: C-FM
   (near-tautological, needs an "optional" lever), C-PRICE (a pooled Spearman passes at zero
   within-site skill), C-BLIND (reach under its own bar), C7 (red on honest ties, with 5 trials split
   3/2) and C-REPRO (all-rows agreement fails honestly; no band exists in the store today) (B10-B13).
4. **The bench mapping is not what §5 assumes**: calibration is per SUBJECT (the run is ~20 h of
   timed work, not ~40 min), arms of one site are separate runs (no paired trials), the trace-build
   levers cannot reach the PINNED tier, and the scratch tier is never stored (B14).

Every item is a design-text fix (most small), plus five bench questions and two Frank questions. No
finding argues against the bake-off itself; several argue for reordering it so the expensive
per-box stage is not paid before the cheap early stop (B18).

**Recommendation:** no build go on rev 0. A rev 1 applying the FIX-NOW items, with the §9.3 table
drilled on constructed outcome vectors (including the design's own §9.1 prediction) before it is
pre-registered, then a SHORT re-check round (fix-induced + simulate). The bench questions (B2, B14)
should go out now: their answers decide whether the bench or grid1 is the primary harness, which
changes §5 wholesale.

## Findings and proposed dispositions

### Answer level (could reach a wrong verdict)

- **B1 (PREMISE-5, SIM-5, MEAS-9) option (a) is J and is also a row 3-6 candidate.** §2/§0/F4 say
  (a)'s case is architectural, not numeric; §9.3's preamble lists (a) among "the options". Then (a)
  can be crowned "on numbers" (verdict drill S5 → `row5 (a) WINS`), and any real option that picks
  what J picks can never be δ below (a), so row 5 cannot crown it; row 3's "largest placement-loss
  difference" includes (a)'s identical 0. Verifier: HOLDS; with (a) removed, a Dx that is genuinely
  better on 20 sites reads `row5 Dx WINS` (with (a) present: row 7). SIM-5's 12-site example
  overstates (12 < 15 is UNDERPOWERED either way).
  **FIX-NOW**: (a)/J out of the candidate set, reported as the model reference; (a)'s architectural
  case enters only through §9.4. One sentence plus the preamble.

- **B2 (PREMISE-3, MEAS-1, COST-2 part 1) the bench times a REPEATED loop; the design requires
  STREAMED.** pcrec-bench `docs/methodology.md:74,76` ("times a loop of repeated calls to the same
  compiled matcher", per-subject calibration), `harness_contract.md:146-154`, `testees/pcrec/timed.h`
  (one buffer, an iteration count). A 64 KiB window is walked ~4,000 times in a row: regime_eval's
  `rep64k`, which it measured cutting branch misses ~100x and ruled "UNGATED" (regime_eval.md
  :216-218). The no-search/walk-heavy arms (MN, R2.5's no-search row, FM's NEXT rows) are flattered.
  §5.1's "Its discipline is the one the bake-off wants" is false on the one regime axis §1 names as
  decision-flipping. Verifier: HOLDS for the ordinary regimes, and found what all three critics
  missed: bench schema v1.11 has a `call-stream` regime (`unusual_set_v1.md` §7, `gate_shape_v14.md`
  §3.8), one subject replayed as a declared call list, a candidate vehicle for a 64-window stream.
  **FIX-NOW**: a new bench question B7 ("can a cell stream N distinct windows per timed pass, e.g. as
  a `call-stream` list over the 64 windows?"); if no, grid1 (streamed) is the PRIMARY harness and
  COST-11 (b) is NOTED, as §5.1's fallback already says. Add a rep-vs-stream control column on a
  subsample so the regime is measured (regime_eval's P-PRED shape). This changes §5 enough that it
  should be answered before rev 1 freezes §5.

- **B3 (PREMISE-7, SIM-10, MEAS-5) row 1's "PRICING DOES NOT PAY" leans on a vacuous C-BLIND.**
  C-BLIND's population is 2 salted options per KIT site from L-KIT's dump (§3.5); without L-KIT
  (F7's leaning, "do not wait") it is 0 of 0 and "≤ 10 %" holds. With L-KIT, 2 draws detect a
  blind spot covering 5 % of options on 9.75 % of sites: under its own 10 % bar even if the model is
  blind on EVERY site. It has no planted failure, against §1 item 2's "every control in §8 has a
  planted failure". Row 0 does not say whether a NOT-RUN control is red. Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: C-BLIND's reach as a detection probability for a stated f, sized to it; random
  cross-path arms (salted deny sets) so the probe exists without L-KIT; row 1 needs a non-empty
  population above a prereg floor, else it reads "does not pay ON THE BACKEND SLOTS" or
  INCONCLUSIVE; row 0 states NOT RUN blocks every verdict that leans on that control; a planted
  failure (an option hidden from the model-chosen set).

- **B4 (SIM-6, SIM-7, SIM-14, MEAS-8, MEAS-10, MEAS-11) the verdict table's order and power.**
  Drilled with exact arithmetic on constructed H-open vectors (`simulate/bin/verdict.py`, read and
  re-run by the verifier):
  - Row 4 (site-count tie, ≥ 95 % of sites) precedes row 5 (mean): A1 losing 80 % on 5 of 110 sites
    (3.6 pp of mean regret, nearly 2δ) reads `row4 TIE` (S2).
  - The design's own §9.1 prediction (priced options = J, MN half-way, FM worse) reads `row7
    INCONCLUSIVE` (S1); all options identical reads `row3 UNRESOLVABLE` because 3 x (zero spread)
    is beaten by any model error (S1b). No row for "the top group ties, the rest lose".
  - At H-open ~110, the 15-site power floor means a pair must differ on 13.6 % of sites, while
    §9.1 predicts ≤ 5 % disagreement among A1/HY/Dx: the (a′)-vs-(d) pair is UNDERPOWERED by
    construction. At the stage-0 floor (60 H → 40 H-open) a pair must differ on 37.5 %.
  - An unreachable region: differing on 9 % of 220 sites by 10 % is powered, fails row 4 (91 %) and
    row 5 (0.9 pp), lands on row 7 "more sites", which cannot help; row 7's own label says
    "UNDERPOWERED".
  - Row 5's robustness clause is non-inferiority with no power condition: an underpowered Zen 1
    subset reads "robust" by default.
  - Strata: J1's balance gives 10-13 H-open sites per text class even if every question qualified,
    so no class reaches 15 and §7.3's sign test is vacuous on the axis §1 names as deciding; MISS
    bin ~6 sites; density/call shape are cell attributes in a site-counted rule.
  - Rows 1-5 never name the call shape or prior arm they read (four candidate numbers each); row
    4's per-site "§7.2 tests" are population tests.
  Verifier: HOLDS (all), noting that ~50 % pass probability at exactly δ is inherent to an "at least
  δ" test, not a defect.
  **FIX-NOW**: rewrite §9.3 as a declared first-match table with: (a)/J removed (B1); a top-group
  tie read before the model-error row; row 4 as a paired mean-equivalence (interval inside ±δ) plus
  a worst-site guard; row 3 per comparison; row 7 only on UNDERPOWERED, with J3's size DERIVED from
  a power calculation at §9.1's predicted disagreement rate (per class if the class strata are to
  gate, else pre-register the classes as ungated and name them); UNDERPOWERED applied to the
  robustness clause; the deciding reading per row named. Then **drill the table**: the prereg ships
  the constructed-vector drill (the critics' `verdict.py` is a seed) and its expected verdicts, the
  §9.1 prediction among them.

- **B5 (PREMISE-1, SIM-1) L-ROW by name is not "only what a flag deny does".** Rows without a deny
  bit are in part rows NO deny may reach: RETRY `exact`/`clamped`/`retry-anchored` (deny 0,
  `.contract = CG_FIXED`; `emit_dfa.c:10430-10447`: "nothing may make an exact-language hybrid's
  retry adaptive", past `clamped` "a give-up can become an answer"); and the `-bounded`/unbounded
  NEXT pairs share deny bits so no flag splits them, while the unbounded predicate does not exclude
  `views` (`:6599-6604`, `:4603-4605`). None is its slot's fallback, so §3.1's walk names them
  (35-pattern dry walk: `RETRY exact` 6x, `byte-class-bounded` 6x, `memchr-bounded` 2x, `clamped`
  1x). `foobar\d --engine=vm -fprefilter` stamps `RX_VM_RESEED "exact"`. Verifier: HOLDS; severity
  design (the defect is in the lever's spec; its effect is a hard stop or an unsound arm).
  **FIX-NOW**: L-ROW refuses any row with `.contract` set and denies by DENY CLASS (a `-bounded`
  row with its twin); C-LEVER gains a planted `RETRY:exact` deny that must be refused. The "no
  wrongness risk" argument is then true and should be restated on that basis.

- **B6 (PREMISE-6, SIM-9) the decomposition and J's role.** (1) A1/A1u/HY/KF/MN keep MARGIN 2/1
  (`memfn/src/window.c:669-684`, a PARKED bias); Dx/Dp/J are not stated either way (K4 could be read
  to include Dx's kit level): if they differ, "placement loss" between A1 and Dx includes a guard,
  not a seam. (2) Per cell 1+r = (1+m)(1+p); placement loss can be negative (a restricted pick can
  be measured faster than J's). (3) Row 2 reads only J vs FM, so "THE MODEL CANNOT BEAT FIRST-MATCH"
  can stop the bake-off while MN beats FM by 3 pp (S6). "Ceiling" (§0 item 2) is wrong: J is the
  model's optimum, not a measured bound. Verifier: HOLDS (MARGIN part PARTLY: ambiguous, not
  contradictory).
  **FIX-NOW**: MARGIN as a declared on/off dimension of every kit-level rule, the (a′)-vs-(d) pair
  read at equal MARGIN; signed placement loss as a difference of mean regrets; row 2 reads "the best
  option beats FM by less than δ"; drop "ceiling".

- **B7 (SIM-8, MEAS-20.4) model error is measured against a per-CELL oracle, but every rule picks
  once per SITE.** Where a site's best arm differs between cells (§5.2 says it does by construction:
  no-search wins DENSE, loses MISS), even a perfect model has regret: two-cell example, best single
  pick 30 % regret, counted as "model error"; it inflates row 3, and C-SCORE's ORACLE rule cannot
  score 0 under the one-pick rule (so a §8 control is red by construction, row 0). Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: a SITE oracle (best single arm per site, per site x class under P-SUBJ) as the
  reference for model error and for C-SCORE; the per-cell gap reported separately as the
  "compile-time choice" loss.

### Design level (cannot reach a verdict, or not trustworthy)

- **B8 (PREMISE-2, PREMISE-9, SIM-2, SIM-4) the census walk's scope.** The walk denies every
  non-fallback row on the path, including slots the design does not price: FINISH twins
  (`-fno-anchored-dfa` changes only `rx_match`, which neither call shape times; `foo\d+`: 10
  distinct artifacts, 5 of them twins), RECOVER doubling every NEXT arm (UUID pattern: 14 distinct
  under 6 flags), WINDOW/BOUND/PRESENCE-none. FINISH's totality is PER HAND (`emit_dfa.c:11394-11400`),
  but `cand_fallback` is hand-blind (`:10739-10747`), so the walk would deny the AT hand's last row
  (`search-from`) after `-fno-anchored-dfa`, a `finish-no-hand` failure. Conversely the walk never
  reaches the route pair (its levers are forces, not row denies): 0 of 35 sampled patterns got a
  route-pair arm, though `(?<=^|\W)\w+(?=\W|$)` has a valid `-fno-prefilter` arm; the "engine
  route" stratum and the route half of the backend argmin are empty. Arm counts (and §5.6's ~9)
  undercount. Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: walk only the priced slots §3.1 itself lists; reach dependent slots (RECOVER, FINISH)
  as the default of the upstream choice; seed the walk with each route-pair lever; dedupe on the
  bytes reachable from the TIMED entries; restate the arm prediction from a dry-run census before
  the bench sizes the run.

- **B9 (SIM-3, PREMISE-15 c/f) "the path" is not one row per slot.** One artifact holds several
  selected rows per (slot, route), keyed by asker and hand (`\b[A-Z]{4,}\b`: NEXT dfa
  `first-class-bounded` and `next-none`; FINISH `verify-at` and `report`); stamps share tokens across
  rows (four `handoff*` rows stamp `handoff`); `CandPath` holds routes, masks and LOCATE/RECOVER only
  (`emit_dfa.c:11828-11858`); D156 add. 2 generates LOCATE x FINISH stamps only, not
  `RX_DFA_PREFILTER`/`RX_REQ_WHY`/`RX_VM_RESEED`. Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: define the path as the set of CANDROW (slot, route, asker, hand) → row records; stamps
  as the independent cross-check only where a stamp names a unique row; correct the Words entry.

- **B10 (PREMISE-8, SIM-13, MEAS-18) C-FM and C-ENUM rest on an "optional" lever, on the default
  path only.** FM's valid rows exist only through L-VALID ("optional ... not needed for the bake-off's
  correctness"), which is absent from every view (§6.1), and covers the default path while
  predicates read other slots' selections under other deny bits (`emit_dfa.c:10675-10702`). The first
  valid row in table order IS `cand_select`'s pick, so the check compares the compiler with itself;
  without L-VALID, returning the empty-deny census arm satisfies C-FM. Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: L-VALID required, run per census arm; C-FM re-derives the landing row of EVERY census
  arm from (its deny set, the valid-row records, the table order) per CANDROW key; say plainly what
  C-FM can and cannot catch.

- **B11 (MEAS-4) C-PRICE's pooled rank correlation cannot fire.** Between-site scale dominates a
  pooled Spearman: at zero within-site skill it reads 0.88-1.00 while picks are at chance
  (synthetic, verifier re-ran). Mitigated in part by row 2 (a zero-skill J usually fails it).
  **FIX-NOW**: gate on a within-site statistic (mean per-site Spearman, or pick accuracy against a
  per-site chance baseline).

- **B12 (MEAS-6) C7 goes red on honest noise; its planted failure does not reach it.** With one A/A
  pair as the cell floor and 5 trials split 3/2, ~half of truly tied cells qualify and ~35 % of those
  flip oracle between halves (bar 5 %). Verifier: PARTLY: regime_eval's own threshold
  `NULL_c = max(AA_c, ν x ns_walk(c))` (regime_eval.md:540) would largely remove this, and §5.4 never
  defines ν; the planted failure (an A/A NULL check) cannot make C7 (a) or (b) red: holds.
  **FIX-NOW**: adopt regime_eval's NULL_c (or a pooled-noise floor) by name; derive C7(b)'s bar by
  simulation from measured noise, pre-registered; plant a C7 failure (one half's trials scaled).

- **B13 (PREMISE-12, MEAS-7, MEAS-13) C-REPRO and the card floor.** All 68 rows of the only set
  (`measure_store/zen4-grid1/1/set.tsv`) have `noise_e6 = -` and `n = -`; "every row within its own

  20 % relative A/A floor is undefined on signed/near-zero rows (four negative `kit.delta` rows;
  armread's VM C_match ~0). Verifier: HOLDS.
  **FIX-NOW**: `noise_e6` and `n` REQUIRED on every row the bake-off reads (K7 and the pcrec half);
  a per-row test at a pre-registered family-wise level with a measured between-window term; the
  planted offset sized at the band's edge; the card floor stated against the row's contribution to a
  price at H's rates, not its own value.

  (4) "never in `store/`" (`testees/pcrec/CLAUDE.md:60`), so the D sweep (K-fit, C-PRICE's stop,
  C-FM/C-KIT/C-RULE) has no record id for §4.1's by-reference provenance or G3's re-run. (5)
  `inconclusive-spread` records are UNRANKED and re-measured once, an uncounted population; the
  bench tolerates one 1.5x trial because it reports medians, while the design reads 2-trial
  half-means (a 1.4x trial moves a half by 20 %; ~1 % of rows carry one). Verifier: HOLDS (all).
  **FIX-NOW** (text) **+ bench questions**: B7 (stream, B2), B8 (a multi-config interleaved pass,
  all arms of a site in one rotated block, with the A/A configs spread through it), B4 restated as
  the PINNED-tier question with its two costed answers (test-only CLI spelling in the default build:

## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

- **A decision table over measured outcomes gets DRILLED, not read.** The simulate lens found the
  design's own predicted outcome landing on the wrong verdict row, and a 2x-δ loss read as a tie,
  only by applying the table first-match to constructed outcome vectors. Proposed text for the
  simulate row of the lens table: "... and, where the subject's output is a verdict/decision table
  over measured outcomes, apply it first-match to constructed vectors, the design's OWN predicted
  outcome first, then adversarial ones (r? bakeoff: the predicted tie read INCONCLUSIVE)."
- **An external harness is reviewed by its timed loop, not its summary.** Three lenses independently
  found that the bench's loop repeats one subject (the design read the bench's discipline from its
  methodology summary); the verifier then found a bench regime (`call-stream`) all three missed.
  Proposed text for the measurement lens: "for every harness the design times on, quote its timed
  loop and its calibration unit, and list its regimes."
- **Denying a lane's probe directory forces re-derivation, which pays.** Critics re-ran every cited
  probe and found two that do not reproduce as stated (B22); the design's conclusions held, its
  example did not. Keep the probe dir on the default design-mode denylist (skill §3.3).
