Subject: `docs/design/k18_memo_design.md` (merge 95cba2d, status PROPOSED)

## Headline

**A2 the DESIGN is affirmed; the note is not landable as written, and §6
ruling 1 should be WITHDRAWN, not answered.** The prototype the note
measured is not the design the note describes: `clo_visit` restores the
open-loop stack's DEPTH per frame but not its ENTRIES (S3), and that one
bug is simultaneously (a) the refutation of the note's `nonstacktop == 0`
cell — the counter §2a instructs the rewrite to land as an assertion fires
on 358 of 4,369 patterns, so a literal rewrite ships an assertion that
aborts the compiler on a 28-character regex (S10) — and (b) the ENTIRE
cost residual the ruling request is built on: with a two-line fix that only
ADDS per-frame work, the "user-reachable 39-second compile" at the parser's
nesting cap becomes **0.419 s**, the Θ(d⁴) "cost law" dissolves, and all
four random patterns that blow a 60 s budget under A2 compile in 0.11–2.9 s
(S16). Frank was being asked to authorise a deliberately inexact compiler
past a depth threshold to mitigate a prototype bug.

On the question that matters most, A2 survived everything three critics
could throw at it: across the note's evidence plus ~11,000 independent
patterns and ~330,000 span cells (randomised grammar, forced non-zero match
starts through the reverse machine, trie/no-trie crossing, a no-memo
reference walk), there are **zero cells where A2 is wrong and the shipped
compiler is right**, and every measured population count in the note
reproduced exactly on a fresh rebuild.

## Findings and triage dispositions

Disposition vocabulary: **AMEND** = the note-revision lane fixes the note
(and prototype) with measurement before the rewrite lane opens; **WITHDRAW**
= remove a ruling request; **PROBE** = follow-up work outside the note;
**RECORD** = no change owed, kept as evidence.

### Blockers

- **S16 (semantics) — the cost residual is the S3 stack bug, not the
  design.** 44.36 s → 0.419 s at nest250 with entries restored; 48x fewer
  visits, 67x fewer contexts, emitted C byte-identical (32-pattern ladder,
  0 differ); the fuzz-found constant-factor pattern unaffected (fast path
  and stack fix are orthogonal — both needed, neither substitutes).
  **Disposition: AMEND** — fix the prototype stack restore, re-take EVERY
  cost number (Θ table, §2c pricing, inflation x1.006, corpus aggregate
  with a timing harness that beats the process-startup floor), and
  **WITHDRAW §6 ruling 1**: on this evidence the honest recommendation is
  NO threshold. Any future threshold proposal must engage D22
  (refuse-over-silent-degrade, per V2) and D46 (the selection point must be
  observable/forceable, per V3) — the panel's judgement is that with the
  stack fixed there is nothing left for a threshold to mitigate.
- **S14 (semantics) — the cost model is posed on the wrong variable.**
  Bounded repeats replicate loop bodies (`A_REP` nesting copies), so a
  78-char random pattern hits effective depth 11 and does not finish under
  A2 (900 s) while shipped takes 0.118 s; sweeping the repeat count k holds
  depth constant at 11 while compile time rises 22x — cost tracks CONTEXT
  COUNT, not nesting depth; D=64 would never fire on any of these (~0.1%
  of random patterns). **Disposition: AMEND** — re-mark the Θ(d⁴) cell as
  a fit to one family; §5's cost gates gain a bounded-repeat-times-
  nullable-loop row and a per-pattern COMPILE-TIME BUDGET on the fuzzer
  legs (a compile that never finishes is not a divergence and was invisible
  to §4.4's fuzz control). After the S16 fix these patterns are 0.11–2.9 s,
  so this survives as a gate-design finding, not a live cost problem.
- **S10 (semantics) — `nonstacktop == 0` refuted on the note's own
  prototype.** 0 on the note's corpora (reproduced), 358/4,369 on deeper
  shapes — the note's generator tops out at two loop levels, so the cell
  was measured true on a corpus that cannot reach the failure. Every
  divergence is a MISSED redirect on the reverse machine — verbatim the
  K18 defect class, currently latent (0 answer changes, S3).
  **Disposition: AMEND** — with the S16 stack fix the assertion is sound
  (0 on all 4,369 with entries restored) and the cell becomes true as
  written; the revision states the invariant explicitly and §5 item 6
  keeps BOTH assertions (nonstacktop and the S9 no-repeat push scan).
- **M-B1 (measure) — `gen_adversarial.py`'s `altnest`/`k18nest` families
  (18/70 patterns, the two families named after K18's own shape) are
  syntactically invalid** ("multiple repeat"; needs `"(?:%s)*" % s`) and
  were silently excluded from §2a's adversarial claim with no disclosure
  in the note, the CLAUDE.md, or the MANIFEST. **Disposition: AMEND** —
  one-line generator fix, re-run the sweep, disclose the denominator.

### Majors

- **S3 (semantics) — the stack corruption itself.** Real (552/1,001
  patterns reach the decision with a divergent verdict, overwhelmingly on
  the reverse machine), latent (0 emitted-C changes vs the fixed variant;
  non-vacuous harness: 405/1,001 differ base-vs-A2). **Disposition:
  AMEND** (the S16 fix); the design's no-repeat invariant survives the
  corruption only by coincidence of mechanism (S9) — the revision records
  that argument.
- **S8 (semantics) — fourth structural sub-case: laziness is not the
  ingredient, the PREFERRED arm is.** `(?:(?:b*|a)?)*` (the K18 entry's
  own control with arms swapped) is a live miscompile, oracle-confirmed
  twice; so are an empty-arm form and a concatenation form the entry lists
  as non-diverging. The 165-case acceptance corpus is lazy-only; the
  witnesses are in none of it. A2 fixes all of them (the dense shape sweep
  covers the order axis, so this is a prose/corpus defect, not an A2
  hole). **Disposition: AMEND** — §1 re-characterised; the guard corpus
  gains an arm-order axis (every diverging shape in both orders, greedy
  and lazy); `docs/dev/known_issues.md` K18's control list corrected in
  the same change.
- **S12 (semantics) — §3's "strictly stronger than K17's argument,
  subsumes it" does not hold.** The new proof needs the finite-context
  premise (setup ii) that S10 shows unguarded, and it proves termination,
  not correctness — K17's shape argument would catch a construct the
  measure argument cannot. Half-prototype evidence (S5): removing the
  open-set test loses termination outright (SIGABRT/SIGSEGV at 20,000x
  capacity). **Disposition: AMEND** — decreasing-measure kept STRUCTURAL;
  the comparative sentence deleted; setup (ii) promoted to an explicit
  asserted obligation.
- **M-M1 (measure) + V5 (valplan) — §4.4's fuzzer-red footnote is stale

  AMEND** — back-annotate §4.4 and the docs/design/CLAUDE.md summary

- **V1 (valplan) — `tests/harness/run.sh:256`'s bare `timeout 60` on

## Reflection

- The panel instrument worked exactly as D6 intends: the note's own
  self-flagged soft places (BELIEVED marks) were where two critics dug,
  and the decisive finding came from refusing to trust a STRUCTURAL claim
  ("the redirect truncates, the frame restores") against the prototype's
  actual code. §7's own lesson — an instrument sharing a failure mode with
  what it measures — applied to the note one level up: byte-identical
  output cannot see a cost bug, so the cost numbers were measuring a
  defect while the correctness oracle stayed green.
- The check-design memory held again: the note's corpora could not reach
  the failure (generator tops out at two loop levels), so a true-on-its-
  evidence MEASURED cell was false of the design space. Independent
  generators are not optional panel equipment.
- Five failed attacks recorded in A2's favour are as load-bearing as the
  three blockers: the revision lane inherits not just defects but a map of
  what is already known solid.
