### PREMISE-1 CONFIRMED (design; effort small in the note, medium at build; blast: §5.1, §6 T4, units L1-L2)
§5.1's premise "At a positive lookaround, the run walk holds `acc`, the facts of everything to its RIGHT (`req.c:671-674`)" is true only for the LEFTMOST element of a CAT spine. The spine is left-deep (`PCREC_SPINE_EACH`, `src/core/internal.h:336-339`: elements are `->r`, last is `->l`); `rb_walk_at`'s `A_CAT` arm walks `a->r` through `rb_walk` into a FRESH slot (`req.c:682`), and every `rb_walk_at` starts with `rb_init(acc)` (`req.c:677`). So a lookahead anywhere but first in its sequence (`bar(?=baz)`, `a(?=b)[bc]`, `x(?=ab|ac)a.`) sees `acc` = `rr_unit()` (head n=0, all=true, maxw 0) at its arm, never its right neighbour. Applying §5.1's lookahead bullet as written there: `h > acc.head.n` and `acc.all` -> the whole body head EXTENDS (ahead h, not all); then `rr_cat` (`req.c:450-468`, which has no guard clause) joins the left tail with that head as if consumed after it. For `a(?=b)[bc]` that yields `a b [bc]` (3 positions) where `b` and `[bc]` occupy the SAME subject byte: oracle `a(?=b)[bc]` on `ab` = M 0 2 (p2probe), and `ab` holds no `ab[bc]` -> NOMATCH: a deleted match if built as written. The lookbehind bullet already puts its intersection in `rr_cat`; the lookahead bullet must too (the guard carried in the RbRuns value and intersected/extended by `rr_cat` against `r.head`), i.e. T4 is a CAT-combinator rule, not an arm rule. No witness in §5.7 has a non-leftmost lookahead whose guard overlaps its right neighbour (W-L2's lookahead is leftmost in its group); add one (`a(?=b)[bc]` on `ab`).

### PREMISE-5 CONFIRMED (doc / predicted movers; effort trivial; blast: §5.2's lookahead example, G5's spot checks)
§5.2: "`x(?=ab|ac)a.` -> `xa` plus the guard `a` at 1: intersection `a ∩ a`, then `[bc]` at 2 out of the cube domain: no extension". `[bc]` IS in the cube domain (0x62/0x63 differ in one bit; `pos_set` = 2): measured at the build tree, `x(?:ab|ac)` whole run `786162/fffffe` and `xa[bc]` `786162/fffffe` (3 positions, mask `fe` at position 2), and the note's own `x(?:a[bc]){2}y` prediction relies on `[bc]` being a run position. The lookahead body's common head is therefore `a[bc]` (2 positions, `rr_alt`'s cube hull), which lies INSIDE the consumed `a.`: the T4 INSIDE row predicts `xa[bc]` (23 bits), not `xa`. Oracle: `x(?=ab|ac)a.` on `xab` M 0 3, on `xad` N (p2probe), so `xa[bc]` is necessary. (Also note PREMISE-1: this lookahead is not leftmost, so the arm as written does not see `a.` at all.)

### PREMISE-6 CONFIRMED (design; effort small; blast: §5.3 bullet 3, §8 L3 "at most MF_MAX_BACK", §13 Q5)
The note bounds BACK by `MF_MAX_BACK` (8) because "the memory-functions kit accepts window positions at most 8 bytes before the candidate" (§13 Q5; §5.3 bullet 3). But by §5.3's own account the window statement's R does not move at BACK (A5: R stays walk-proven, never below 0 before U-G1), and the only seam that carries BACK in L3 is the PRESENCE span's `omin` (and, per PREMISE-2(b), `head_max`). `MF_MAX_BACK` governs window spans (`memfn.h:586-591`, checked `memfn_sites.c:587-592`) and term offsets (`memfn.h:614`), not `omin`: `omin` is an `int32_t` (`memfn.h:696`) whose only kit check is `p->omin < 0` refused (`memfn/src/compose.c:608`); the pre-check's FUNC part window starts at 0 from the HIT (`emit_dfa.c:1128-1130`), unaffected by back. So the 8-byte cap is borrowed from a seam L3 does not use; the real bound is whatever the kit's `omin` change admits (a kit decision), and PCRE2's/D189's lookbehind lengths (up to 255 chars varying, 65,535 fixed) are the population. Q5's leaning text ("the kit accepts window positions at most 8 bytes ...") should name the presence seam, or the reach row should cite why the window bound is chosen as a policy rather than a kit limit.
