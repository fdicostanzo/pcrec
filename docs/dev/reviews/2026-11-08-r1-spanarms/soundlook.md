## Findings
### SOUNDLOOK-1: the lookbehind extension's `all` is unstated; reading it as materialized + `all` double-counts the guard (CONFIRMED by hand-execution + oracle)
- Severity: answer (latent, at L1/L3 build). Effort: one sentence + one witness. Blast radius: every lookbehind whose body reaches past an `all` left neighbour inside a group.
- §5.1 bullet 2 says that when `t > l.tail.n` and `l.all` "the rest extends before l's start: reach back grows". For the lookahead mirror it says explicitly "the result is not `all`"; for the lookbehind it says nothing about `o.all` or whether the leftover becomes head BYTES or stays a pending guard.
- Hand-execution, `y(?:x(?<=yx))z`, reading "extends" as "prepended to o.head, o.all = l.all && r.all = true": group: rr_cat(l=`x` all, r=lb-unit with guard `yx`): intersect x∩x, leftover `y` -> o.head=`yx`, all=true. Outer: rr_cat(l=`y`, r=group): o.head = rn_app(`y`, `yx`) = `yyx`, then `z` -> `yyxz`.
- Oracle: `p2probe - (hex y(?:x(?<=yx))z) 0:(hex yxz)` -> `M 0 3`. `yyxz` is absent from `yxz`: a pre-check answers NOMATCH. Correct value: the leftover stays a PENDING guard (intersected again by the next left neighbour: y∩y), or o.all = false.
- No witness covers it: W-L2 is the lookahead mirror only. Proposed: state "a back extension is never head bytes below the top level (a pending guard), or clears `all`", plus witness `y(?:x(?<=yx))z` on `yxz` (0,3).

### SOUNDLOOK-2: guard propagation through ALT, stacking, nesting and the tail is unstated; each natural reading below deletes a match (CONFIRMED by hand-execution + oracle)
- Severity: answer (latent, L1-L3). Effort: one declared row per combinator (rr_cat, rr_alt, REP, affix extraction) in T4. Blast radius: every pattern with a guard not at a CAT edge.
- §5.1 states the guard only at the arm and in rr_cat(l, r)'s left-neighbour meeting. It says nothing for: (a) rr_alt of a branch carrying a pending back guard; (b) two pending back guards at one cursor; (c) a lookaround body that itself carries a guard, when its HEAD/TAIL affix is taken; (d) whether an extension may enter `tail`/`best` (K82 offsets).
- (a) `(?:(?<=a)b|c)`: if rr_alt keeps the left branch's guard beside the hull head `[bc]` -> run `a[bc]` back 1. Oracle: subject `c` from 0 -> `M 0 1`; `a[bc]` absent -> NOMATCH. Correct: hull of both guards, an absent guard = no guard (drop).
- (b) `(?<=ab)(?<=b)c`: the left lookbehind is the `all` width-0 neighbour of the right one's pending `b`; "the rest extends before l's start" read as prepend after l's own guard -> `abbc` back 3. Oracle: `abc` from 0 and 2 -> `M 2 3`; `abbc` absent -> NOMATCH. Correct: intersect the two guards at equal offsets (`ab`).
- (c) `x(?=(?<=x)y)y`: the body's head affix with its inner back guard flattened to offset 0 -> H=`xy` at [0,2); intersect with acc.head `y`: x∩y empty -> an emptied position. Oracle: `xy` -> `M 0 2`. And `(?<=a(?=b))b`: body tail taken with the inner lookahead's extension -> B=`b` at [-1,0) -> `bb` back 1; oracle `ab` -> `M 1 2`. Correct: an affix is the body's CONSUMED affix; nested guards are dropped or carried at their own offsets.
- (d) If an extension enters `tail`, `rn_tail_off(maxw, n)` (req.c:428-431) under-states the start offset by the extension length (K too small, handoff jumps past the match): `[a-z]?[a-z]a(?=bc)` tail `abc` stated at off 0, real start up to 2; oracle `zzabc` -> `M 0 3`. §5.1 says "EXTEND the head", which implies head only; say it for tail and best explicitly.

### SOUNDLOOK-3: the reach is not computable as written across nesting levels: RbRuns has no minimum width, and the only coverage measure the note names is maxw (SUSPECTED design gap; one CONFIRMED wrong-answer reading)
- Severity: answer (BACK) / design (INSIDE vs AHEAD). Effort: design (a pending-guard field per head plus a min width, or "reach never shrinks"). Blast radius: every guard inside a group.
- `rb_walk_at` re-seeds `acc` per subtree (`rb_init(acc)`, req.c:677), so a lookahead's extension at the arm is measured against the right side of its OWN subtree only. §5.6/W-L2 claim `(?:(?=abcd)ab)cd` is row INSIDE at reach (0,0) ("extension inside the outer match"); deciding that needs the outer right side's MINIMUM width (`(?:(?=abcd)ab)c?` on `abcd` -> `M 0 3`, `d` outside; `c??` -> `M 0 2`, `cd` outside; maxw of the right side is 1 in both), and RbRuns carries only `maxw` (req.c:154). §5.1 says "at (0,0) every guard position outside the match is dropped" without saying WHERE (arm, subtree boundary, top level).
- If L1 drops at the arm/subtree (the only locally decidable place), W-L2's site (an extension's `all`) is never reached at L1, whose gate lists it (§8).
- BACK reading that deletes matches: `y*(?:ab(?<=xab)c)`: inside the group `l=ab` is `all`, the leftover `x` gives `xabc` back 1 into the group's best; outer rr_cat(l=`y*`) with "back shrinks by what l.maxw covers" (§5.1's own measure) -> back 0 -> reach (0,0) keeps `xabc` with no step back. Oracle: `xabc` from 1 -> `M 1 4`; scan [1,4) has no `xabc` -> NOMATCH. Sound rule: back never shrinks (or shrinks by MIN width). AHEAD misclassification is answer-benign (§5.4), BACK is not.
