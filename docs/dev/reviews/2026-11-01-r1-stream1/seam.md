### SEAM-1 — the §2 seam contract is forward-only; today's start table already carries the backward half, and the merge drops it
- CONFIRMED (grep). Severity: design (F4). Effort: medium (a table half, not a mechanism). Blast: every seam. Hits §2 "The seam contract", Pick 1.
- Today `struct CandRow` (src/gen/emit_dfa.c:5670-5713) carries, besides what a row HANDS (`hands`, CT_*), what the next node ACCEPTS (`take[route][]`, :5695), what the row USES (`needs[route]`, :5689), its give-up CONTRACT (`contract`, CG_FIXED, :5682, enum :9829-9832), and `cand_nodes[]` (:9930-) declares the cross-slot READ graph (`reads`, `under`, `by`) and the KIT ASKS (`kask`, `kfields`, :9876-9893) with a trace-build cycle check (`table-read-cycle`, `table-ask-unread`, :11280). PRESENCE's `dominated` (the [LANDWALK-PRES] decision) is exactly such a read: PRESENCE reads LOCATE "the BODY's locator answers presence itself" (:9944-9950).
- The subject's seam table (pos, slots, proof, reentry, effects, exact, meters) keeps only the HAND half: Pick 1 says "its `CAND_HAND_*` types become the seam's position types" and names no home for take/needs/contract/reads/kask. Every field in the table is written by the upstream side and read by the downstream side at run time.
- Consequence: the design makes the [LANDWALK-PRES] shape (a local decision blind to a cheaper check across the seam) the DEFAULT. The one place today where both directions are declared and checked is the part the merge flattens.
- Fix: split the contract into a PLAN-TIME half (accepts, demands, needs, contract, cards read from neighbours, along a declared acyclic read graph: today's cand_nodes discipline generalised to stream segments) and the RUN-TIME half (the §2 table). See SEAM-14 for the general form.

### SEAM-2 — deleting the kit's `MF_SK_*` consumer kinds deletes the consumer-to-kit channel the kit's measured rows are keyed on
- CONFIRMED (grep). Severity: design (F4, D157) + speed risk. Effort: small to rename by behaviour; medium if deleted and re-derived. Blast: every kit site; a kit abi event. Hits §2 "The kit", §4.3 X14.
- The subject: the engine-context kinds (emit_dfa.c:1417-1425, cite holds) "are deleted, because a site's facts come from its seam." But `mf_site.site_kind` is documented as THE CONSUMER KIND, "how the site's result is consumed" (memfn/include/memfn.h:767-770, :414-416), read by the finder table's gate (memfn/src/finder.c:398-412, `SF_SEEK_ROLE` at :812). - The naming complaint is real (D174: names say what the caller sees, never the engine), but the FACT must survive: re-express the kind as consumer facts (result used as a position vs a verdict only, a re-seed after each hit, what fronts the route), not delete it. ### SEAM-3 — no ask phase: the planner cannot know what a kit instruction proves before the kit answers, and the design names no channel for the answer
- CONFIRMED (grep) for today's channel; the gap is in the subject's text. Severity: design (F4, D146/D157, D182 (c) rule 3). Effort: small (name it). Blast: every segment that reads `proof` downstream of FIND/SKIP/VERIFY/PRESENT. Hits §2 `proof`, "Who segments".
- Under D157 pcrec does not presume the kit's method, so pcrec cannot know which offsets a FIND's text actually tests. Today that answer flows back through `mf_ask` (a pure query of the kit, made BEFORE the selection that reads it; emit_dfa.c:9876-9893, :11600-11628), returning `mf_class` (`exists`, `proven0`, `proven_prev`, `libc_free`, `includes`; CAND_ASKF_* at :8283), and the kit states `proven[d]` as "the set the text tests at d" (memfn.h, R-25). [CAND-SPAN]'s ruling depends on that: "a pass PROVES those offsets for the walk's rewind/verify" (plan.md:663 row).
- The subject's planner "writes the stream from the one IR program" and the kit renders later; `proof` has no named writer for kit kinds. A planner that admits a downstream row on a proof (row 7's END pin, CAND-SPAN's start-past-the-span) either presumes the kit's method (a D157 breach) or must re-ask.
- Fix: name `mf_ask` (or its successor) as the planner's kit channel; a kit instruction's `proof` is the kit's ANSWER, recorded as a record field (D182 (c) rule 3); keep today's "an ask is a read, no cycle closes through the kit" check. ### SEAM-4 — the typing rule licenses `UPPER` from a SUPERSET segment, which is unsound; today's projection allows only `LOWER`
- CONFIRMED (python `re` + code). Severity: answer. Effort: one word in the rule, plus a per-coordinate type. Blast: every superset seam (prefilter, gates, lookaround-erased, count-collapsed). Hits §2 Pick 2.

- Repro: `a(?=b)|c` on "ac": python `re.search` gives (1,2); the erased superset `a|c` gives (0,1). Handing `UPPER 0` ("no match starts above 0") loses the match at 1. Same shape on today's H3 witness `(?>a|ab)c|abcd` on "abcd": true (0,4), erased (0,3) (the END moves DOWN, so neither bound holds for the end).
- Today's code already does this right: `cand_project` (emit_dfa.c:13644-13651) maps SPAN and ENDSET to `LOWER` on a superset body ("its start is only a lower bound and its end nothing"), NOMATCH survives.

## Summary

- The §2 seam contract carries facts FORWARD only; today's start table, kit ABI and lmwalk table already carry six kinds of BACKWARD plan-time facts, and the merge drops them (SEAM-1, -2, -3, -9). That makes the [LANDWALK-PRES] blindness the default.

- The family's general form is a plan-time CARD half of every seam (SEAM-14). Its first site is P-c, and its unlocks are LANDWALK-PRES, CAND-SPAN, consumer kinds named by behaviour, per-entry tilings, and F2/F3.
