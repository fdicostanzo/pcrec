## Lessons for the guide

- A ruling that says "as today" or "already works" needs a run at the fold, not only in the record. Proposed COMMON
  line for fold rounds: "every 'as today' / 'already' sentence in a ruled statement is checked against the BEFORE
  documents by text and by the old engine". Lens 1 found RULING-3 that way in minutes.
- A marker for a lean that is "entailed by" rulings needs three classes, not two: states, depends, and free or
  ruled-answer. Ask the fold lane to print the site table itself (RULING-4).
