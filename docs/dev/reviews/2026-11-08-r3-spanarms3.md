## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **A run witness must differ inside the EMITTED window.** The design's SIM models and the r2
   verifier tested the whole run; the artifact tests 8 positions. Add to the readiness and soundness
   rows: "for a run/set witness, compute the emitted window (`pcrec_req_window`) for correct and
   sabotaged runs; a difference outside it fails only under the whole-run test (`--memfn=no-fd-pres-pick`)".
2. **A check named in a gate must hold the witness in its population.** "check N green" over a fixed
   list (`WIT`) is not a witness until the pattern is in the list (K35 shape, again). Readiness lens:
   for every gate check, grep its population for the witness.
3. **An equality control must be shown to fail on the row it names.** W-C1's lowering equality was
   true with and without the row under test.
