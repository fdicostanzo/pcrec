F1 MAJOR (startset.md §2 V/F predicates): the VM hat has no density/cost admission. V needs only |S|<256; first-memchr applies to any |S|=1, so a dense S enters the seek and skips nothing (the [OPT-3] symptom). §4.4 "no model, needs none" + one guard cell. Evidence: `a(\w)\1` (vm/none, REQ_BYTE 97, S={a}) on a random 8-symbol subject at a-density d, find-all base→twin: d=5% table x1.1 / memchr x1.2; 17% x1.0 / x0.9; 33% x0.9 / x0.9; 50% x0.9 / x0.9; 80% x0.9 / x0.6. Base replicated across two binaries within ~1%. The loss is per call / per dense match (44k matches/MiB at 33%), not per retry: on `a(\w)\1\1\1\1\1` (rare matches) the twins win at every d up to 50%, only memchr loses at 80% (x0.9). Fix: dense single-byte cell and |S|>=128 cell (`.`-led, S=255) as do-not-regress in the alpha; either a MASS-based admission via the existing pcrec_find_set_ppm (no new constant) or state the sign flip as a filed trigger.
