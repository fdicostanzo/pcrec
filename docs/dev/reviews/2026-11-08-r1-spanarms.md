## Findings (consolidated; PROPOSED dispositions)

### Answer-level (latent: a build from the text as written would delete matches)

**F1 — the lookahead arm never sees its right neighbour** (PREMISE-1; verifier HOLDS). §5.1 says "at a positive lookaround, the run walk holds `acc`, the facts of everything to its RIGHT (`req.c:671-674`)". This holds only for the LEFTMOST element of a CAT spine. The spine is left-nested (`PCREC_SPINE_EACH`, `internal.h:336-339`). The `A_CAT` arm walks each `->r` element into a fresh slot (`req.c:681-685`), and `rb_walk_at` starts with `rb_init(acc)` (`:677`). So `a(?=b)[bc]`'s lookahead sees `rr_unit()`, its whole head "extends", and `rr_cat` then lays `b` and `[bc]` end to end. Oracle: `a(?=b)[bc]` on `ab` gives (0,2), and `ab` does not hold the stated run, so the match is lost. Both the note's predicted mover and the verifier read the INTENT as intersection with the right neighbour; the TEXT gives a different result. Fix: the lookahead guard is carried in the run value and met inside `rr_cat` against `r.head`, exactly as the lookbehind bullet already does. T4 becomes a CAT-combinator rule, not an arm rule. Add witness `a(?=b)[bc]` / `ab`. **Proposed: FIX-NOW (rev 1 of §5.1/T4), before any L-unit build go.**

**F2 — guard propagation through the combinators is unstated; natural readings delete matches** (SOUNDLOOK-1, -2, -3; verifier: -1 HOLDS as a text gap, -2 (c)(d) HOLD and (a)(b) PARTLY, -3 PARTLY with the structural gap HOLDING). Missing rules, each with an oracle witness the critic built:
- (a) the lookbehind extension's `all` flag, and whether the leftover becomes head bytes or stays a pending guard: `y(?:x(?<=yx))z` on `yxz` (0,3). Read as bytes + `all`, the run becomes `yyxz`.
- (b) ALT of a branch that carries a guard: `(?:(?<=a)b|c)` on `c`.
- (c) two guards at one cursor: `(?<=ab)(?<=b)c` on `abc` (2,3), which must intersect at equal offsets.
- (d) a lookaround body that itself holds a lookaround, when its affix is taken: `x(?=(?<=x)y)y` on `xy`, and `(?<=a(?=b))b` on `ab`. An affix is the body's CONSUMED affix only.
- (e) whether an extension may enter `tail`/`best`. `rn_tail_off` would under-state K: `[a-z]?[a-z]a(?=bc)` on `zzabc`.
- (f) the reach across nesting. `RbRuns` has no minimum width, so INSIDE vs AHEAD at reach (0,0) cannot be decided from `maxw`: `(?:(?=abcd)ab)c?` and `c??`. The direction of `back` at outer levels is also unstated: `y*(?:ab(?<=xab)c)` from offset 1. The sound rule is "back never shrinks" (or shrinks by MIN width).

Fix: one declared guard row per combinator in T4 (CAT, ALT, REP, affix extraction, top level), a pending-guard field and a min width (or "reach never shrinks"), and these witnesses in the L units' gates. **Proposed: FIX-NOW (rev 1, together with F1).**

**F3 — no witness for the one REP plant that moves an answer: `maxw` from the c unrolled copies** (SOUNDREP-4; verifier HOLDS). Saturation makes c < m the normal case. Keeping `maxw = c·w` under-states `rb.off = l.maxw + ...` (`req.c:463`) for everything to the right. The text is right (§3.1 point 3: "maxw is always the exact `rr_rep_w`"), but no witness has c < m with a right neighbour. Witness: `(a.){10}xyz` measures `req_run_maxoff=20` and `RX_REQ_HANDOFF="20"` (VM) today. With the sabotage K becomes 4, and subject `a-a-a-a-a-a-a-a-a-a-xyz` (libpcre2 (0,23)) answers NOMATCH. **Proposed: FIX-NOW (add W-R6; a DFA twin needs a VM-forcing or non-dominated shape).**

**F4 — T2 row 1 has no guard column; a min-0 quantified lookaround must drop its guard** (SOUNDREP-6; verifier PARTLY: §2's own table already says "none", so this is a gap between two tables of the same note). Witness `x(?=ab)?ac` on `xac` (0,3). **Proposed: FIX-NOW (add the column to T2; add the witness to L1's gate).**

**F6 — W-R5 is an equivalent mutant; the saturation bound is one copy loose, and "no stored field can change" is false for `best.off`** (SOUNDREP-2, SIM-2, SIM-3; verifier HOLDS, SIM-3 PARTLY).

## Lessons for the guide (proposed; see .coord/process.md for evidence)

1. **premise lens:** when a design says "at node X the walk holds Y", find where Y is computed and check that X's arm receives it at EVERY position of X in its parent: first, middle and last of a spine, and inside a group. Here (F1) the soundness critics executed the rules "literally" from the arm outward; only the premise critic asked what the arm is handed.
2. **verifier brief:** for each "as written this deletes a match" claim, ask whether a reasonable alternative reading of the text avoids it (then PARTLY, a text gap). This round it separated forced errors (F1, F3) from unstated rules (F2, F4) cleanly, which is what the fix lane needs.
