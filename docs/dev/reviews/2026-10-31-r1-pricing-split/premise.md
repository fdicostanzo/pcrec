### PREMISE-4 (CONFIRMED; severity design; CHANGES the claim in Q1.4 item 1 that "the fold is itself a dominance", a supporting claim of the seam rule's rule 2)
**The presence fold (kit P2, the `dominated` row) is NOT a pure dominance today: one of its three disjuncts is a prior-rate comparison.**
Kit row P2, `memfn/src/finder.c:335-339`: "G1, ported: span 0's proof covers the span (identity, run_verified, or the density conjunct on the prior): dropped", basis `FB_UNMEASURED_DEFAULT`. pcrec's shadow expectation is `emit_dfa.c:8546-8555` (`req_byte_dominated_by`: `return pcrec_find_no_commoner(pcrec_find_byte_rate(cx), p, q);`). Under seam rule 2 ("A DOMINANCE prune may read facts ... never a number") the density disjunct must leave the fold and become a path-price term. The §1.2 table marks `dominated` as P, which is right. But Q1.4 item 1's "the fold is itself a dominance ... so the kit can keep folding wherever it can" is true only of D176 Q4's TARGET fold (coverage: "when the chosen stop's first find ... covers the presence byte", decisions.md D176 Q4), not of today's P2.
Also missing from crossing 1: on the route with no DFA scan in front, presence is not a price at all. Kit P0 makes a REQUIRED span undeniable (finder.c:330-333, "(K65, K66; G-1, G-2); undeniable (D158)"), and `req_route_one_attempt`'s comment (emit_dfa.c:8494-8499) shows the pre-check is what keeps `^([a-z]+)+@` from giving up. So presence on/off is a price only for OPTIONAL spans, which is exactly Q-WR-9's own scope ("unless the span's `need` is OPTIONAL"). The study's Q4 "term: PRESENCE ... on/off price" should say so. Otherwise a priced presence term could switch off a contract-bearing pass.

### PREMISE-10 (CONFIRMED; severity maintenance; Q6 stage-1 hazard mitigation is partly wrong)
**The quiet walk does not protect the facts' `used` bit, so "or the quiet walk" is not a valid mitigation. And a shadow ARGMIN, unlike first-match, evaluates rows the default walk never reaches.**
`cand_select_quiet` (emit_dfa.c:10869-10878) suppresses trace records and the read graph ("a check walk, not a read"). It runs the same predicates. `used` is set by the public accessors on any pass ask (`src/facts/facts.c:18-20`, `:128 if (pass) pf->used |= pf_bit(f);`), and the predicates make such asks: `req_set_leads_applies` calls `(void)pcrec_fact_req_set(s->cx)` "as a PASS ... (the facts' `used` record ...)", emit_dfa.c:8616-8623.
First-match stops at the first applying row. An argmin must evaluate EVERY row's predicate, including rows below today's pick. So it reaches accessors and kit asks (`req_pres_ask`, `mf_ask`) the default build never makes, and moves `--emit-facts`' `used` column and the kit's MFTRACE/ask records. The only correct mitigation of the two is the `_stated` accessors (facts.h:520-531), and that means the shadow's predicates must be separate non-pass spellings of the real ones: a second derivation, which is the parallel-mechanism hazard that the general-mechanisms rule and K35 warn about. Stage 1 needs a design for "evaluate all rows without consuming", for example a per-walk pass/stated mode on the accessors.

### PREMISE-12 (CONFIRMED by code reading; severity design; qualifies Q2.2's "That nesting is exact", a load-bearing step of Summary 3)
**The outer/inner argmin is exact only if a route's validity does not depend on the inner path. Under a size cap it does.**
The study's formula is `min over valid routes rt of ( match_phase(rt) + min over valid paths p on rt of price(p) )`. "Valid routes" is assumed fixed before the inner min. But the size-cap ladder (compile.c:1358-1399) decides whether an artifact FITS from the emitted size, which depends on the strategies chosen (`drop-handoff` exists because the handoff rows add bytes, `drop-anchored` because FINISH `verify-at` does), and it retries with strategies removed. So (route, path) validity is joint: a route can be valid with path p and invalid with path p'. In that case "the minimum over a union is the minimum of the minimums" does not apply, because the union is not a union of per-route sets fixed in advance. The fix is small. Either state validity over (route, path) pairs and keep the ladder as the late-validity source (the study already says the build-and-retry loop is "the source of late validity facts", RECOMMENDATION front end), or declare emitted-size caps outside the argmin. But Summary 3's "That nesting is exact" needs that qualifier.
