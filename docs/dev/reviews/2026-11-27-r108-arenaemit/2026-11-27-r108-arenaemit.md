for P2/P3 to PRICE a set, keeps one scalar, and leaves the tables on the caller's arena; it is reached via `whole()`

identical by construction. PLAN rather than FIX-NOW: it is a new client (S14c) outside this unit's design row, and it
needs its own memgate measurement under the same levers (D77); the manager may fold it into this lane instead.
Sibling not proposed: `pcrec_clskit_partition`'s DP scratch is mixed-lifetime (`out->sec` must live on the parent).
