## 1. Findings and rulings

### Blocker

**M1. E3's re-description bypasses the K96 gate.** RPT rewrites the site it is given:
- STMT/ASSIGN becomes FUNC;
- `plan_hint` is set over pcrec's MF_NO_PRED;
- SETs are merged into a RUN, all before ofsskip renders it.
So ofsskip's contract is checked against a site pcrec never stated, and pcrec's own field
is overwritten (integration.md §14.9 item 1).
- **Ruling: ACCEPTED.** The revision makes NO RPT change. Instead, ofsskip's own contract
  widens to serve STMT/ASSIGN (precheck.c:337-353 is the precedent):
  - its `uses` include fn_ref, fn_name, decl_point, result and on_miss, and it declines
    where any is unstated;
  - the scan position becomes a declared first-match slot: pcrec's plan_hint where pcrec
    states it, else the kit's stop by mass;
  - the SET-to-RUN merge becomes a kit-wide canonicalization, merging by OFFSET (S5).
  RPT's FF_PROOF → fd_nested_walk and `kit_report_exact` stay unchanged. ofsskip is first in
  arms[], so the composer's walk picks it.
- **S4** (kit_report_exact could check itself on s′) dissolves under this ruling, because no
  s′ exists. Its fix becomes a check in the revision: REQUIRED is computed from pcrec's site.

### Major

**M2. The fallback is an if-then, not a declared row.** ACCEPTED. The fallback is the arms
table's floor row (generic, compose.c:146).

**M3. E1 hard-codes the walk inside ofsskip.**
- That is a second walk implementation (D122). It also puts the form choice where
  [MEMFN-REGIME] cannot reach it.
- ACCEPTED. The stop's text is the FIND slot's answer for a one-term sub-site, as a fn_rows
  BODY row (`fn-find`). It is the walk today under Frank's ruling, and [MEMFN-REGIME]'s row
  later, with no new code.

**M4 + S3. The read limits under ctx.** ACCEPTED, both.
- A ctx item before span 0 reads below `pos`. The widened row serves `read_floor` SUBJECT
  only and renders `absent` at offset < 0.
- Every ctx item keeps `pred_test`'s own bound (generic.c:166-183, `guard_by_caller` NO).
  The span-0 bound `c + K > n` does not cover a FAILS item after span 0. Counterexample:
  span "cat", FAILS `s`@3, subject "cat".
- The §4 soundness argument gains the ctx offsets.

**M5. E4 (the failure shift) is a REPORT special case.** ACCEPTED. E4 is either:
- its own fn_rows BODY row (`fn-shift`), with predicate "some j has t > 1", its own
  options.def deny and a census; or
- not built.
Which one depends on the follow-up job (§3). sound's unlock (Horspool over sets) is the
general form to weigh first, if SHF misses hex8.

**S1. A verify chain that drops a ctx item leaves `proven[]` unchanged.** `kit_class` reads
the declared `tests`, not the rendered text, so family C's equality stays green.
- ACCEPTED. `verify_chain` and the scan return the set of terms they actually rendered, and
  an assertion holds it equal to `tests`.
- R27-d's expected check is corrected to BIND/EXACT.
- A drift plant is added.

**S2. Over-reads cannot be seen.** proof_run.c:274 holds subjects in an uninitialised
`s[8]`.
- ACCEPTED. Each subject is copied to the END of an exact-size buffer. The bytes just
  outside it are filled with a per-site sentinel that satisfies every term, so any
  over-read yields a false candidate and EXACT goes red. `make asan` is a second axis.
- New plants: R27-g (a ctx item's bound removed) and R27-h (memchr length + 1).

**T1. The tie rule (Q2) is not measured.** No RIGHTMOST arm exists, and P3's tie
(presence.c:15) is RIGHTMOST. ACCEPTED: the follow-up job adds arm PRR. Until it reads,
the tie is a declared column (unlock U5), not a second rule.

**T2. Correctness is gated only on 72 subjects.** ACCEPTED: the follow-up job adds a
no-clock differential stage.
- About 2000 random small subjects per cell, over each cell's stop, set and ctx bytes,
  lengths 0..40.
- COV, PRO and SHF are each compared against python `re`.

**T3. The proxy is not faithful on the 3 literal cells.** ACCEPTED: arm PRF, a FUNC-shaped
`noinline` twin, for lit-l8, foobar and file-ext-order (search_short is call-cost bound).

**T4. The SIMD layer is not read or named.** ACCEPTED.
- The table header records the memfn profile.
- §5 states that only the scalar layer is read, and that the SIMD verdict is pcrec-bench's
  (D144 addendum 4).
