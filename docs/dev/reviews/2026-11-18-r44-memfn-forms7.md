### A. Variant x regime layout effect: the fit and the drill share one model (FIDELITY-5 + ESTIMATOR-1) — speed/design, CONDITIONAL
`text_effects` fits one effect per variant over every regime of a text (`text_of`, cards.py:1636-1645 drops the
regime), and drill (xxiii)'s layout model is the same shape (`synth_body` :2146, `synth_layout_effect` :2159), so no
drill can see the case where a 16-B shift speeds one regime's hot loop and slows another's. With a pure
variant x regime effect (heads only, tau 2 %), the gated row fails on both counts: P(h* != 64) 0.073 over 900 nights
(verifier, own model; accept 0.05) and voids 0.64-0.67 (accept 0.10); tau 3 %: P 0.193, voids 1.000. Whether real
pools carry such an effect is UNMEASURED (both critics, verifier). The verifier corrected ESTIMATOR-1's "at the line"
to above it.
PROPOSED: FIX-NOW (small form): add the interaction model to drill (xxiii) as a gated row, and have `rule` read the
night's own variant x regime interaction from the `variants` column (per text, the share of variant variance that
is regime-specific) and refuse or fall back to a per-(text, regime) term when it exceeds what the per-text term
assumes. PLAN the full per-(text, regime) fit with its own upper bound, trigger: the first night's measured
interaction share above that line.

### B. A never-picked noisy head lowers every other head's power (ESTIMATOR-2) — speed
`layout_text_term` (:1784-1797) floors every head in a cell at max over the cell's heads of exp(z sqrt(V_I + V_h)) - 1,
though H(8) vs I has variance V_I + V_8. A losing H(1) with layout tau 4 % takes H(8)'s 5 % win from 284 to 151 of
300 (verifier; critic 288 -> 149). Cannot add a false pick.
PROPOSED: FIX-NOW: a per-(cell, head) floor for the head-vs-I predicates (wins, confirm, W/E); head-vs-head dominance
on V_h + V_h'; add a never-picked noisy loser to drill (xxiii)'s power rows as a gated row.

### C. The 32-B half of the pool construction has no guard (FIDELITY-6 + ESTIMATOR-7) — maintenance
`pool_refusal` (:620-631) never reads the 32-B phases `pool_proof` computes; the selftest's `spans` predicate passes
any PHASE_ORDER. Restoring rev 11's (0, 1, 2, 3) passes both while every alignment pair keeps every hot loop's 32-B
phase (hex24 h8 and I, real exes; 2000 of 2000 pools by construction). Side remark (ESTIMATOR-7, by reading): the
refusal passes a pool with 2 distinct layouts while `text_var` charges sL2 / POOL_SIZE.
PROPOSED: FIX-NOW: refuse a pool whose variants within one relative layout share a per-loop 32-B phase vector, and a
selftest row that fails on (0, 1, 2, 3); charge sL2 / k for k distinct layouts (or refuse k < POOL_SIZE).

### D. The confirm re-times step 1's exes; a second confirm round repeats I's draws (FIDELITY-3 + ESTIMATOR-5) — design/doc + maintenance
`pool_variants` hashes the text only, `build_cell` returns the cached exes, so the confirm re-times the same 4-exe
pool: the pool-mean offset (text_var's sL2 / POOL_SIZE term) replicates between night and confirm, and across nights
on one build. cards.py:1864 "a layout draw cannot replicate except by chance" implies otherwise; r45_reply:880 sits
under rev 12's "superseded" bracket but no rev-12 sentence states what replaced it (verifier's correction). A second
confirm round salts identically (file name only), repeats I's exact draw sequence, and its I row replaces round 1's.
PROPOSED: FIX-NOW: state it in rev 12 (the exe level rests on `layout_text_term`; the confirm is not an exe-level
control) and reword :1864; salt the draw with the confirm round and pair each head's confirm with the I row of its
own round. PLAN a fresh pool for the confirm (run-id-salted, 4 more compiles per confirmed arm), the manager's call
on whether the K1 guarantee is wanted back.

### E. A re-used RUNDIR keeps the run id, the G2 record and the draw sequence (FIDELITY-4) — maintenance
