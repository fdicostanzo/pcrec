### Answer level

- **F1 STRICT FORMS INVERT** (SIMULATE-1, FIXIND-2, FIXIND-8; CONFIRMED, re-run). Rev 17 lets the projection name any
  author label, so a compared label can be UNSET (an optional group) or non-digit. `P & !lex_le2(s, lo)` /
  `P & !lex_ge2(s, hi)` ("rev 16, unchanged") then ACCEPT such a match: with `/(?<h>\d{1,2})(?::(?<m>\d\d))?/` and
  bound [9, 30], `9` is both "strictly above" and "strictly below" 9:30; `ab:00` under a `\w` field is "strictly above".
  Rev 16's own always-set helper labels hid this; no evidence population has an optional or non-digit label.
  RULING: **FIX-NOW**. Spell strict as `lex_ge2(s, lo) & !lex_le2(s, lo)` (and the mirror), which gates on the labels;
  or restrict the derived forms to always-set digit labels. One sentence plus a table row.
- **F2 CAVEAT (5) OVERSTATES REJECTION** (SIMULATE-2; CONFIRMED). An unset LATER field is never read when an earlier
  field decides: `lex_between2([(h:),(m:)], [9,30], [11,0])` accepts `10` with m unset. RULING: **FIX-NOW** (rewrite
  caveat (5): rejected only when the compare reaches the unset entry; fold with F1).
- **F3 BOUND LITERALS AND CAPTURED BOUNDS** (SIMULATE-4 SUSPECTED on §2.4's reading; FIXIND-6 SUSPECTED). `padded` pads
  only the compared side; a bound written `[09, 00]` reads (by §2.4 "unless the literal has them") as two-digit
  numerals and drops `9:30` from 9:00..10:00 silently. Captured bounds `[$a1, $a2]` have no literal to fix the reading,
  yet BOUNDS AND LEVELS calls them "existing rules, not new ones". RULING: **FIX-NOW** (state how a bound is read:
  canonical numerals or an error/lint on a leading-zero bound; for match bounds say a declared reading is needed, or
  pad both sides, pointing at §2.4's REASONING).
- **F4 REPEATED LABELS** (SIMULATE-5, FIXIND-3; CONFIRMED by rule emulation). `[(h:), (h:)]` silently collapses to the
  first field (`9:45` rejected by a ge [9,30]); a label inside a loop makes each condition quantify over every
  iteration separately (`10:00,9:30,` rejected though every tuple is >= 9:00; zero iterations accepted). RULING:
  **FIX-NOW** (two caveats: repeated projection label = error or named warning; looped label = per-label, not
  per-tuple, with the in-item spelling for a per-tuple compare).
- **F5 SEARCHED MODE** (SIMULATE-6; CONFIRMED). The §8 example lines are whole programs; searched they accept
  `19:30` (span `9:30`), `1.40`, `11.4`, `1.100`, `0.1.4.0`. All 35 stated rows keep their answers. RULING:
  **FIX-NOW** (doc: one sentence and a pinned form, optionally two searched rows).

## Lessons for the guide (ACCEPTED by the manager 2026-11-15 as process changes; see below)

- When a revision MOVES a fact's producer from the library to the AUTHOR (here: labels from the body's own `t1..t3`
  to the author's pattern), re-run every derived form that uses `!` over author inputs with an OPTIONAL, a REPEATED and
  a NON-DIGIT field: a negation turns "false on unset" into "true on unset". Two lenses independently found this in
  minutes; the evidence populations were all always-set.
- An evidence table row that shows a "refusal" must name where the refusal comes from; an emulator's own `raise` is a
  control sharing a source with the subject.
