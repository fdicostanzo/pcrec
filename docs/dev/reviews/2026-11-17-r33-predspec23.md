## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. When a revision RE-LEGALISES an operator (here NOT on conditions), re-run every SAME row, every warning predicate
   and every compile error's domain whose premise was "X never happens": rev 22's "UNKNOWN and FALSE drop the same
   rows" silently underwrote L83, `default.unused` (b) and the §5.2 error (F1, F7, F8). Candidate addition to the
   fix-induced lens: "for an operator a revision makes legal again, list every rule whose stated reason assumed it
   absent".
2. When the evidence engine and the text agree on every number, the gaps are in what the ENGINE decides that the
   TEXT does not (F2, F4): a lens should grep the engine's header for its own readings and check each is a sentence
   of the subject (the predspec23b header said "the next branch when its filters reject"; the text never did).
