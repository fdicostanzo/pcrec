### EMITTED-1 (CONFIRMED; size, with a measurement-validity edge) — the lever's site text is 2.9x (SWAR) to 6x (x86-64-v3) the fn-memchr body it replaces, because the verify chain and the mask are rendered more than once
- Repro: `build/pcrec-harness [--harness=mfind] --features all -p on|off --pattern '(ABC)'` (+ `-fmemfn-simd=x86-64|x86-64-v3`), `gcc -O2 -fno-inline -c`, `nm -S`: off `ofsskip__body` 165 B; on SWAR `__body` 484 B; at x86-64-v3 `__body` 436 + `__w16` 267 + `__w32` 297 = 1000 B. Whole-object .text (inlined, -O2): +223 B (SWAR), +431 B (w16), +695 B (w32) for a ONE-site pattern; `(abc)(abc)` (two sites) 92+157 -> 329+452 B. Files: emitted/fz/w/{r12,w16-r12,w32-r12,r15}/{on,off}.c.
- Cause (memfn/src/vrun.c `mfind_text`): the body writes the VERIFY CHAIN twice (the short-span byte loop and the mask loop) and the mask computation twice (full block and final block); each SIMD helper writes the chain once more, and x86-64-v3 always emits the w16 rung beside w32, so a v3 site carries FOUR copies of the chain plus the SWAR body for spans < 32. The chain is as wide as the predicate (the guarded_max comment's ~4 KB w16 example).
- Why it matters for an instrument: the ver-6 cards time ONE site in the slot harness, where I-cache pressure is nil, so a fused WIN on find_hit@6 cannot see the 3-6x text the form would cost in a real artifact with many sites (D197's "win triggers building" would then build on a number that omits this cost). The split shape D197 names would render one chain per site.
- Also seen (consistent, not a defect): `<PREFIX>_RUN_WORDS` doubles under the lever for a word-compared chain (`abc[0-9]+xyz`: 1 -> 2) because the body WRITES the compare twice; the helpers' copies are excluded (bracketed). Matches match_api.md 6.3.10 2's definition (compares the text writes).
- Fix: one chain per form: fold the final block into the loop (the masked final block can feed the same `while (m)` by a shared tail) and route the short-span byte loop through the same stream (e.g. a final block over a zero-padded/masked word is not possible without over-read, so a `goto` to one chain site, or render the byte loop as a 1-lane stream that jumps into the same check). Severity: size (plus design note for the card). Effort: small (renderer only). Blast radius: lever-only text; no default byte moves.
- Anchor: memfn/src/vrun.c mfind_text (the `if (!fall)` byte loop and the `else` final block).

### EMITTED-2 (SUSPECTED; speed / design of the timing read) — at SIMD-off the lever's SWAR word scans 8 bytes per ~9 ops against glibc's runtime-dispatched AVX2/EVEX memchr, so a find_byte@6 loss of mfind-swar measures search WIDTH, not fusion
- What the asm shows (gcc 15 -O2, `(ABC)`, emitted/fz/w/r12/on.s): per 8-byte block: one 8-byte load, xor, and, add, or, not, and, test+branch, plus the `e - i >= 8` block test; per candidate: tzcnt, shift, add, the chain, `m &= m-1`. The old body calls `memchr@PLT` once per candidate (glibc picks its AVX2/EVEX kernel at load time on the zen4 box regardless of the artifact's -march), then restarts at cand+1+k. So: SPARSE long subjects (find_byte@6, miss-only): SWAR expected several x SLOWER than fn-memchr; DENSE rejected candidates (find_hit@6 `reject` dim): SWAR expected faster (no call + prologue per candidate, no re-entry). ## Standing questions

### Sibling of a family
The mfind renderer is the third member of the "one block, mask of candidates, hand each to a check" family: vrun-w16/w32 (run scan with the KB second filter and padded verify, over fn-pair), vany (`mf_kernel_find_any`, 2-3 bytes, standalone, unrouted) and now mfind (1 byte, the offset-skip verify chain). All three hand-roll the same pieces: broadcast/compare/movemask spellings (`ops_of`), the guarded entry test with fall-through to the next rung, the final overlapped block with lanes below `i` masked, and the `ctz; m &= m-1` stream. mfind_text already reuses vrun's `ops_of` and vrun's contract macro; the final-block masking and the stream loop are written a third time (vany's final block deliberately differs: no mask-off, since its consumer re-checks). At three members this is the forest-for-trees threshold.

### General form + unlocks (with triggers)
General form: ONE block-mask renderer parameterised by (lane source: SWAR word | level vector; member test: 1 byte | 2-3 bytes | run first-filter + second filter; consumer: verify chain in place | return first | callback stream; final-block policy: mask-off-below-i | re-hand). vrun, vany and mfind become rows of one declared table (pcrec-decisions-as-first-match-tables), and D190's ruled split shape (T-FIND search row + S-mask stream row + RESTART variant) falls out as the (search, stream) factorisation of the same renderer, which is exactly what D197's win branch would build.
Unlocks: (1) the single-piece cells the kit-grid ver-6 header says are owed (search vs walk; stream RESTART vs MASK) become two renderings of the same site differing in ONE variable (apples-to-apples), instead of the bundle compare; (2) the chain-once text shape (EMITTED-1's fix) lands once for every member; (3) vany gets routed for free when a 2-3 byte site appears.
Triggers: build only on D197's WIN branch (the fused win is the owner's stated trigger), or when a fourth hand-rolled block-mask loop is proposed; on a LOSS, the shared form is not needed and mfind stays an instrument. Until then: file it, no build (build-under-measurement).
