Owner: I faster earns "the speed dial option can be always inline"; the DEFAULT stays sub-span loops with a measured head. CONTRARY to his direction. Also contrary to the reply's own §6 Q1 "a choice must hold in every regime" (K-1): one regime's win at one k blocks the default for every card.
