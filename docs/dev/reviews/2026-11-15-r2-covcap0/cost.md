### COST-1 (design/maintenance; SUSPECTED, strong; effort S-M; blast: the build's delivery bar) "No abi event" (§2.3, G5) contradicts D76/D94 as the recursion identity gate enforces it

Precedent in `docs/dev/history/abi_changelog.md`: the cover route's own landing was abi 92 (:226-233, "its search body, stamps ... move"), the VM hat row abi 62 (:642), and D148's ruling accepts "the abi bump it costs (offset-k selections move under utf8)". The design's own "build must still grep ... to CONFIRM" (§2.3) is answered by the grep: the gate pins the mover. What to do: say in §2.3/G5 that the build is an abi event (bump, re-pin A+B, changelog entry, D94's reader grep, the suites that count) or argue D76 reads "selection" differently. Cost: pre-release bump is cheap (memory pcrec-abi-changes-pre-release) but it is a listed ritual, not "no event"; G5's "no pcrec abi bump" would have to be dropped or a ruling asked.
