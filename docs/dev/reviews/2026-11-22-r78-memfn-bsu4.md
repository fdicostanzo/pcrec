## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **A harness's floors must count what was DRIVEN, not only what was GENERATED.** These must be part of the check: per-placement runs, per-layer runs, and a self-witness for each guard page. Here 4 mutations disabling half the promised regime passed green, while every generation floor held (G2V-1).
2. **A sabotage row cited as a guard-page witness must be benign without the guard.** Otherwise it witnesses the answer leg, not the guard (PLANTSB-2).
3. **A check that holds an ORDER over a table must pin every row ahead of the moved one, not only the ones the design names.** Here T-deny was left out (PLANTSA-2).
