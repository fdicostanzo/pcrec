## Q53-Q55 for Frank (the kit session's recommendations)

**Q53: the libc record.** Should the stamp record the libc functions the
search code calls?
- (a) A second every-artifact line, `<PREFIX>_MEMFN_LIBC`. **RECOMMENDED**
  with B4/B5's refinement:
  - it lists the libc functions the artifact's code CALLS (the whole
    artifact, comments/strings excluded), as a source-level inventory;
  - fixed-size idiom loads (`memcpy` of a constant 1-8 bytes) are
    EXCLUDED, because they are register loads, not calls;
  - its control derives the names from the compile (`nm -u` of a
    `-O0 -fno-builtin` object, minus the same constant-size rule), and
    shares no source with the kit or pcrec's lists;
  - born with `MEMFN_FORMS` in R4a′.
- (b) A field inside `MEMFN_FORMS`. Rejected by addendum 3's
  `none`-iff-identical rule.
- (c) "Search code only", as rev 4.3 wrote it. Not recommended: no
  pcrec-side check can delimit "search code", so the control cannot be
  independent.
- (d) The inventory WITH idiom loads. Simpler to check, but R4d would
  read memchr → memcpy, a misleading record.

**Q54: N7 under completeness.** Is the encoding seam's span compare
(`$_span_match[_caseless]`) a site in the migration's sense?
**RECOMMENDED: yes**, listed `pending`, with B6's corrections:
- the seam's owner is D58/DD-12, not D23;
- the definition widens to "search or span-compare site";
- C17 scans `src/enc/`;
- M7 bumps `MF_VOCAB` for a run-time-operand `mismatch`.
The fold and encoding semantics stay the encoding's, and the seam entry
builds the `mf_site`. The alternative, a third manifest state ("owned
elsewhere"), is the half-migration addendum 5 rules out.

**Q55: the kit's plan at SIMD-off.** Addenda 3 and 4 together mean
`MEMFN_FORMS` is constant `none` on every default artifact until R4f
(B1). **RECOMMENDED: accept that**, written down:
- At the default build, a scalar-layer change (R4d, M5′, any re-plan) is
  attributed by its abi event's movers census and its `--memfn=no-NAME`
  row. The bench labels kit state by the build recipe it records (abi,
  pcrec commit, argv; B2).
- C11's FORMS half is UNREACHED until the first SIMD-on form (B3).
- pcrec's own stamps and notes stop describing the scan form (`@idx`,
  `REQ_BYTE`, the [OPT-REQPOS] form half) in R4d's spec hunk (A3).
- `<PREFIX>_DFA_PREFILTER_OFFSETS` already shows M5′'s chosen offsets
  (B11).

The alternatives, each FILED with the trigger "a bench consumer asks":
- (b) an always-present `<PREFIX>_MEMFN_OPTS` line carrying the
  `--memfn=` string. Cheap, D81-compliant, and free if born in R4a′.
- (c) a third line naming the kit's plan, with its own abi event.

If Frank wants attribution IN the artifact now, (b) is the cheaper of
the two and should ride R4a′.
