## Headline

**Rev 1's fixes hold in intent, and the drill reproduces (23/23, byte-identical output), but the
drill is NOT the tables as written, and several fixes introduced new defects.** No finding shows the
experiment crowning a wrong placement with certainty (the verifier downgraded every "answer" claim to
design), but two are answer-RISKS: the pair tests run on a different scale from the regret the margin
and row 1 are argued on, and can order a pair the other way (X2); and the K-fit/robustness/strata
readings never name which of the two new site sets they read (X3). Beyond those:

- **The drill is not the text.** In nine places verdict_drill.py implements something the text does
  not say, and three drill scenarios land on target only through that (D12b skips the reach gate, D13
  carries no row-1 data, every scenario uses a regret-scale d and every-site differences). Read
  literally, condition (iii) makes SUPERIOR unreachable (17 of 24 scenarios off target). (X1, X6, X12,
  X25)
- **The power analysis is partly wrong.** The EQUIV column uses the zero-difference formula; the
  stage-0 floor of 40 sites is ~0.78 power, not 0.84 (bootstrap: ~45-50 needed). Superiority with all
  four conditions is ~0.65-0.71 at the stated n (80 % needs ~20 differing sites), and "16 differing
  sites whatever the gap" is false near δ by the design's own script (~35 at 2.5 points). (X8, X9)
- **New cycles and holes from the fixes.** Row 1 (the early stop, stage 2) leans on C-SCORE (stage 3)
  and the sha guard refuses H before the rules exist (X5). The H-frame consumes H-sealed (X4).
  Without L-KIT, row 1's backend-only verdict is unreachable as written (X6). C-BLIND's null rate is 0
  by construction (X7). T2 has no rows for NO PLACEMENT BEATS FM / UNRESOLVABLE / 6b (X11).
- **The timing re-cast re-defines regime_eval's terms and under-costs grid1.** NULL_c collapses to one
  A/A gap (not regime_eval's definition, cited "by name"); halves of two use grid1's lower-of-two
  median; D is verdict-bearing at 3 launches; ext cells have no libpcre2 leg; grid1's quiet gate
  proceeds when loaded; C-XH's bench window-end fallback is the wrong mechanism, its plant may not
  turn it red, and its floors are misquoted. **grid2 (`kit-grid@2`, regime_eval's own harness) already
  supplies base#c, the re-queue, streamed windows and the libpcre2 wiring; rev 1 never cites it.**
  (X13-X15, X20-X26)

The design may proceed to rev 2 without a build go; a third, short re-check centred on the pair-test
scale, the drill-vs-text alignment and the grid2 decision is proposed.

## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **"By name" cites redefine.** Three rev-1 fixes cite another document's term "by name" or "as in
   X" and then define it differently (NULL_c, N, C7's halves). Proposed critics.md lesson: a
   fix-induced or premise lens checks every "by name"/"as X defines" cite against X's text, formula by
   formula.
2. **A drill script is a second statement of the table.** "Implements §9.3 exactly as written" was
   false in nine places; three scenarios landed only through the divergences. The simulate lens's
   "implement from the TEXT first, then diff against the script" order found them. Proposed skill
   text for the simulate lens: "when the design ships its own drill or simulator, implement the rule
   from the text BEFORE reading the script, then diff".
3. **A fix that adds a SET leaves every other reader's set unstated** (X3, X4): the r7 sibling-field
   rule applied to populations. Proposed addition to the fix-induced lens question: "when a fix adds a
   population or site set, list every metric, control and row and the set each reads".
