### The deciding reason and its numbers (design)
- **C1 (READY-1) FIX-NOW, design; manager ruling before the go.** The 64 KB block that "decides" D-1 is today's
  `pcrec_arena_own_slot` (arena.c:42-45: the AOwn record comes from the arena's own blocks, arena.c:17-19 opens a
  `max(sz, ABLOCK_MIN)` block). The note's (A) ("heap blocks join through `Arena.owned`") does not say where the owned
  record lives. S10-S12 put nothing else in a child (`nb`, `tail`, `o` are heap StrBufs), so an (A) whose record comes
  from the parent arena or from `calloc` takes no child block: about 16 B, against (B)'s 256 B per rewrite. The design's
  escape clause ("keeping its owned records outside the child, which is (B)'s heap record") mislabels this. Fix: state
  D-1's deciding reason as the FORM (kinds, LIFO unlink, generation stamp, L6's bounded free: (B)'s registry by
  another name), give (A)'s cost as "0 to 64 KB by where the owned record lives", and drop "the B9 deciding number"
  from Standing question 1. The mechanism does not flip; under r102's B9 ruling the manager notes the changed reason
  before the owner's go.
- **C2 (READY-2) FIX-NOW, doc.** The numbers re-derive exactly (min 111,520; median 736,368; 8.9%; 4,994 over 5%;
  6,607 over 1%; p90 differs by rank convention only). But column 4 is rc: 1,385 rows are refusals, 819 of them at
  exactly 111,520 B (parse rejects, e.g. `\A*`), which run no emitter and no scope. Successful compiles only (6,017):
  median 821,680 (+8.0%), over 5% on 3,782, over 1% on 5,393. Also "the k155fix base reads a +48 B constant" is false
  (+48 on 7,211; +65,608 on 20, -4,000 on 25, ...), and today's tree is the AFTER side.
- **C3 (READY-3) FIX-NOW, design.** The liveness premise HOLDS on a probe: 146 of 150 sampled successful corpus compiles
  reach their final peak inside `pcrec_memfn_stamps_render` (gdb on k155fix's injected binary). But the cited evidence
  did not show it (k155fix moved the buffers INTO the arena, where retention shows at any later peak), C0(b)'s STOP
  tests a premise that is true while C1's false premise has no STOP, its population includes the 19% refusals, and
  "the peak instant's live builders attributed" needs an instrument that exists nowhere before T1. Fix: state the
  method and the population (successful compiles), and record the 146/150 read.

### Fix-induced holes in the LIFO check (memory, design)
- **C4 (FIXIND-4) FIX-NOW, memory (latent).** L1's test runs in `pcrec_scope_close` and `pcrec_scope_free` only. The four
  registering calls (`pcrec_scope_malloc`, `_calloc`, `_sb`, `_arena`) push with no check, so `scope_malloc(&S1)` while
  S2 is open lands the entry above m2, and `close(&S2)` frees it while S1's client still uses it (B1's class, drill in
  `review/fixind.md` D10). A dead handle pushes an unchecked base entry (an un-refused hold, around L7). Fix: one shared
  `scope_check(s)` in all six calls; the free-the-mark unit test gains the step.
- **C5 (FIXIND-5) FIX-NOW, design.** The stamp clause (`a->open->gen == s->gen`) has no control that must fail:
  sabotage (4) is refused by the pointer half; no row deletes the stamp; unit test (4b) fails without the stamp only if
  `calloc` reuses the address. Probe: glibc 2.43 reuses it (`same=1`), under `-fsanitize=address` it does not
  (`same=0`), so under `make asan` 4b passes with the stamp gone. Fix: 4b asserts the reuse precondition (or forges the
  handle with the live mark's address and an old `gen`, no allocator luck), plus a sabotage row "drop the gen compare"
  detected by 4b, run with REACH_ONLY.
- **C19 (FIXIND-10) FIX-NOW for (i) and (vi), DOC for the rest.** (i) L1's test dereferences NULL on a zero handle with
  no scope open (`NULL == NULL`, then `a->open->gen`): test `a->open` first. (vi) The drill unit tests run on a detached
  arena where refusals `abort`, yet continue past refusing steps: name the fork-per-refusal shape. DOC: (ii) the
  post-condition's `a->open == prev` conjunct is vacuous (step 4 just assigned it) and `below` always equals
  `mark->next`; (iii) `pcrec_scope_open`'s step order is unstated; (iv) `pcrec_err_chan(NULL)` must be legal (detached
  arenas); (v) `gen` resets in `pcrec_arena_free`, so stamps are unique per attempt only: say a handle cannot survive an
  attempt (true: its frame is below the setjmp), or keep `gen` across the free.
