### H1. SIMULATE-1: G2's defect survives for BETWEEN-RUN noise. VERIFY HOLDS.

**The defect.** The per-pool floor reads only launch-to-launch scatter, so one regime whose arms carry a per-arm
offset still breaks the acceptance.
- **Repro.** The drill is cards.py's own `analyse`/`noise_drill` over 300 nights. Each arm of one regime, its A/A arms
  included, gets an offset `exp(N(0, tau))` across all 5 launches.

| case | SIMULATE P(h* != 64) | VERIFY P(h* != 64) |
|---|---|---|
| not-warm, tau 1 % | 0.030 | 0.050 |
| not-warm, tau 2 % | 0.227 | 0.257 |
| not-warm, tau 3 % | 0.337 | 0.337 |
| near-miss, tau 2 % (a regime read today) | | 0.290 |
| tau 2 % in EVERY regime (control) | 0.003 | 0.003 |

- **Why.** The floor is `f(r, l) = max(f_AA, launch term)`.
  - The launch term cannot see an arm-level offset.
  - The A/A term is one night-wide RMS, which dilutes one regime's offset: r36 G2's mechanism, one level up.
  - Rev 8's heterogeneous drills scale only the launch sigma. The drill's noise model therefore shares its source
    with the estimator it tests (learnings §3), and the 0.000s cannot see this shape.
- **The premise rev 8 uses to reject a per-(regime, layer) A/A floor is false.** Rev 8 says the night times "ONE A/A
  pair per (regime, layer) ... needs about eight A/A pairs". In fact the night already times about 40 hash-equal arms
  in 9 pools per (regime, layer): I's pool members, with all 10 arms at lit40 and abc60. That gives about 31
  between-arm contrasts with no extra arm (VERIFY counted them under the `h >= k` model). Membership is measured on a
  real night, so the count is the model's expectation.
- **Masked today:** step (5) fires on every real night.
- **PROPOSED: FIX-NOW (design + script, M)**, in the same lane as H2.
  - The per-(regime, layer) floor adds a term from the RMS of the hash-equal arms' log-median contrasts in that pool.
    This is the full ratio noise: launch plus between-run.
  - The noise drill gains per-arm-offset variants: one regime at tau 2 % and 3 %, and the A/A cells spared. The
    acceptance stays the same, P(h* != 64) <= 5 % over >= 300 nights, printed.
  - The text withdraws "one A/A pair ... eight times the A/A arms".
- **Alternative:** the kit manager may rule H1 PLAN, with the hard trigger "the bench's NOT-WARM / short-subject
  methods replace the approximations", plus a refusal in `rule` until then. r36 G2 was ruled FIX-NOW over the same
  alternative.

## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **Simulate lens, BETWEEN-RUN noise.** A floor estimated from launch-to-launch scatter must be drilled with
   ARM-LEVEL offsets: one per arm, held across all its launches, in one regime, sparing the A/A cells. A drill that
   varies only the launch sigma shares its source with that estimator (r37 H1: 0.000 on every rev 8 variant, 0.257 at
   a 2 % per-arm offset).
2. **Simulate lens, SMALLEST POOL.** A pooled (robust) reading is drilled at its SMALLEST pool with a WHOLE-ARM shift
   (all launches of one member). A median-moving split in a large pool proves only the large pool (r37 H2: hex32's
   3-arm pool 0.000, hex64's 2-arm pool 0.34).
3. **Check-adequacy lens, THE DEFECT'S ARM.** A reach control renders the arm the defect DEGENERATES TO (an ignored
   deny gives the default head; a non-moving movers file gives lever-64 bytes) and counts where it differs. Proving
   the detector's regex works on some other arm is not reach (r37 H4, H7; r36 G7 was the first).
4. **Check-adequacy lens, SHARED COUNTERS.** When a fix adds a pin or control to a pass that shares a counter or
   floor with an older population, plant the fix's DELETION. A floor met by the older population alone is green
   on it (r37 H5, H6).
