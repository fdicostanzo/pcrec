## Lessons for the guide

1. A warning whose text makes a SEMANTIC claim ("always holds and adds nothing") gets its reach tested by the claim, not only by the positions: plant a NON-edge anchor in each fire-position and check the claim (SIMULATE-1; the lanes' table (C) tested only edge anchors, so its "where it fires, with and without give the same answer" column was true of the population and false of the rule).
2. When a revision folds "X is decided after macros are expanded", drill the hand expansion against the macro call for every row of the table: a rule that tells them apart is keyed off authorship, i.e. the name (SIMULATE-1/3).
3. A restatement of an earlier decision ("rev 13 (23) said X, rev 14 restates") gets a diff of the two sentences: rev 14 dropped rev 13's hole exclusion (FIXIND-8).
