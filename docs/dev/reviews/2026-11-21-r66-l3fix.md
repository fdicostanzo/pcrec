## Lessons for the guide (proposed)
1. **A route-keyed fact needs the FLAG table, not the engine name** (EMIT-2): a walk parameter that predicts "a
   downstream row can exist" from options must be checked against every flag that FORCES that row on (here
   `-fprefilter`), not only against the engine. Proposed critics.md line under contract: "for a fact derived from
   options to predict a later route, enumerate the `forced-on`/`forced-off` rows of that route's admission table".
2. **"Nothing to keep" must be stated over the downstream's landmark, not the producer's object** (EMIT-3): a rule
   protecting a re-entry is keyed on what the re-entry reads (run OR byte), or it leaks a class.
3. **A new counter printed by a check is a floor or a deletion** (CHECK-1): the K35 shape recurring inside a fix lane.
