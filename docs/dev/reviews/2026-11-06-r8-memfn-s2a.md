## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **A trace record added as a witness should be a TEXT IMAGE of the whole handed object, not of the new field alone.** SEAM-1:
   a record scoped to the unit's own field left the neighbouring field (value) of the same struct unwitnessed, and its stated
   purpose (the column-swap class) covered both. Proposed addition to the seam round trip lens: "for a record added as a
   witness, plant a wrong value in each NEIGHBOURING field of the struct the record describes; each must move the record."
2. **Floors written as `>=` with slack let a deleted fixture pass** (CHECKS-2). That is already in the check lens ("plant a
   DELETED line"). It worked; keep it.
