## Findings (consolidated; PROPOSED dispositions)

### Answer-level

**S1 — §5.4 A3's "the move at `k <= 0` ... is sound and loose at a negative K ... a precision change, not a soundness one" is false: under BACK it reports a match before the start offset** (FIXIND-5; verifier V-A HOLDS). Under L3 the landmark (the run's start) lies `t` bytes BEFORE the attempt. `emit_req_handoff_rest` (`emit_dfa.c:1167-1204`) emits nothing at `k <= 0` (byte), so the body starts at the kit's hit `c`; its comment's invariant "`c >= posvar` always" (`:1158`) is what BACK breaks, and `handoff_position - posvar` is a `size_t` that wraps under utf8. `emit_req_landmark_move` (`:7793-7813`) re-asks only `if (landmark_position < pos)` and then sets `pos = landmark_position`. Witness: `(?<=ab)ab[a-z]*q` on `abababq` from 3: libpcre2 M 4 7. Its spelled twin `abab[a-z]*q` takes the handoff (`RX_REQ_HANDOFF "0"`, measured; the VM route also hands off: `x(?<=ab)ab[a-z]*q` gives `"1"`). Scan floor `max(0, 3-2) = 1`, hit `c = 2`; the stated move starts the body at 2 and reports (2,7). The only sound move is `max(posvar, c + t)`, i.e. `max(posvar, c - K)` with K signed and no `size_t` wrap; at re-entry the re-ask condition becomes `landmark_position + t < pos`. The text FORCES this error: it states the unsound move as sound. **Proposed: FIX-NOW** (A3's cell; name `emit_req_handoff_rest` and `emit_req_landmark_move` among L3's changed sites; add W-L10 = this witness, red under the move A3 now states).
