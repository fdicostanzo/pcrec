## Reflection

The note's method held: its census and citation discipline survived
~50-point adversarial re-derivation with zero numeric discrepancies.
Both critics converged independently on the single blocker, and it is
the exact failure §13 item 5 warned about — an unreproduced survey
claim — living in the one section whose survey was NOT re-run. The
standing lesson (checks that share a source with what they check) has a
requirements-note corollary: a "grep returns N hits" claim is a check,
and it must be re-runnable and re-run. The fix pass is targeted, not a
rewrite; [DD-13b] does not start until it lands.
