## BLOCKING (all ACCEPTED; the revision may not merge without each)

- **[E1] The lowering never reaches the VM emitter.** compile.c:1228 hands
  the VM emitter the AST ROOT, not the IR; emit_vm.c reads u.cls.bits at
  four sites. A lowering placed at §2.1's position miscompiles every VM
  artifact (all of M6's modules) — silent, not a refusal. The design must
  state the lowering's position in compile.c's actual pass chain (the
  AST→AST rewrite is the likely repair) and re-derive §5.6's premises
  under it.
- **[E2] §2.1's byte-instance rule + §2.7's code-point-space complement
  refuse every negated class under --encoding=byte** (`[^a]`, `\D`, `\W`,
  `.` …), i.e. stage 1's whole acceptance corpus. The complement universe
  must be per-encoding, which today's PcrecEnc cannot carry (no
  max-code-point field) — a seam change to record per D58, or a symbolic
  negation carried to the lowering; the design must choose and must keep
  the explicit `\x{>FF}`-refuses-under-byte cell distinct from the
  complement case.
- **[C1] Stage 1's identity gate stands alone** — no structural check
  proves the interval pipeline exists; a no-op refactor passes the same
  bar. Pair the gate with codegen/structural assertions (learnings §4's
  named combination).
- **[C2] The §8.1 positive control is unfloored** (K35). State floors as
  lookaround §9.2 does.
- **[C3] No sabotage row declares SAB_REACH/SAB_REACH_POP** ([MECH-REACH],
  standing since 2026-08-25). All eight rows gain reach declarations at
