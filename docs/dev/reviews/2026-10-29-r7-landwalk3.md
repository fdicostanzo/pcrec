## Lessons for the guide (proposed)
1. **A relocate inherits the TARGET row's declarations, and every reader must know it.** A field
   read as "the selected row's" (`cand_locate_of(cx)->…`) is wrong for a cell visited on another
   row's behalf. When a design adds a relocate, grep every `cand_locate_of(cx)->` read reachable
   from the target's cells (FIX-1: two such reads, one crash, one silent speed loss, no check).
2. **A check that skips a zero/unstated field cannot see a new row that leaves it unstated.**
   `table-recover-not-total` skips `recover == 0`; the walk's missing hand passed it. Rows that
   declare hands state every hand field explicitly (memory `pcrec-no-silent-defaults`).
3. **Deleting a deny conjunct moves the deny's readers, not just its spelling.** SLOT-6 moved the
   deny to `.c.deny`; the build gate, which does not go through `cand_select`, lost it (FIX-4).
4. **A recommended call form is priced, not only proved.** SLOT-10's `_match` call is
   answer-identical and quadratic under an opt-in flag; a design that reuses an ENTRY reuses its
   prologue.
