### HOUSE-4  CONFIRMED  design/altitude  S  window.c:129-138 + kit.h:376-387: kit-wide pick primitive housed in a family file

### HOUSE-6  CONFIRMED  design  XS  window.c c_bound (~922-931): re-derives which row ran
c_bound picks `m->has_stop ? p->yset : rd_set(d, p)`, i.e. repeats c_stop_holds / c_floor_holds (window.c ~889-890) inline instead of being two rows (C-bound-yes after C-yes, C-bound-floor after C-floor) or reading a recorded field. The table's own `holds`/`apply` pair for C-bound is c_always, so the row's real predicate lives in an if inside its apply: a declared row whose condition is bypassed (architecture.md "decisions are declared tables"). Cheap fix: the two meets chosen by the existing `c_stop_holds(d)` call, or split rows.

### HOUSE-9  SUSPECTED  naming  XS  gate_cand fields (kit.h:984-993) and window.c `walked`
