# 2026-10-05 r2 — light D6 panel: [MEMFN] integration.md revision 2 (K0, the capability-and-price query)

Subject: `docs/design/memfn/integration.md` rev 2 (lane memfnk0, merged 49b5b80d). Frank's ruling under review: pcrec carries NO architecture knowledge; it passes an opaque token to the kit's K0 query, gets a price list back, and has one arch-blind `kit` row. Two read-only critics:

- **k0crit1** (opus): soundness of the price model, re-derived from the published Linux/Mac transcripts.
- **k0crit2** (sonnet): boundary, contract and checks.

Verdict: the ARCHITECTURE holds. The selection re-derives in the right direction from the published numbers (≤64 B select, unbounded decline), and opacity is achievable. The MEASUREMENT LAYER is not ready, in four ways:

- the price regime (dependent vs independent, hit vs miss) flips verdicts and is unspecified;
- "corners prove dominance" is a model assumption with a counterexample;
- pcrec's own rows are priced from the kit's reference loops, so the shared-source trap applies, and pcrec already holds measured machine constants;
- recalibration moving emitted bytes without an abi event breaks D103.

The checks are weak in the usual places: the arch-blindness regex misses most arch vocabulary; the timed control has no home, no long subjects and no armv8 arm; and the sabotage rows name timed detectors mech cannot run. All findings ACCEPTED (manager); revision lane memfnk0r3.

## Reflection

Fourth panel in two days. The pattern is now unmistakable: every design that introduces a measured quantity gets the same three findings. (1) The measurement regime is unstated and flips the answer. (2) The control is computed from the same source as the subject. (3) The new data moves emitted bytes without the abi ritual. Worth a standing line in the design-note template: "name the regime; name the independent control; name what moves when the data is regenerated."
