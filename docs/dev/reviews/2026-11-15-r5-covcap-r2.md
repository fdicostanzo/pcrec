## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

- A design whose premise is "today's tree does X" (here: "`[ab]{1000}` lists 24") was wrong on a one-compile check that two critics found independently in minutes; the design brief template should require each "today" number to carry the exact command that produced it.
- A design that keeps a named helper ("keeps `sw_take`") while changing its contract (cross-depth identity) is a recurring shape (three critics found it): the premise lens's brief should ask "for each KEPT function, does the new text require a property it lacks".
