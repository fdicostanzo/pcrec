## Answer for the launcher's REQ_BYTE question (contract lens, verified)

The PICK does **not** honour "the byte lies in what the REPORTED match contains" under `\K`, before or after E2; it does
honour D191's "a byte every match (attempt) CONSUMES" everywhere. Against libpcre2 reported bounds: BASE already violates
it (`abc\K` 'b' vs (3,3); `xy\Kz` 'x' vs (3,4); `zq\Ke` 'z'); E2 adds new violations wherever the joined run's rarest
member sits before `\K` (`ab\Kcd` c->b vs (4,6); `q\Kab` b->q; `z\Keee` e->z vs (1,4); `[a-z]ab\Kcd`; `ab\K(?<=ab)cd`;
`qz\K(?<=qz)wv`) and removes one (`xy\Kz` x->z). Lookaround joins (E-5) never move the byte outside the match: no body is
read. **No answer moves** (every reader scans `[search_from, n)` and attempts start at or after it): 0 diffs vs libpcre2
at every start offset, both engines, ~60 patterns (contract) + 12 (verifier) + the emitted lens's ~680 patterns.
