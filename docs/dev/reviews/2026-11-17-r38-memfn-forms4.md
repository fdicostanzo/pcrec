## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **Simulate lens, NOISE AT EVERY GRANULARITY.** A pooled noise estimate is drilled with noise confined to each
   level of the structure it pools over (night, regime, layer, PATTERN, arm) and with a STEP in launch order between
   a pool's members; the drill that varies noise at the estimator's own pooling level shares its source with it
   (r38 J1 0.40-0.69 at one pattern's 2 % offset; J2 0.15-0.46 at a 4-5 % step; both 0.000 under every ruled drill).
2. **Check-adequacy lens, DERIVED SPLITS.** A counter split by SUBTRACTION (`total - part`) is not a split: plant
   the part's deletion and watch the derived half move silently (r38 J6: 8 -> 16, green).
3. **Coordinator, DETECTORS BEFORE LAUNCH.** Launch critics only after the coordinator's detector runs finish (or
   name in-progress logs in COMMON): a critic read an empty S3304 log and re-ran it (r38).
