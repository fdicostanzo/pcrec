## Panel verdict in one paragraph

The note's CENTRAL result survives, strengthened: the repaired
possessification rule (unique-iteration + non-nullable + disjoint-or-exact)
is sound for GREEDY quantifiers at 0 counterexamples against BOTH oracles
(the soundness critic re-ran the full 5,016×260 family against libpcre2 —
§8 item 6's own recorded gap — and attacked the transitive-FOLLOW line,
body assertions, and capture-offset blindness with failing-direction
controls; all HELD). The two headline censuses (18%/82%) recount exactly
from raw data and are robust to leave-one-out. What did NOT survive: the
note's LAZY extension (F1, a real refutation the note's own probe
structurally could not see), a §3.4 derivation stated without its
zero-iteration clause (42% of its own validation population), and the
rung-census "distinct (pattern, quantifier)" figures (wrong once, twice
repeated; unreproducible as a column; one mixed-source table row).

## Findings and dispositions

### S-F1 (REFUTED — the one design-level hit). Lazy quantifiers are NOT
possessive-equivalent on the disjointness arm when the rest of the pattern
is nullable. §2.3's lazy paragraph claimed greedy/lazy/possessive span
agreement; measured: 316/316 diverging cells (python AND libpcre2 agree),
all disjointness-arm, all nullable-rest; 0 on the exact-count arm. Repro:
`a{1,3}?` on "aaaa" → (0,1) lazy vs (0,3) possessive. §2.3 step 5's "the
follow's first-byte test fails" is vacuous when the follow is nullable —
greedy tops out at the chain's top where the vacuous follow also succeeds
(why the greedy differential stayed clean); lazy stops at the bottom.
Blast radius: 10 of the corpus's 183 possessifiable quantifiers are
lazy+disjoint+nullable-rest (4 bounded), including `a{1,3}?` in
tests/base/bounded_repeats.rxt; realistic set: 0 (no lazy quantifiers).
No live miscompile — nothing is implemented; this is the design-first
process catching it before an emitter exists.
DISPOSITION: fix-with-measurement. Rewrite §2.3's lazy paragraph and §8
item 3: lazy on the disjointness arm additionally requires a NON-NULLABLE
follow / cannot-end-here conjunct (probe_possess.py already computes
`may_end`/`end_here` and never consults it — the repair is its own dead
code). Re-run the lazified differential with the conjunct; expected 0.
§5.3's greedy-AND-lazy sweep becomes mandatory. §2.1's "zero resume
frames" must exclude the lazy/disjoint case until the conjunct holds.

### S-F2 (NARROWED). `$` in the follow is safe — proved and measured
(0/720) — but ONLY while MULTILINE is off (180/720 diverging under (?m);
upward-closure argument breaks per-newline). pcrec refuses (?m) today
(module `assertions`), so the exemption is safe NOW; the gate must be a
live !multiline check, not a comment. Failing-direction controls: \b/\B
follows give 332/720, ^ gives 80/240 — the 0 is not inert.
DISPOSITION: promote §8 item 2 / §2.7's `$` from BELIEVED to
MEASURED-WITH-GATE; state the live-gate requirement in §2.

### S-F3 (NARROWED, load-bearing wording). §3.4's backward-scan capture
derivation as WRITTEN is wrong on every ZERO-ITERATION match (both groups
must be unset; the prose's [end-2,end-1) is meaningless there). The
archived probe plainly had the clause (its own output records 6,259
zero-iteration matches of 15,036 — 42% of the validated population — at 0
mismatches); the write-up dropped it. An implementation lane building the
reverse-deterministic rung from the prose emits a wrong group on 42% of
the motivating cell's matches.
DISPOSITION: fix prose — add the zero-iteration clause. Bonus recorded:
with the clause, the derivation is also 0/… for m>0 shapes.

### S-F4 (NARROWED). §2.7 bullet 3's "wrong in the right direction" is not
a blanket property of the probe: its IGNORECASE model is unsound (python
folds case at compile time, so the probe's FIRST((?i)a) = {a}, missing A).
PCREC ITSELF IS SAFE STRUCTURALLY — src/parse/parse.c's `cls_casefold`
folds case into every A_CLASS bitmap at parse time, and every literal is
an A_CLASS, so the byte-set model is exact for caseless in the compiler.
Census impact: 1 harmless row; 18%/82% survive.
DISPOSITION: qualify §2.7 bullet 3 to categories; add the STRUCTURAL
caseless note so a porting lane does not inherit the probe's model.

### S-F5 (NARROWED; §6's claim itself now STRONGER). §6's instrument was
span-only and python-only. Rebuilt captures-aware and three-way: 15,600
cells, pcrec == libpcre2 on ALL (span and every slot); python disagrees
with libpcre2 on 106 cells, all `(|a){m,n}` captures — a two-way python
check on §6's own family would have raised a FALSE alarm against pcrec.
DISPOSITION: note §6's instrument scope in the doc; add `(|a){m,n}` as a
named cell in §5.3's dense list — a measured argument that the three-way
rule earns itself on this row.

### S-F6 (UPSTREAM). PCRE2 10.46 does not backtrack into a PRECEDING item
after a possessive/atomic BOUNDED repeat of a GROUP (`a?(?:b){0,4}+a` on
"a": PCRE2 no-match, python (0,1), correct (0,1)); both conditions
necessary (character item, `*+`, no preceding item, lazy prefix all
match). Consequence for the note: python-possessive and PCRE2-possessive
are not interchangeable oracles in exactly the swept family; §5.1's
pcrec-vs-pcrec differential (already primary) is primary for a measured
reason. These 3 cells were the only "counterexamples" in the critic's
capture-focused sweep — all PCRE2-side.
DISPOSITION: new docs/dev/upstream_issues.md row (manager); one sentence
in §2.4/§5.3 citing it.
