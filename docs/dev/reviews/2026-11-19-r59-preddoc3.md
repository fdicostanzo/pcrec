## Lessons for the guide

1. A COMPLETENESS lens is cheapest as two critics, each building its control from the sources FIRST and committing it
   as an appendix before the diff (both did, 7.5 and 10.9 min); the census appendices are the round's most reusable
   output and should be kept as TSV beside the documents for the next conversion (COMPB general form).
2. A blind READ-WHOLE pass finds what source-anchored lenses cannot: undefined primitives, contradictory readings,
   a body that cannot produce its own example (READ-4 was missed by every earlier round and by the lane's evidence,
   which ran a model of the body rather than its text: the run kit modelled `{n,}` as the count).
3. An example lens that re-implements the pieces' own model checks consistency, not truth: "92 of 92 equal" coexists
   with READ-4 because the example critic, like the lane, translated the body by its intent.
