### PREMISE-1 — "a caller compares scenarios by calling again" has nothing to compare until the revisit trigger
CONFIRMED (by reading). Severity: design. Effort: one paragraph. Blast radius: §The designer/emitter split (1), A-PLAN end state.
§The designer/emitter split (1) (span_pick.md rev 4 l.100-106) says purity "makes one plan sufficient: a caller compares scenarios by calling again". But (i) the reply carries no price (l.79 "prices and alternatives live ONLY in the trace"; trace is MFTRACE-build stderr, gate.c :929-936, never a reply field), and (ii) through stage 1 the reply does not even carry method/resume/frame (l.93-95). A caller calling twice gets two plans and no ordering between them; the trace is not a caller-readable channel (the design itself says "the artifact carries no trace", l.466-467). So comparison by re-call is impossible until the REVISIT TRIGGER (whole-artifact pricing, price beside the plan), which the design lists as a later event, not as the condition that makes the sufficiency claim true. The roadmap end state ("architecture chooses across providers under its rules", roadmap.md l.384-386) is the reader this blocks. Fix: say "one plan is sufficient ONCE the price is readable; until then scenarios are compared only by the bench", or make "price beside the plan" part of the property rather than its revisit trigger. Unlock (general form): plan(facts, frame) -> (plan, price) as the provider interface architecture reads; trigger = un-deferring whole-artifact/engine pricing.

### PREMISE-2 — the property's input list does not match the plan call's actual signature or reads
CONFIRMED (code). Severity: design/doc. Effort: small (restate the tuple). Blast radius: §The designer/emitter split (1); unit 1d's witness.
Property (l.101-103): plan = f(site facts, frame: engine/target/layer, measure set, bundle). The code:
- The plan call `mf_wdecide(const mf_art*, const mf_site*, mf_wreply*)` (window.c :1058) takes NO frame: "the decision reads no frame fact (§0)" (window.c :1061), and §The shape itself says form/handoff/site_kind are phase-2 frame facts until unit 3.0 (l.54, l.65-70). Layer is not an input either: fd_find hard-codes `q.ff.layer = CL_L_OFF` (finder.c :934); the SIMD layer reaches rows only via the per-site `policy` word (gate.c :434-445, :772). So in stage 1 "frame" is NOT an argument of the plan; it is an argument of the text call. The property is true of the stage-3.0 call, not of the call 1d makes pure.
- Measure set and bundle are not per-call arguments: they are bound once per artifact by `mf_art_begin` (compose.c :869-882: art->ms, art->an) and read through the art (finder.c :469, :626; presence.c :50 kit_byte_rate). "Callable once per scenario" for a scenario that differs in set or bundle therefore means a NEW mf_art (arena, prefix, denies) per scenario, not "calling again". The design should say which scenario axes are per-call (site, later frame) and which are per-art (set, bundle, denies, policy).
- Unlisted per-art inputs on decision paths: `art->denies` (walked by compose.c :286/:290, ofsskip.c :463/:488/:514, runcmp.c :219; mf_ask refuses when s->denies != art->denies, compose.c :966) — arguably a site fact since mf_site carries `denies`, but the art copy is what the walks read; `art->levers` (test-only, read by precheck.c :161 `implied_by`, a T-PRES decider) — outside the plan call, but a hidden per-art input of a named decider.
Fix: state the tuple as (site facts incl. denies and policy; per-art: measure set, bundle, levers; frame from unit 3.0), and scope "callable per scenario" to the per-call axes.

### PREMISE-5 — `log`, one of the two "REAL DATA" kinds, is a SYNTHESIZED corpus, and it sets the worst case that rejects the digit stops
CONFIRMED (provenance). Severity: design. Effort: small (relabel + say what it means) or owner question. Blast radius: §The pick 1 ("REAL DATA, the shipped analysis bundles"), §Robustness l.284-287 ("never resting on the authored prior alone"), D186 add. 2 guard (a).
src/findings/log.rxt header and :89/:166 `fidelity synthesized`; third_party/synth-log-lines-v1/PROVENANCE.md: "SYNTHESIZED. Not vendored from an external source", generated by gen_corpus.py (SEED 20260927) with an invented vocabulary and deliberately authored digit/'-' properties. Only `weblog` (third_party/elastic-examples-apache-logs-bc53b584, first 1,000,000 bytes of Apache combined-log lines) is real text. In smp20.out (hit 20) the worst cases of '3' (5.925), '4' (6.654), '1' (8.939), '2' (9.670) are ALL set by `log`, and the sweep's '3' region is "set by `log`" (l.314). ### PREMISE-6 — the with-data/real-kind band's Poisson `sqrt(n)` understates the bundles' sampling noise 2-5x
CONFIRMED (measured, light). Severity: design (guard (b) and the with-data departure fire on too-small bands). Effort: small (block bootstrap instead of Poisson). Blast radius: §With data l.195-196, the band l.236-240, §The measurement regime l.455.
Repro: python3 -I PREMISE/disp.py over the two vendored corpora, splitting each into 20 / 100 equal blocks and comparing per-block count variance to the mean (Poisson => var/mean ~ 1). weblog (apache_logs.txt): var/mean 'x' 18.6, 'X' 9.2, '/' 4.4, '3' 19.8, '4' 8.3 (20 blocks); 3.6-14.7 at 100 blocks. log (synthetic): '/' 2.1, '3' 5.5, '4' 24.6. Byte occurrences in line-structured text are clustered (repeated IPs, paths, ids), so the SD of a rate is 2-5x the Poisson `sqrt(n)` the design adds. For `log`, a seeded generator's output is not a sample of any population at all, so "sampling noise" has no defined meaning there. Fix: band terms from a block (line-chunk) bootstrap of the bundle, which pcrec-analyze could emit beside the counts (a `noise` row per byte); or declare synthetic kinds noise-free and say so.

### PREMISE-7 — ratio-to-best minimax is not independent of irrelevant candidates: a row's pick depends on which OTHER candidates are priced
CONFIRMED (computed on smp20.out's own prices). Severity: design (a mover attributed to the wrong row; per-row evidence under D186 assumes a row's movers are its own). Effort: medium (choose the loss form or pin the candidate set). Blast radius: §The pick 1-4, Growth rule (l.160), later rows (l.416-420), step 4 (unpriced candidates).
`loss_r(c) = price_r(c) / min_c' price_r(c')` makes every loss depend on the best candidate under r, so adding a candidate that is never picked can flip the pick between two others. Repro: python3 -I PREMISE/iia.py (prices copied from span_pick/smp20.out). At hit(PAIR) 40: candidates {X,x} and '/' only -> W({X,x}) = 3.056 (F), W('/') = 2.838 (log) -> minimax picks '/'; add '4' (never picked) -> weblog's best becomes '4' (302,235), W('/') rises to 4.055 -> pick flips to {X,x}. 16 such flips over the subsets at hit 40, 4 at hit 20 (e.g. {'3','4'} picks '4'; adding '/' flips to '3'). Consequences: (i) step 1's "candidates differ from the default only at the row's levels" makes the pick a function of the row's level set, not only of prices; (ii) step 4 ("unpriced candidate: cannot be picked") does not say whether an unpriced candidate is in the denominator — it cannot be, so when a later card lands (Growth rule: "a new method adds a T-FIND row plus its card"), sites of an EARLIER row can move between two old candidates; that mover belongs to no row's evidence. Fix options: absolute regret `price_r(c) - min price_r` has the same IIA defect; the IIA-safe forms are a pairwise test against the incumbent (depart to c only if max_r price_r(c)/price_r(default) < 1 - band, i.e. "never worse than today's under any kind by more than X, better under some"), or freeze the candidate universe per row and re-run every earlier row's census when a card lands. General form: a choice rule's axioms (IIA, monotonicity) declared in the design next to the rule; trigger: the first row whose level set differs from row 1's.

### PREMISE-8 — "first order, in quadrature" through `max_r` and `min_c'` is undefined at kinks and the design names no rule for them
SUSPECTED (analytic; confirm by computing the band at the sweep's switch points once any noise exists). Severity: design. Effort: small (one sentence of rule). Blast radius: the band (l.236-240), guard (b).
W(c) = max over kinds of a ratio whose denominator is a min over candidates. A first-order propagation needs a fixed argmax kind and a fixed argmin candidate; both switch exactly where decisions are close (smp20.out: under F the five one-member stops tie EXACTLY, 147,929 each; the sweep's switch points are where two kinds' losses cross). The cancellation rule written for prices ("a card both read with one coefficient cancels", l.237) does not carry over when W(default) and W(c*) are set by DIFFERENT kinds (smp20 hit 20: {X,x}'s worst under F, '/''s under weblog), where the same card enters with different, mass-weighted coefficients. The noise inputs do not exist yet (`mf_measure` has no noise field, l.262; kit rows carry `noise_e6` '-'), so the band is uncomputable today, which the design does say. Ask: state the rule (e.g. the band is the max over the near-tied argmax kinds / argmin candidates of the first-order band, or a Monte-Carlo over card noise), and add it to the pcrec-side python re-price so the control can reproduce it.

### PREMISE-10 (GOALS) — a minimax departure that is not Pareto against the default PREDICTS a Goal-2 loss; Goal 2 and the rule conflict unstated
CONFIRMED (smp20.out arithmetic). Severity: design. Effort: small (one rule sentence or an owner question). Blast radius: Goals 2 and 5, §The pick 3, §Staging batch gate.
Goal 2 (l.19-24): each row's movers read new/old <= 1 + floor "in every regime", else the row narrows. Minimax lowers the WORST loss, not every loss: a departure from default d to c* is predicted slower under every kind r where price_r(c*) > price_r(d). In the design's own sweep, '/' becomes minimax at hits 20/80 k<=2.60 while {X,x} stays cheaper under `log` (smp20.out hit 20: log {X,x} 128,384 vs '/' 385,409) — a departure the rule would take that predicts a 3x loss on log-shaped subjects. If a 588-style run includes such a subject the row narrows (Goal 2) after the rule chose it (Goal 5): the ranked goals catch it, but only by measurement after the fact. A rule that departs only when c* is no worse than d under EVERY kind beyond band (Pareto vs the incumbent) cannot predict a Goal-2 loss on a known kind and is also IIA-safe (PREMISE-7); minimax is weaker. Not a refutation of the ruling (D186 add. 2 chose minimax), but the design should say which predicted per-kind losses a minimax departure is ALLOWED to carry into Goal 2's bar (D186 add. 2: an owner-approved exception) so the conflict is decided before the run, not by it.

## General forms and unlocks (standing question)
- Pure plan face. General form: `plan(site facts, frame, art config{set, bundle, denies, policy, levers}) -> (plan, trace[, price])`, a total function with no art writes. Unlocks: (1) memoization keyed by a site digest (formhash's normalization is the precedent) — trigger: plan calls measured above a named share of compile time; (2) counterfactual asks by pcrec's architecture (kit site vs automaton rendering, roadmap A-PLAN end state) — trigger: whole-artifact pricing un-deferred, which also makes PREMISE-1's claim true; (3) golden plan-in tests with no C — trigger: unit 1d.
- Minimax over kinds. General form: a robust choice rule over a DECLARED kind registry (each kind: source, fidelity, noise model, role) with its axioms stated (IIA, Pareto). Unlocks: kinds added as data (held-out bundles, an adversarial UTF-8 kind for C5) without touching the rule — trigger: [BENCH-WITHDATA]'s held-out bundles or [SPAN-PICK-UTF8]; fidelity-filtered kinds (PREMISE-5).
- Noise in the band. General form: every number a pick reads carries a noise model (card: measured; bundle: block bootstrap; constant: ARGUED/UNMEASURED with a declared width). Unlock: MARGIN and HORS_MIN become band-carrying rows — trigger: stage 2's noise channel.
