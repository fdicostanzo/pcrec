# CRITIC simulate: spanarms2
## Findings
### SIM-1 — the guards' storage bound and which end a long guard keeps are unstated; the "keep the first 32" reading deletes a match at reach 0 (TEXT GAP)

CONFIRMED (in the model, both readings run). Severity: answer, through a text gap (a build that picks the wrong end is unsound; the right end is sound). Effort: one sentence in §2 "Pending guards" + one T4 cell. Blast radius: guards longer than `PCREC_MAX_REQ_RUN_SCAN` (32) positions, i.e. lookbehind/lookahead bodies (or stacked/nested items carried by CAT-EDGE and ITEM's "preceded by"/"followed by") longer than 32 bytes. Note: §2 "Pending guards" ("Each guard position is a cube, like a run position"), §5.1 ITEM, §5.2 CAT-EDGE; no rev-0 F-id (new).

The note stores `lg`/`rg` "like a run position" but never says a guard is bounded, nor that `lg` must keep its NEAR (rightmost, next-to-cursor) positions and `rg` its near (leftmost) ones. The run's own storage keeps the FIRST 32 for a head (`rn_app`) and the LAST 32 for a tail (`rn_pre`); a guard spelled with `rn_app` (ITEM's "preceded by the body's own `lg`", CAT-EDGE's merged leftover, TOP's `lg ++ head`) keeps `lg`'s FAR end and the stored positions are then read at the wrong offsets.

Repro (model `model.py`, `OPT['gcap']`; `long.py`): `[ac]{17}[bc]{17}(?<=(?<=ca{16})b{17}).` on `c a^16 b^17 d` (35 bytes). libpcre2 10.46: M 0 35 (anchored at 0), `MAXLOOKBEHIND` 17. The outer item's `lg` is `c a^16 b^17` (34 positions: ITEM's lookbehind row, the body's `lg` `ca{16}` preceded onto its tail `b^17`).
- reading NEAR (lg keeps its last 32, rg its first 32): run `[ac][ac]a^15b^15`@0, holds. Sound.
- reading FIRST (`rn_app`-style, keep the first 32): `lg` stored `c a^16 b^15` read at [-32,0); CAT-L/CAT-EDGE meet it two positions off; run `[ac][ac]ca^14[bc][bc]b^13`@0 (252 bits, clears the floor) does NOT hold (subject byte 2 is `a`): the match (0,35) is DELETED at reach 0; the walk also states the dead fact (EMPTY-MEET), which [DESC-DEAD] would turn into a constant NOMATCH.
- unbounded (no cap): sound.
Neither reading is forced by the text; the deleting one is the natural spelling of "lg ++ head"/"preceded by" with the run's own append. Fix: §2 states the guard bound (`PCREC_MAX_REQ_RUN_SCAN` positions per side, or its own limits row) and that truncation keeps the positions adjacent to the cursor (lg = tail form, rg = head form); a T4 witness of > 32 guard positions.

### SIM-2 — EMPTY-MEET states the dead fact for a guard-vs-consumed empty meet but (on its literal reading) not for a guard-vs-guard one

CONFIRMED (model, both readings sound). Severity: doc (a census/U-dead undercount, no answer). Effort: one clause in the EMPTY-MEET row. Blast radius: L1's U-dead census ([DESC-DEAD]'s trigger count). Note: §5.2 row EMPTY-MEET; §10 U-dead. No rev-0 F-id.

The row's first sentence ("an empty intersection at a position proves the subtree cannot match: the walk states the dead fact") covers "any meet", but its second sentence gives the guard-vs-guard case its own outcome ("dropped with every position outward of it") and says nothing about the dead fact. Read literally: `(?=a)(?=b)c` IS stated dead (the `rg` `b` meets the consumed `c`), while `(?<=a)(?<=b)c` and `(?:(?=ab)a)(?=c)` are NOT (their contradiction is guard-vs-guard: `a`∩`b` at offset -1, `b`∩`c` at offset 0 after the group). libpcre2: 0 successful attempts for all three over every subject <= 5 over {a,b,c} and every p. Both readings are sound (a guard-guard empty meet proves death just as well); the literal one makes the U-dead census undercount the stacked-lookaround shapes. Fix: "... is dropped with every position outward of it, and the dead fact is stated as for any empty meet" (or say deliberately not, and why).
