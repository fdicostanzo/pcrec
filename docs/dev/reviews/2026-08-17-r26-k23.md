## Panel verdict in one paragraph

The DESIGN CORE HELD AND WAS STRENGTHENED: MRL's soundness argument is
PREFERENCE-BLIND (minrest bounds whether an accepting continuation
EXISTS — a language property, order-invariant — so deleting a subtree
with no accepting leaf cannot move the first accepting leaf under any
preference; the engine critic set out to break this and instead proved
it on the lazy-outer exemplar: 10,621,635 → 0 steps, captures identical
to python), and the closed-form step law is exact OUT OF SAMPLE (three
new instances, diff 0). What fell is the EMITTED FORM and the EVIDENCE:
§4.1's clamp is unsound on stride>1 cursor rungs (E1, measured, 5/8
subjects wrong on a live-population shape), the 855-cell differential
was structurally blind to exactly that (E2/E9 — single-byte-alphabet
corpora, the note's own §11.1 lesson unapplied to its own generator),
and the forward-work numbers feeding ruling request 5 are an unarchived
lane proxy, not D49's meter (E6 ≡ M2). This is the R25 shape again:
mechanism verified, form corrected, numbers re-anchored.

## BLOCKERS

**E1 — §4.1's clamp is UNSOUND on any cursor rung with stride > 1**
(companion E9 sizes it; §4.5 explicitly extends the clamp to those
rungs). The clamp `rx_cur = n − MINREST` lands the cursor OFF the
iteration lattice: a stride-W span loop admits only pos, pos+W, …, and
an off-lattice cursor poisons the whole retreat chain — the correct
cursor value is DELETED from the choice set, violating §4.2's own
soundness step 3 ("never introduces a candidate that was not there";
the unrounded clamp introduces one). MEASURED on
`((?:ab){10,20}){10,50}` — same rung structure and the IDENTICAL
baseline step count (10,621,636) as the note's exemplar, so it is in
K23's live population: subjects `ab`×100+`z`, +`a`, +`zzz`,
`ab`×101+`a`, `ab`×105+`z` all return **nomatch** under the §4.1 clamp
where baseline and python match (5 of 8 rows wrong; `z`+`ab`×100 agrees
only by residue accident). E9's sweep: 12 stride>1 shapes, EVERY one
wrong somewhere, 80 of 192 cells. THE FIX IS VERIFIED CHEAP ARITHMETIC:
round down onto the cursor's lattice,
`cap = pos + W·⌊(n − MINREST − pos)/W⌋` (cap < pos ⇒ fail) — restores
every row, step count stays 1. The rule appears NOWHERE in the note.
DISPOSITION: lane adds the lattice-rounding rule to §4.1/§4.3/§4.5,
re-derives §4.2 step 3 over it, and re-runs the differential over a
corpus with stride AND residue axes (E2). Nothing else is adoptable
until that number exists.

**E2/E9 — the differential was structurally blind to E1** — both

rung was ever exercised; a corpus without a residue axis cannot see a
parity bug. DISPOSITION: with E1 — generator gains stride (multi-byte
bodies incl. `(?:ab)`, classes with multi-byte spellings) and residue
(subject length mod stride, prefix offsets) axes; re-run; report cells.

**M2 — §4.6's forward-work numbers (55,684,363 → 190 → 109) and the

column is the lane's own loop-iteration proxy, NOT D49's RX_ERR_WORK
(which had no emitted charge sites when the lane measured — the

either commits a probe that actually counts (now possible — the
