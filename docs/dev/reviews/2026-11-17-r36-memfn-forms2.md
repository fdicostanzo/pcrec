## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **Simulate lens, HETEROGENEOUS NOISE.** A pooled-floor acceptance drill must vary the noise per regime/layer and
   between the control (A/A) cells and the arms. A drill that generates one sigma for everything proves only that
   premise (r36 SIMULATE-1: 1-3 % on homogeneous noise, 31-37 % with one regime 3x).
2. **Simulate lens, MEDIAN-MOVING SPLIT.** A bimodality gate's positive control must use a split that moves the
   statistic the rule reads (3 of 5 launches) at sizes from f to 2f. A 2-of-5 split proves only that the gate can
   fire (r36 SIMULATE-2).
3. **Fix-induced lens, RAISED RANGE KILLS AN EQUALITY.** When a fix raises a test's range past a constant (G2W_REACH
   64 -> 256), every check that read "nothing reaches the constant" as an equality dies. List each, and replace it
   with a pin AT the constant (r36 G1: the shipped head became unpinned).
4. **Check-adequacy lens, PLACEMENT.** A check added to one step of a multi-step procedure must be able to fail on
   THAT step's population. Plant its defect and confirm it reds on the step where it runs (r36 G7: the deny check
   ran on the one step where an ignored deny cannot loop).
