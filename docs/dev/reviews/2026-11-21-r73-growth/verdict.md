### VERDICT-6 — library stage: the control-matcher check passes a matcher that CRASHES or GIVES UP on the non-matching subject
CONFIRMED by reading (logic; not reproduced, a corrupted control is not constructible without editing pcrec). DIRECTION library-vs-process: "must still compile a control pattern correctly at the end". run_growth.py:324 fails only when `ok.returncode != 0 or nok.returncode == 0`; cli.md §1.10¶1 gives the emitted main()'s codes as 0 match, 1 no-match, 2 usage, 3 give-up, and a signal is a negative returncode in subprocess. Fix: `nok.returncode != 1`.
Severity: answer (narrow). Effort: trivial. Site: run_growth.py:322-326.
