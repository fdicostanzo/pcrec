## Family and general form (contract lens's standing questions)

- **Family:** `rr_alt_rows` is the 4th `{row, arm}` first-match restore table in req.c (`rn_choice_rows`, `rb_rep_rows`,
  `rb_zero_rows`). They use three scan idioms: sizeof-bounded, `last`-bounded and unbounded (two of them). Proposal: one bounded
  scan helper that enforces an arm-0 catch-all; this subsumes CONTRACT-1. The arm population also wants one source
  (CONTRACT-4). Trigger: it is cheap now, and due at the latest with the L1 `look` arm.
- **General form + unlocks:** treat an alternation's value as a set of per-WIDTH runs (the `req_pieces` domain already lists
  `6:[..]|7:[..]`); rule (1) is the case of a single width class. Unlocks: unequal-width alternations keep head and tail across
  a bounded gap (a gapped pre-check), and each table row gets a generated "row not restored" sabotage row instead of a
  hand-written one (S1921/S1930/S3131). Trigger: a mover census showing unequal-width alternations losing a run below
  `PCREC_MIN_REQ_RUN_BITS`, or losing a rarer byte, on the corpus or bench.

## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

1. **check adequacy, reach that echoes its own request.** PLANTS-1: a SAB_REACH that prints a parameter the probe was GIVEN
   (a requested site count) passes on the planted build too. Proposed lens text: "for every reach, ask what it prints on the
   PLANTED build; a reach that prints a requested parameter or a constant proves nothing."
2. **Arms named beside a witness file are a population.** FLOORS-1 and CONTRACT-4: lists of arms (a witness header's
   `# arms:`, the axes INTERNAL_ARMS) are populations nobody counts. Proposed lens text: "per declared arm list, show one block
   or row each arm MOVES, or the list's own statement that the arm is an inert control."
