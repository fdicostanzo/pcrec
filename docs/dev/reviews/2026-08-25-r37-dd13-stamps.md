## What the panel changes going forward

and the hybrid's stamps, one abi event, coordinated with srTier's abi 5.
The check-design lesson, third instance today: a control that only
floors a population answers "did it vanish", never "is it the right
one" — an exact manifest is the general form wherever the set is small
and named.
