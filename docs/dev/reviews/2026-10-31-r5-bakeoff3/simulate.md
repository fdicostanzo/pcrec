### SIM-2 The "1.5x" large-loss bar is read on the regret scale but argued as a ratio to the site's best; the tail order flips between the two, and `member_big_loss`'s (1 + r_u)/(1 + r_v) is on neither scale
- status: CONFIRMED
- severity: answer
- effort: small
- blast radius: TAIL-WIN can crown the member with MORE sites slower than 1.5x their best (PR O1's bar, F13's words "more than 1.5x slower than its best"); row 6b (TIE WITH NAMED LOSSES, "differ by more than 1.5x") fires on pairs that differ by less; `fm_big_sites` blocks row 1 on FM sites that are under 1.5x.
- where: §0 Words "regret" (r = m_pick − m_so, m = mean over cells of t/t_cell-oracle, so m_so ≥ 1 and m_so − 1 is the compile-time choice loss); §7.5 statistic (`L_u` = regret − floor > `big_loss_bp`, "1.5x outside the noise band"); TSV `param big_loss_bp` ("above 1.5x", PR O1), `pred member_big_loss`, `pred fm_big_sites`; §7.1 reported worst case ("sites above 1.5x their site oracle"); X2.
- finding: regret r > 0.5 means the pick is 1.5x its site oracle only when m_so = 1, i.e. only where the site has no compile-time choice loss. The true ratio is (m_so + r)/m_so, so wherever m_so > 1 the indicator fires below 1.5x (r = 0.52 at m_so = 1.25 is 1.416x). `member_big_loss`'s (1 + r_u)/(1 + r_v) is the true ratio (m_so + r_u)/(m_so + r_v) only at m_so = 1 and always overstates it otherwise. X2 unified the PAIR scale; this threshold is still argued on the ratio scale and read on the regret scale.
- repro: `simulate/work/scale.py` → `scale.out`, 300 sites, half with m_so = 1.0 and half with m_so = 1.25 (built from per-site time ratios): A1 has 2 sites at 1.55x its best plus small spread losses; Dx has 12 sites at r = 0.52 (1.416x its best); equal means (2.23 vs 2.08 pt). Large losses: regret scale A1 2, Dx 12; ratio scale (PR O1) A1 2, Dx 0. Drill and my evaluator both read `('TAIL-WIN', 'A1')` [6t|6t]: the member with the 1.5x losses wins the tail over the member with none. Second case: a plain tie where Dx differs from A1 on one m_so = 1.25 site by 1.416x: (1 + r_u)/(1 + r_v) − 1 = 0.520 > 0.5, and both read `TIE-WITH-LOSSES` [6b] instead of TIE [6].
- proposed fix: define the indicator on the ratio the bar is argued on, `(m_so + r_u)/m_so − 1 − NULL_i/m_so > big_loss_bp` (m_so is already in the archive as the compile-time choice loss), and `member_big_loss` as `(m_so + r_u)/(m_so + r_v)`; or relabel `big_loss_bp` as "0.5 points of regret" and drop "1.5x" and the PR O1 citation (F13 then asks Frank a different question). Add the m_so > 1 case to the drill.

### SIM-4 C-BLIND's beat bar mixes a per-cell TIME (NULL_c) with a ratio and is read per cell, not on the site's regret
- status: CONFIRMED
- severity: doc
- effort: small
- blast radius: a red C-BLIND (row 1 blocked) from a draw that is faster on one cell but not a better compile-time pick (higher site-mean regret); conservative (row 1 does not fire, T1 reads), so no wrong verdict, but the reach and false-red arithmetic of §8 assumes one comparison per draw.
- where: §8 "A beat ... faster than every model-chosen timed arm of its site by more than max(NULL_c, `blind_beat_bp` = 500, 5 %) on BOTH cross-fit halves"; TSV `param blind_beat_bp` ("max(NULL_c, 5 %)"); X2.
- finding: NULL_c is a cell's time (§5.4), 5 % and 500 bp are the same ratio stated twice, and the unit (a cell, or the site's mean over cells) is not named; every other statistic is on the per-site regret scale. If the comparison is per cell, a draw with one fast cell counts as a blind spot although no compile-time pick would choose it, and the false-red bound `1 − (1 − p0²)^(k n)` undercounts (one trial per CELL, not per draw).
- repro: read §8 and the TSV text: the max mixes a time with a ratio and names no unit; §7.1 says C-BLIND reads H-core "on the regret scale" by the section's opening sentence.
- proposed fix: state the beat on the site's regret: a draw beats when its regret against the model-chosen best is below −max(NULL_i, 5 %) on both halves; drop the duplicated 500 bp / 5 %.

### SIM-6 The tail step is gated behind all-EQUIV, but the large losses it looks for widen the mean's interval: on 130 H-core sites it can decide only at six or seven one-way 1.5x losses, and more losses read INCONCLUSIVE
- status: CONFIRMED
- severity: design
- effort: small
- blast radius: the more a member loses in the tail, the LESS the tail step can decide on H-core: 6 one-way 1.55x losses read TAIL-WIN; 8, 9 or 10 read INCONCLUSIVE ("needs about 134-171 sites"), so a tail question costs a frame the text never prices for it (§5.6 prices the frame only "if a pair is UNDERPOWERED", which here is CAUSED by the tail). §7.2's "on H-core alone it separates members only when one of them carries at least six large losses" and F13's "it is the six-places floor that decides" overstate it: on H-core the window is 6-7, and the edge is seed-dependent (my evaluator and the drill disagree at 7).
- where: §7.5 ("read only when every pair in G is EQUIV"); §7.2 "The tail step needs at least six"; §12 F13; TSV rows 6s/6t/6b (`G_all_equiv and ...`); X25 (d).
- finding: a member with k one-way 0.55 regret spikes and equal means has a paired difference whose 90 % bootstrap interval is about ±1.645 · 0.55 · sqrt(k)/n; at n = 130 that crosses ±δ near k = 7, so EQUIV fails exactly when the tail evidence grows.
- repro: `simulate/work/tail2.py` → `tail2.out`, 130 sites, A1 with k one-way 1.55x spikes, Dx with the same total spread evenly: k = 6 → drill `TAIL-WIN Dx` [6t]; k = 7 → drill 6t, mine row 7 (seed edge); k = 8 → `INCONCLUSIVE 'A1-Dx needs ~134 sites'`; k = 9 → ~168; k = 10 → ~171. Also `tail.out` T3 (7 vs 1), T4, T5, T6, T7, T8 at 130 sites: all INCONCLUSIVE, tail never read.
- proposed fix: state the window plainly in §7.2 and F13, and either (a) read the tail on an UNDERPOWERED pair too (a pair whose interval is wide only through units that are large losses may be TAIL-read: rows 6s/6t/6b gated on `G_all_equiv_or_tail_bound`), or (b) price the tail's frame in §5.6 and let the tail open it (see SIM-1).
