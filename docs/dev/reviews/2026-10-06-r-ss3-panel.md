## Lesson

An answer differential cannot see a pattern that no longer compiles; the
blocker was found by a COMPILE-ONLY fuzz (4 hits in 9,000). Any change that
adds a build-time assertion needs a "deny compiles ⇒ default compiles"
check over a generated population.
