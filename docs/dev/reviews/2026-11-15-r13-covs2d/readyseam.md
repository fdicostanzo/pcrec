### READYSEAM-1 — item 4's proof range `[MF_PROOF_LO, k + 1)` drops today's check past `k + 1` (C14 weakens for every short cover)
CONFIRMED (code read). Severity: answer (an independent control loses coverage). Effort: one sentence. Blast: every cover, k 1..31.
§11.1 item 4 (line ~740) changes `pcrec_cover_proof_diff` (known.c:649) from `[MF_PROOF_LO, MF_MAX_FWD)` to `[MF_PROOF_LO, k + 1)`. Today, for `k < 32`, offsets `k+1 .. 32` are compared against "nothing proven" (all ones, known.c:654); known.h:91 states C14 as "nothing elsewhere". A kit text that tests a byte at `k + 2` (an over-read, a widened load-compare) proves a set there, which today reads `over@<k+2>` and declines; under item 4 it is never looked at, and the cover silently skips matches. Repro of the reading: `foobar` (k 6) today checks d = 7..32 all-ones; item 4 checks none of them.
Fix: loop over the REPLY's counted range (whatever the kit declares, at least `k + 1`), require the reply's count >= `k + 1` (named internal error otherwise), and keep "all ones" for every `d > k`. "The reply's counted range" in the same bullet already says this; the formula `[MF_PROOF_LO, k + 1)` contradicts it.
