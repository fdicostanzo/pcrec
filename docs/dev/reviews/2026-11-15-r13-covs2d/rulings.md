### RULINGS-5 — The UNMEASURED 32 is the bound the owner named as borrowed, and the default rule's ceiling is the bench sample's edge, not a measurement (CONFIRMED by text; design; small; §11.2 "How the default is chosen", §11.6 Order (4)-(6))

- The rule caps the default at the longest TIMED mover ("Widths longer than the longest timed mover are unmeasured and so off by default"). The timed set (§11.4 A+B) tops out at `lit-l40` (40). The owner's go ("Go on all stages", "span up to 4095") covers the capability, so this is not a veto; it is the reason to add the long cell, so the default is measured where the capability is.
- Fix: make "time on the joint branch, land once with the measured default" the plan of record in §11.6 (fallback: land at 32 as written); add one or two synthetic long cells to §11.4 B so the rule's admissible range reaches past 40, or state plainly in §11.2 that the default cannot exceed 40 by rule and why that is acceptable.
