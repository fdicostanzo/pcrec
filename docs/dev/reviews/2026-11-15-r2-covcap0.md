## PROBED-AND-HELD (summary; per-critic counts in review/)
- **The cap is the only conjunct.** 146 probes on V1/V2: every 25-31 shape covers, 32 covers with C7, 33+ never covers. lit-l40, the uuid patterns and uuid-near-miss stay on the engine. The 4 predicted bench movers move in both encodings.
- **G1.** 0 mismatches on 2,000 subject runs vs python `re`.
- **G2 sample.** 46 patterns × 2 encodings. Only the predicted fixed-width bodies move. Variable-width long-prefix bodies move their listing only.
- **§2.3's deeper-walk claim holds, including the shared subset budget.** Breadth-first creation is depth-monotone, so a budget exhaustion at depth ≥ 48 only leaves deeper subsets unexpanded.
- **Every reader of `w->k` at k = 32 is in bounds.** This covers cover_window (len 32 = MF_WSPAN_POS), proof_diff (proven[40] of [41]) and pcrec_known_derive (which has no caller).
- **Kit bounds at 32 hold.** The proof range [-8, 33) covers the after-context.
- **No spec text states 24 for the cover or the slice.**
- **D90 row shape and the D174 name hold.**
