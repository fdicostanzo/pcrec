### MODECLOSURE-2 — join-order DOES move the reported stretch (SEARCHED / FIND-ALL) when an operand's first path is not its longest; and `&` does not distribute over `|` on its RIGHT side (CONFIRMED)
- Place: top level, SEARCHED and FIND-ALL; inside `atomic` it also moves WHICH STARTS HOLD. Draft: R.1.4 lines 486-491 ("It moves only WHICH LABELS a reported row carries, never whether a program holds or the stretch it reports"), line 499 ("`&` DISTRIBUTES over `|`", unqualified), §R.8 join-order row (line 726, "holds and stretches identical").
- E5 and D1 use operands whose PCRE-first paths are all greedy (PAB, PCD) and a CONDITION as the right operand of the distribution. With a lazy operand the claim fails (t3.py, engine NOW rules, DSP = 1,365 texts):
  - J1 `G & L` vs `L & G`, G := `/(?<a>\d+)/`, L := `/(?<c>\d+?)/`, stretch only (no labels): WHOLE 0; SEARCHED 1,008; FIND-ALL 1,197 differ (first `11`: left-outer reports `11`@0, right-outer `1`@0, and FIND-ALL [`11`] vs [`1`, `1`]). So `&` is not commutative in what a search reports, and the join-order reading is answer-moving, not a labels-only choice.
  - DI1 `X & (B | A)` vs `(X & B) | (X & A)`, X := `/\d+/`, B := `/\d/`, A := `/\d\d/`: SEARCHED 1,008 / FIND-ALL 1,197 differ (first `11`); DI2 `(B | A) & X` vs `(B & X) | (A & X)`: 0 / 0 / 0.   - J2b `atomic(G & L) & (c: "1")` left vs right outer: SEARCHED 660 / FIND-ALL 693 differ (first `11`: left `1`@1, right `1`@0): inside atomic the join order decides whether a match exists at a start.
- Consequence: join-order (a MANAGER'S READING) is offered to the owner as moving labels only (0 / 0 / 0 on holds and stretch, E5); the true stake is the reported stretch of every `&` of two multi-path patterns in a search, and holding inside atomic/lookahead. - Fix (doc + E5): restate join-order's effect with a lazy-operand example in all three modes and the atomic place; state "`(B | A) & X` = `(B & X) | (A & X)`" only (left distributivity), and that `X & (B | A)` reorders paths.
- Severity: answer (an open item presented with the wrong stake). Effort: small. Blast radius: R.1.4, §R.8 join-order, D1.

### MODECLOSURE-3 — "adding a condition never admits a text" and "holds = some path survives" are false inside `atomic` (and so inside a lookahead), a place R.1.3 does not list (CONFIRMED)
- Place: nested, inside `atomic(...)`; draft R.1.3 lines 478-481 (positive places listed: the top of the chain, a `|` branch, P; the exceptions listed: NOT, complement) and PART D D2; R.1.4 ATOMIC (line 509-515).
- Repro (t3.py, engine NOW rules, DIG4 = 341 texts): AD1 `atomic((B & (l: <20)) | A) & (l: <20)` over `atomic(B | A) & (l: <20)` (B := `/(?<l>\d\d)/`, A := `/(?<l>\d)\d/`; the added condition is at a POSITIVE place, inside a branch inside atomic): ADMITS on WHOLE 12, SEARCHED 156 of 341 (first `00`: `00` is not a canonical numeral, so B's l fails inside and atomic commits to A, l = `0`; and on `25`: without it FAILS, B8; with it the inner condition rejects B, atomic commits to A, l = `2`, HOLDS).
