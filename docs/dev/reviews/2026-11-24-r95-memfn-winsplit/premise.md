### PREMISE-1 (CONFIRMED; doc/design; effort S; blast: §2's premise SCC and its edge list) §2 "today" cycle includes two false edges: kitgraph reads locals as calls
§2 says at T the SCC is {finder, generic, mismatch, ofsskip, presence, window} with edges "mismatch.c -> window.c (`shape`), ofsskip.c -> window.c (`count`)" (CENSUS of names used in function bodies).
