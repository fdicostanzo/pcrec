## Lessons for the guide

1. A design that promises "no witness needs re-pointing when X moves" gets a drill of every row by KIND (verdict,
   facts, answer, mech REACH) at X's extreme admissible values: the `@D` mechanism covered verdict rows only, and two
   critics independently found facts and REACH rows pinned to a literal (FIND-2).
2. A "compile FAILS" criterion is checked against the compiler's fallback ladders first: pcrec's size-cap ladder
   turns most failures into re-routes (FIND-1).
3. A design's own reader-finding grep is RE-RUN by the readiness critic and its output diffed against the design's
   hit list: the subject's own grep printed S526, S3223 and three writers its lists dropped (FIND-4, -10).
