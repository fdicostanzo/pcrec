## Lessons for the guide (proposed)
1. **A fix that turns a restated predicate into a read of another slot must trace the read's
   cycle and its fixed points.** D3's cure (read NEXT, do not restate it) created SLOT-1: the
   row's own `whole` changes what NEXT selects. A slot read from a row that changes the read
   slot's inputs is a counterfactual read, and it must be named.
2. **"Unchanged in kind" claims about a self-check are checked by evaluating the check's
   condition, not its name.** SLOT-2: the cited inclusion was the failure condition.
3. **A route conjunct is the finisher, not the body shape.** A VM hybrid's prefilter body is
   CR_DFA; cover already learned this (C1 `finisher-dfa`); SLOT-4 re-learned it.
4. **A soundness lens needs a control that can fail for every rule it calls sound.** SOUND's
   three controls (EXCLUSION violated, non-overlapping stream, F1 on non-unique ML) are what
   make its 0-wrong count mean something; keep that shape.
