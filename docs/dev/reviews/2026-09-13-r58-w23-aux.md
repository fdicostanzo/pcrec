## Method notes (for learnings)

1. A forward grep finds SPELLINGS; the inverse method finds MECHANISMS;
   neither finds DISPOSITION TEXT — the residue class of this round.
   The complete sweep is all three: grep, inverse walk, and reading
   every grep hit against every disposition that names it (B1's coda).
2. A question deliberately sent to a panel OPEN must not ship as
   settled anywhere in the same revision (A10/A1) — contingent sites
   need naming in the open decision itself, so the ruling has a
   checklist.
3. Two independent critics converging on one repair from opposite
   directions (A1 from the normative-site contradiction, A2 from the
   fixture) is the strongest signal in the round — the repair (one
   general parameter) is adopted, not either local patch.
