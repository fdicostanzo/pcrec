# CRITIC modeclosure: predspec23

## Findings

### MODECLOSURE-1: R.1.1/R.1.3 put a `records` item and a `contains`/`occurrences` argument in the WHOLE-value mode; the role table and the block's own evidence put them elsewhere
CONFIRMED. Severity: design (answers move). Effort: small (one sentence in R.1.1 naming each nested place's mode, citing THE ROLE TABLE rows). Blast radius: R.1.1, R.1.3, R.4 ("in a filter role its text is that role's stretch"), R.4.1 (ii) "where it surprises", every nested use of `!`, NOT, text.none.
- §R.1.1 (rev23:409-410): "In a filter role (either side of `&`, a `label:` set, a `contains`/`occurrences` argument, a `records` item) the text is that role's STRETCH (rev 22 §11 THE ROLE TABLE: a filter role matches its stretch whole)"; §R.1.3 (rev23:427-428): "A WHOLE-VALUE program (a filter role; ...) has, on a text, at most the rows whose stretch is the whole text".
- But THE ROLE TABLE (rev23:3864-3865) makes a `records` item row 7: NOT a filter role, "its file text (searched, §7)"; and §11 (rev23:3849-3850) says the argument of `contains`/`occurrences` "lands in a hole of the body" (row 5, "a hole never opens one"), which §2.6 (rev23:2036-2049) reads as PCRE's parse of the gap body, not a whole match of the argument.
- §R's own evidence already uses the searched reading for the contains argument: D11 `t2 & contains(t2 & !q)` on `9:30` HOLDS, "its hit `9`" (R.11 rev23:997) — a sub-stretch, impossible under R.1.3's whole-value reading.
- Repro (probe1.py W3, engine imported read-only): `t2 & !q` on the text `9:30`: WHOLE FAILS, SEARCHED HOLDS `9`@0, find-all [`9`@0, `30`@2]. So a `records` item `t2 & !q` over a file `9:30` yields 0 records under R.1.1 (whole) and 2 records under row 7 (searched); `contains(t2 & !q)` on the stretch `9:30` is FALSE under R.1.1 and TRUE under D11/§2.6.
- The question's three insertion points therefore do not share one meaning: the definition block names FOUR nested places with one mode while the body gives them three (row 3 whole, row 5 hole/string set, row 7 searched). Fix: R.1.1 lists the nested places by ROLE TABLE row with the mode each has (label: whole; hole incl. contains/occurrences argument: string set inside the enclosing role's text; records item: searched over the file text), and R.4's "in a filter role" sentence says which.

### MODECLOSURE-2: the hits of `occurrences`/`contains` over a FILTERED argument have two definitions, and one is order-underdetermined (hit count and group questions move)
CONFIRMED (the divergence); the choice is the subject's to state. Severity: answer. Effort: small-medium (state which; if the string-set reading, say what order the set's strings take). Blast radius: every `occurrences(P & c)`, `count`/`occurs.*` over `hit`, `records`, §R.1.9's "as `records` and `occurrences` already work".
- §R.1.9 (rev23:506-508): later matches are "leftmost, non-overlapping find-all, as `records` and `occurrences` already work" (R22's retry: the left operand's preference order of ends). But §2.6 (rev23:2047-2049): "The hits are those of PCRE's OWN matching of the body", the argument spliced into a HOLE, and a hole holding a program with conditions is "its STRING SET" (§R.4.1, rev23:637-638). A string set has no preference order, so PCRE's parse of the body `(?:[\s\S]*?(?<hit>SET))*...` picks whichever member the set's (unstated) alternation order tries first.
- Repro (probe2/probe1 W4): argument `/(?<n>\d+)/ & (n: <100)`; on `1234`: R22 find-all hits [`12`, `34`]; the string-set body with the set's strings shortest-first gives [`1`,`2`,`3`,`4`], longest-first [`12`,`34`]. On `123456`: 3 hits vs 6. So `occurrences(r & (n: <100)) & occurs.at_least(4, ...)`/`count` answers are not fixed by the definition block in any mode (WHOLE, SEARCHED or nested).
- Fix: R.1 states the hit list of a filtered argument as R22's find-all within the enclosing stretch (what R.1.9 and D11 assume), and §2.6's "PCRE's own matching" is scoped to an unfiltered regex argument (where both agree).

### MODECLOSURE-3: "adding a condition never admits" is stated without its insertion point in §4.2, and is FALSE through find-all (records, occurrences hits) and inside NOT / complement / occurs.none
CONFIRMED. Severity: doc (the property, as stated in the body) / design (the find-all case is positive polarity and non-obvious). Effort: small (scope the sentence). Blast radius: §4.2 (rev23:2675), §R.5 A4 (rev23:697-701), any reader relying on monotonicity for optimisation.
- §4.2 (rev23:2675): "'Adding a condition never admits' holds in both modes (§R.5 A4)." — no insertion point. §R.5 A4 scopes it ("at the top of an `&` chain AND through a twice-used program") and checks holds/fails only.
- Through find-all (R.1.9's later matches; records; occurrences hits, positive polarity): probe1 W2: item `/(?<n>\d+)/` on `1234` reports [`1234`@0]; with `(n: <100)` ADDED it reports [`12`@0, `34`@2] — the row `34`@2 is ADMITTED (it was swallowed by the longer rejected match). On `123456`: 1 record -> 3. So `occurrences(r, min = 2)` on `1234` FAILS, `occurrences(r & (n: <100), min = 2)` HOLDS: adding a condition admits inside a nested argument, in positive polarity, without NOT.
- Inside NOT (legal since 23:48): `P & NOT c1` -> `P & NOT (c1 & c2)` admits by De Morgan; inside `!q`, `!contains(q)`, `occurs.none(c)`, `(?!...${q})` likewise (downward polarity). These are expected, but the unscoped §4.2 sentence claims "in both modes" with no polarity or insertion point.
- Fix: §4.2 copies A4's scope and adds "a condition added at a positive, non-counting position; find-all reporting (records, `occurrences` counts) is not monotone".

### MODECLOSURE-4: "a missing label never passes a test, negated or not" is false at the NESTED insertion point: a `label:` set turns an inner UNKNOWN into FALSE, so NOT over it admits an absent inner label
CONFIRMED. Severity: design (the property is a plain-words promise; the answers follow the definition but contradict the promise). Effort: small (state the boundary). Blast radius: R.1 PLAIN WORDS (rev23:403-404), R.1.5's 23:48 quote (rev23:466-467), §R.3's "THEY PART" claim (NOT c drops absent rows), §4.2 (rev23:2667-2668).
- R.1 (rev23:403-404): "A missing label never passes a test, negated or not". R.1.5: `(h: S)` is TRUE iff h's value is in S; when S is a program with conditions the inner three-valued answer is collapsed to membership (UNKNOWN -> not in the set), so the third value does not cross a `label:` boundary.
- Repro (probe1 W1; X = `/(?<x>[\d:]+)/`, q = `t2 & ((h, m): >= (9, 30))`): on `9` (inner m ABSENT): `X & NOT (x: q)` HOLDS / HOLDS `9`@0; `X & (x: !q)` HOLDS; `X & (x: t2 & NOT late)` FAILS; the flat `t2 & NOT late` FAILS / FAILS. So NOT applied at the outer level behaves like PATTERN DIFFERENCE for the inner label (keeps the absent row), the opposite of §R.3's NOT column.
- Same at the hole insertion point (a hole is a string set) and in route (i)'s lookahead (rows of its own search, filtered). One meaning per operator holds; the PROPERTY does not: it holds only where the absent label is a label OF THE ROW the NOT tests. Fix: the plain-words sentence and §R.3 say "a label of the row the test reads"; add the nested row to §R.3's table (NOT over a nested program = difference on the inner labels).

### MODECLOSURE-5: `|` of FILTERED operands (`(P & c) | (Q & d)`, `when`'s body) has no stated meaning in §R; the evidence implements an unstated fallback reading on which §R.5 A3 and §R.7b b4 rest
CONFIRMED. Severity: answer (A3's "agree" and b4's counts depend on it). Effort: small (one clause in R.1.4, marked the draft's reading, or a question). Blast radius: R.1.4 `|` bullet (rev23:436-438), R.5 A3 (rev23:701-704), R.7b b4 (rev23:778), §8 `when`.
- R.1.4: a row of `P | Q` is "the one parse PCRE gives that stretch, with the labels of the branch it took", and `(B | A) & (l: "a")` on `aa` REJECTS (no fallback when the filter is outside). It does not say whether a filter INSIDE a branch that rejects sends the parse to the next branch. The predspec23b engine (its header, lines 12, 50-51: "branch 1 first, the next branch when its filters reject") implements the fallback; grep finds no sentence in rev 23 stating it (only §R.10's "`|` of filtered patterns" naming the feature).
- Repro (probe2.py): `when(c, p)` body `(t2 & c & p) | (t2 & !(t2 & c))`, c = `(h: <5)`, WHOLE: on `7` the evidence's reading HOLDS; the one-parse reading without fallback (branch 1's regex t2 always parses, its filter rejects) FAILS; same on `12`. Under the no-fallback reading the else-branch is unreachable and `when(c, p)` = `x & c & p`, so A3's "body and case-when agree" (11,110 vs 11,110) would not hold.
- Fix: state the rule (and its relation to the outside-filter REJECT) in R.1.4 as the draft's reading, or list it in §R.8.

### MODECLOSURE-6: `(x: !B)` with B a PATTERN: R.1.4 says a complement with nothing on its left is an ERROR; §2.6 and R.1.11's set reading make it legal; the nested `label:` place is not assigned a left operand
CONFIRMED (text conflict); answers agree where it is legal. Severity: doc. Effort: small. Blast radius: R.1.4 (rev23:443-446), R.1.11 `!` row (rev23:531), §2.2 WHAT `!` TAKES (rev23:1907-1915), §2.6 (rev23:2045: "`$m !~ r` is `(m: !contains(r))`").
- R.1.4: "A complement with nothing on its left (`${!B}`, `C | !B`, a program that is only `!B`) is a compile ERROR"; R.1.4 also says `contains(r)` "is a pattern like any other". A `label:` set is a nested program (R.1.1), and `(m: !contains(r))` is that program "only `!B`". §2.2 lists `!contains(r)` under PATTERN and `(x: !(<10))` under SET; nothing says which reading a pattern-valued `label:` set takes or that `label:` supplies the left operand (the label's stretch).
- Probe1 W1 shows the set-complement reading and `NOT (x: B)` agree on a present x (`9`, `9:15` HOLD; `9:30` FAILS) — so the fix is wording: "after `label:` the label's stretch is the left operand; `(x: !B)` = `(x: !S_B)`, the set complement".

### MODECLOSURE-7: the evidence's `!contains`, `text.none` and route (i) never consider EMPTY rows, so the "text.none = !contains" equality and both routes are unrun for a nullable B (where §R.6's nullable-loop vacuity also bites them)
CONFIRMED (engine), no stated count moves (q is not nullable). Severity: maintenance (evidence) + doc (R.6 should list it). Effort: small. Blast radius: predspec23_model.py D5/D6 (lines in EX), predspec23b_model.py `q_rows` (`l in range(k + 1, ...)`), §R.4, §R.4.1, §R.6.
- R.1.2: the table is "every POTENTIAL MATCH", which includes empty stretches (the engine's own searched mode uses them: R.6's "empty stretch at 0"). The whole-text tests use `range(k + 1, ...)`, i.e. only non-empty B rows.
- Repro (probe1 W5): B = `/(?:(?<n>\d),)*/ & (n = 9: >=5)` (a "possibly none" default, the R.1.7 idiom). Its empty row survives at every position (zero passes read 9). By the definition text.none(B) is FALSE on every text (and `!contains(B)` too: the body finds the empty hit); the engine's form says TRUE on ``, `1,`, `a`. So the author's "no list of all-large values anywhere" is vacuously FALSE everywhere — the §R.6 nullable-loop class extends to text.none / `!contains` / `(?![\s\S]*${B})`, unlisted.
- Fix: R.6 gains the line; the engines include l = k (or assert B non-nullable).

### MODECLOSURE-8 (NOTE; SIBLING OF A FAMILY): the "not" trio and the "no B" trio are one family, each with an unstated member
SUSPECTED as a gap (a NOTE, not a defect). Severity: design. Effort: small to state.
- The "not" family is really FOUR members on two axes: WHAT is negated (a value set, a row test, a row set) x WHERE the third value goes. §R.3 has NOT c (row test, U stays U), `(h: !S)` (set, U stays U), `P & !(P & c)` (row set, U -> kept). The unstated fourth is MODECLOSURE-4's: NOT over a NESTED program `NOT (x: q)` (row test on the outer row whose inner U was already collapsed: inner U -> kept), which is a row test that behaves as the difference. §R.3 should carry it as a fourth column.
- The "no B" family: `!contains(B)` (the row's stretch), `(?![\s\S]*${B})` (from a position), text.none(B) (the text), and the unstated member `NOT text.some(B)` vs `text.none(B)` is trivial, but the missing one is the CORRELATED form: "no B whose labels relate to this row" (`text.none(B & (h: $h))`, SQL `NOT EXISTS (... where b.h = a.h)`). R.4's "uncorrelated subquery" names it by contrast; R.1.5's "reads only its row" forbids it; nothing says it is out of scope.

### MODECLOSURE-9 (NOTE; GENERAL FORM + UNLOCKS)
- GENERAL FORM the block implies: every place a program stands is (TEXT, MODE) where MODE in {whole, searched-first, searched-all, string-set}; a program denotes a ROW SET over its text; a condition is a three-valued row predicate; every nested place converts the inner row set to a two-valued fact about the outer row (EXISTS, membership, count). One table of places (THE ROLE TABLE extended with a "mode" and a "what crosses the boundary" column: rows/labels/truth) would state MODECLOSURE-1, -2, -4 and route (i)/(ii) at once.
- UNLOCKS: (a) whole-text becomes "a nested place whose text is the enclosing scoped match, mode exists" — route (ii) is then not a new kind but a row of the table; (b) correlated subqueries (the lookahead-labels question and `text.none(B & (h: $h))`) become one column ("may the nested program read the outer row?"); (c) find-all monotonicity (MODECLOSURE-3) is stated per row.
- TRIGGER: the owner unparking **whole-text**, or a second nested place gaining conditions (a positive lookahead's labels, a `records` item with NOT) — at that point the per-place prose drifts (it already has: MODECLOSURE-1).
