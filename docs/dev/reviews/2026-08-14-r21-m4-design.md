## Headline results

1. **A live tier-1 miscompile in shipped pcrec (E-1 → K17)**: the DFA
   loses leftmost-first priority for a lazy nullable prefix + nested
   nullable star + outer star (`(?:b*?(?:a*)*)*` on "ab": pcrec [0,2),
   PCRE2 AND python [0,1)). K1's residue. Found by running P-1's own
   requested probe. Recorded as known_issues.md K17 (61871c0);
   manager-reverified the oracle span live. Frank initially read pcrec as
   correct — that reading is leftmost-LONGEST (POSIX); the ruled standard
   (D3 leftmost-first + D26 PCRE2-exact match tier) and both oracles give
   [0,1).
2. **The design's empty-iteration guard is wrong for bounded repeats
   (E-2)**: 60/225,240 divergences vs libpcre2 as written; 0/225,240 with
   the guard restricted to rmax == -1. PCRE2 replicates bounded repeats.
3. **Allocation-free + D19's thread-stack budget caps capture matching at
   ~2 KB of subject (E-3)** for any pattern with a choice point or
   capture inside an unbounded quantifier — including `a(b|c)+d` under
   D42.1's captures-on default. The step budget binds three orders of
   magnitude too late to notice.
4. **The `ptrdiff_t[2]` span array-typedef converts the M4.5 capture
   change into a silent stack overflow (A-1)** — measured: unchanged
   [M4.4]-era caller recompiled against a bigger artifact smashes its
   stack with zero diagnostics; the struct spelling makes it a hard
   compile error. The post-M4.5 `_search` signature was never stated
   anywhere.
5. **The fixed-literal ABI types do not compile when two differently-
   prefixed generated headers meet in one TU (A-2)** — the composition
   glue case they exist for — because include guards derive from the
   prefix. Same hazard emit_dfa.c:92-96 already documents for rx_span.
6. **The oracles never disagreed (E, ASK-1 refuted)**: python vs PCRE2 =
   0 disagreements across 225,240 pairs + 53 targeted empty-iteration
   cases. Every real disagreement was pcrec-or-design vs BOTH oracles.
   The planned oracle-exclusion mechanism would have HIDDEN K17;
   replaced by a three-way rule (any 2-1 split with pcrec in the
   minority is a bug, never an exclusion).

The attacked-and-held record is as load-bearing as the findings: the
erasure argument, the write/undo trail discipline (exact-old-value,
per-slot — 225,240/225,240 agreement once E-2 is fixed), the §2.2 VM
sketch's logic, the §4.7 cliff guard (measured on 8 MB blowup patterns),
the C-typedef-scoping claim for its stated two-TU case, the three-count
design under --no-captures, and all eleven structural code citations in
engine_m4.md all SURVIVED deliberate attack. The claim-marking scheme
worked: what broke was almost exclusively what was marked BELIEVED.

## Reflection

- The panel paid for itself twice over: a live shipped miscompile (E-1)
  no corpus/fuzzer/critic-with-source had found — found by the blind spot
  the design itself named (P-1's probe) — and two measured ABI landmines
  (A-1, A-2) in the exact features the rulings were proudest of.
- The check-design lesson recurred in a new costume AGAIN: the planned
  oracle-EXCLUSION mechanism (built to protect expectations from oracle
  noise) would have suppressed the one real bug, because the noise it
  guarded against measured zero. Instruments must be pointed at pcrec
  first, oracles second.
- Claim-marking earned its keep measurably: every broken claim was
  BELIEVED or a PROPOSED spelling; every STRUCTURAL citation checked
  clean (11/11), and the one STRUCTURAL that fell (§6.1) fell exactly at
  the seam where its mark was argued half-and-half in the doc itself.
- Three-critic lens separation produced zero duplicated findings and two
  independent convergences (match-here-has-no-caps-output; F8
  staleness), both of which had already been converged on once before by
  the two design lanes — four independent derivations of the same two
  gaps is strong evidence the gaps were real, and none of the four was
  the panel's discovery alone.
