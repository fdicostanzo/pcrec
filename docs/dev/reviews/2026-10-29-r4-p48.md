## Lessons for the guide (proposed)

- **"The N sites" is a claim to trace, not a list to edit.** For every site a rule names, grep the predicate's OTHER readers. Here a helper shared by the entry dispatch and the prefilter placement would have re-coupled two questions a prior step had split. All three lenses found it independently, which says the hazard is easy to miss in a design and easy to find in review.
- **A sabotage pair is two-sided only if both halves have a detector.** Name, for each plant, the check that sees it. An answer-identical plant needs a structural check.
- **A priced row that is "transparent today" has a verdict when its price is missing.** Write that verdict in the row, because two rows' fall-throughs can mean opposite things (F10).
