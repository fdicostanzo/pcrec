The prior-cost family: what goes into each decision today

Report for Frank · lane pcureport · 2026-10-11 · read at main e7a69b78 (abi 75). Sources: docs/design/prior_cost_unify.md rev 2 and its derived census; every formula below re-read in the code it cites. Where the note and the code disagree, the code is reported. No advocacy for or against the unification: this describes what ships.

The five plainest findings

  1. The three "VERDICT" members ask three different questions in three different units. #4 compares two expected jump lengths in bytes and has no hand-set constant. #3 compares a mean gap in bytes against a crossover measured on a Mac, and only picks the starting state of a retry arm that runs either way. #7 is the only cost model: hundredths of a cycle per byte, five cycle constants (three from Zen 1, two with no measurement record) and a 2× bar. No cost constant (a cycle price or a crossover) is read by two members; the only shared hand-set numbers are the prior's 2 ppm floor and the 16-bit run-admission floor, which gate inputs rather than price them.
  2. The real overlaps are small and specific. #3 and #4 both reduce to "is the mean distance between scan stops, 10⁶/ppm bytes, shorter than some byte distance?". The six PICKs already share one primitive and one NONE rule. #7's cycle model and G1's NONE = false do not fit either group.
  3. The adapters preserve quirks of very different weight. A2 preserves a case with zero corpus reach (0 of 181 handoff artifacts). A5's "no scan means 10⁶" branch produces 16 of the 35 corpus adaptive-dense answers: almost half of R4's dense verdicts never read the prior. A1 pairs a documented UP rounding (#4) with an undocumented integer truncation (#7). A4 is a rule, not an adapter.
  4. Two of #7's constants have no measurement record. Rev 2 §3.2 labels C_VERIFY (2.5 c) and C_MISPRED (15 c) "MEASURED, Zen 1"; offset_k_skip.md §4.2 gives a description and no source for either. C_ENTER is half assumed, and C_MEMCHR ships as 0.06 c/B against a measured 0.055.
  5. The unified cost cannot be evaluated today. Its new term, stop_reentry, is unmeasured on every box. The constants that do exist come from two boxes in two units (Zen 1 cycles, Mac ns), and rev 2's own same-box rule forbids combining them.

4. Assessment: same question, or only alike?

Genuinely the same question, same units

  • The six PICKs (#2, #13, #14, #15a, #15b, #21). Each is an argmin (or a sort) of byte masses in ppm, through pcrec_find_pick / cube_mass / seq_mass, with one tie and NONE rule (rightmost, or leftmost for the window). They are already one mechanism; there is nothing left to unify. Evidence: src/core/findings.c:421-611.
  • #3 and #4, as advance comparisons. R4 asks "is 10⁶/mass(S) < gap?" and the K admission asks "is 10⁶/h − K > 10⁶/m?". Both are distances in subject bytes derived from stop rates. A shared, exact advance comparison (rev 2's cross-multiplied adv) would serve both without changing either formula. Rev 2's own grid shows #3's rational form is exact (0 of 1,332 cells differ).

Only alike: different questions or units

  • #7 against #3 and #4. #7 prices cycles per byte with five constants and adopts only at a 2× margin. #3 and #4 have no cycle term at all. Putting #3 and #4 on #7's cost scale means giving them constants they do not use today (rev 2's stop_reentry, step_byte), none of which is measured. That is a new model, not a unification of the existing ones.
  • #3's outcome is not pay-or-decline. adaptive-dense and adaptive run the same arm (CAND_RS_A_ADAPT); the decision only sets where it starts (emit_dfa.c:9078-9092). A cost VERDICT answering PAYS/DECLINE is a different shape of answer.
  • G1 (#1) against the VERDICTs. G1's NONE answer is false (keep the pre-check), while every VERDICT prices NONE by cardinality. Rev 2 F-2 itself says a single NONE answer would move G1 under -e utf8.
  • The rev 2 primitive's NONE against today's members. Rev 2 defines "no scan" as zero stops. Today A2 and A5 treat it as 10⁶ stops, and rev 2 must add adapters to undo its own definition at both sites.
  • The constants' boxes. #7's constants are Zen 1 cycles (2026-08-26) or have no record; #3's are Mac M1 nanosecond crossovers (2026-09-29/30). Rev 2's Q3 (settled) allows only same-box ratios, so no shipped constant pair can be combined.
