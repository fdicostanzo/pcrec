## O-79 — [B117]: the compilee's own `-O` level

Not a bug report, no ask. 62 `capability@0.1` patterns, `pcrec-auto` and
forced `pcrec-vm`, `-O0/-O1/-O3/-Os` against the `-O2` default.

- `auto` routes 29 DFA / 22 VM-hybrid / 11 VM-pure, identical at every `-O`.
- DFA subset at `-O0`: 24 of 29 >15% slower, median x2.24, worst
  `utf8-lead-no-cont` x6.72. At `-Os`: median x1.25, worst
  `wild-validator-email-owasp` x3.41. `-O1` x1.04-x1.16 median. `-O3` flat
  (VM-hybrid 3-6% faster).
- Forced VM at `-O0`: 48 of 62 >15% slower (median x3.10, worst x9.20).
  The 14 flat cells all have an `RX_REQ_BYTE` guard byte that occurs zero
  times in the throughput subjects, so the pre-check answers no-match
  before the VM loop runs.
- `.so` size at `-O0`: median x1.13-x1.14.
- Disposition: filed `[GUIDE-OPT-LEVEL]` (one guide sentence, no numbers
  restated, D80). `utf8-lead-no-cont` is a recurring extreme witness; no
  pcrec action.

## O-80 — K75 / I-123: no ill-formed `-e utf8` cell exists

- Only `bench/utf8@0.1` declares utf8; its 98 subjects verified
  well-formed three ways; invalid-UTF is parked for `@0.3`.
- Other sets carry non-UTF-8 bytes but run byte-mode, so `--utf8` never
  reaches a driver there. The harness does not refuse a `-e utf8` testee
  on such a set; no committed window ever paired them.
- Disposition: no bench alignment needed (`pos = end` stays). Annotated
  `known_issues.md` K75; the cross-pairing hazard is noted, not pursued.

  x31-x200 (asr-lb-varwidth x200, asr-lb-fixed x87, asr-lb-neg x31;

  gcc synth-dense 7.4%, asr-lb-fixed clang synth-1m 6.7%, asr-lb-fixed
  clang synth-64k-asc 6.5%. Syntax: 10 of 27 adaptive cells >5% slower

  x1.0770; lka-pos ss x1.0949 / mc x1.0897; lka-neg ss x1.0874;
  grp-atomic-alt ss x1.0640 / thr x1.0536; lka-nonatomic ss x1.0566).
  Capability: logparse-atomic thr flat (anchored), ss x1.0690; the six
  `clamped` cells unmoved (the 1c prediction confirmed).
- Item 2: lka-pos auto is slower than forced VM at the two smaller
  throughput subjects, faster at the largest; lka-verb never inverts; no
  density-controlled pair exists (3/6/27 matches).
- Answers never moved; the only nonzero `n_wrong` (asr-k-uc, rec-r-uc,
  5 of 42) is identical across configs and pre-existing.
- Disposition: `[OPT-HYB-RESEED-XCALL]`'s D77 trigger is met on the bench
