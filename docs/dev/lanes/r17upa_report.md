# Lane r17upa: uses per activation of R-17's set-FIND sites (memfn kit manager)

2026-10-12. MEASUREMENT lane, counts only, no timing. Branch `lane/r17upa`, cut
from `lane/memfn-r17` 0409c204. The box was used lightly (`taskset -c 12-15`, 4
jobs, a ~3-minute run). The default build and `src/` are untouched.

## Delivered

- **Instrument:** `docs/design/memfn/probes/r17/upa/` (`upa.py`, `upa_driver.c`,
  `summarize_upa.py`, `cells_r17.txt`, own CLAUDE.md). It post-processes a copy
  of the emitted C, putting a counter at every PF/hat table walk and an
  activation at the holding function's entry. It drives the copy exactly as the
  bench's `timed.c` does: search-short = one `rx_search(s, n, 0)`; find-all =
  match_api.md §3.1's loop with the utf8 advance.
- **Data:** `upa/out/` (`counts.tsv.gz`, `sites.tsv`, `controls.tsv`,
  `planted_controls.tsv`, `tables.md`, `provenance.txt`).
- **Reading:** `docs/design/memfn/probes/r17/reading_upa.md`, with the per-cell
  tables, the bands against B_EVEN in (1, 4), the dense corner, the hybrids, a
  summary paragraph for the bench (§7) and the standing questions.
- **Subjects:** the bench's own, regenerated by its generators into
  `build/upa/benchsrc` (a scratch `git archive` of bench 1cb906f6). All 8 sets'
  manifests reproduced byte for byte. Nothing was written to the bench tree.

## Validation (complete)

- `upa.py` over the whole bench, minus `sentinel` (a copy of capability):
  `UPA COMPLETE jobs=704 violations=0 oracle_diff=18`.
  - Population: 270 site-bearing cell × config pairs, 387 with no site, 47
    refused by pcrec.
  - The answers are identical, plain vs instrumented, on every subject.
  - The 18 oracle differences are all `syntax/rec-{1,name,r-uc}` find-all × 2
    configs. They are pcrec give-ups (`giveup=-3`), identical in the plain
    build, a known give-up cell.
  - Monotone, hit-bound and activation-bound controls: 0 violations.
- Planted control (`--plant`, the 11 named cells):
  `jobs=11 violations=11 oracle_diff=85`. Every cell fires.

## Headline

Search-short is BELOW the break-even (median 1 use per call, 0 behind a
pre-check) on every R-17 named cell. In find-all, `adl` (median 31-20,280 per
call) and `alt2`/`alt2r` (6) are ABOVE. The other named cells take 1 use per
call, but at 0-57k ppm, so they fail §9.2's density half. The state-lost dense
corner (≥ 300k ppm, median < 4) holds 138 rows over 46 small-set cells, and it is
the dense controls (`[0-9]`/`[a-z]` on the all-digit/all-letter subjects, and
`\w`/`.`/wide classes on dense text), not the named cells. Most VM hybrids take
1 use per prefilter call. `loglines/level-context` (31) and
`wild-logparse-syslogbase-expanded` (7,669) carry state.
