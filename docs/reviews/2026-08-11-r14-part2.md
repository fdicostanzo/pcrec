## C2 — coherence and contracts (17 findings: 2 CRITICAL, 11 MAJOR, 4 MINOR)

### C2/F1 — CRITICAL — §16.3's two bullets require opposite things

The SHAPE column is deleted (bullet 1: "the caller evaluates first and
inspects the RESULT's shape... dissolves with the column") and then a shape is
demanded for a producer that never runs (bullet 2: `[0-\d]` with `classes`
disabled — "a VERDICT for a real, successfully-recognised construct carries
its shape"). With the producer gated there is no RESULT to read a tag off; the
only source is static row data — the column bullet 1 dissolved. The escape
hatch ("recognised") makes shape an output of the recogniser, which is the
shape column relocated, and §4.4's 60-unread-values objection returns
unanswered. Also orphans §5.3, which specifies the shared default handler in
terms of "the row's shape column" and is not in §17.1's superseded list.
*Repair:* state RECOGNISE returns `(claim, shape)`, say it is the column
relocated, answer the unread-values objection for rows that cannot reach an
endpoint.

### C2/F2 — CRITICAL — the "two-byte lexical test" over-rejects four pinned
accept-controls

Measured: `[0-[a]`, `[0-[:]`, `[0-[:digit]`, `[0-[.]`, `[0-[=]` all COMPILE in
libpcre2; a two-byte test on `[:` `[.` `[=` after `-` returns 150 for four of
them. The predicate §16.3 deletes (`pcrec_ext_class_pair_opens`,
`ext.c:353-372`) is exactly the scan that gets this right — it walks to the
matching close delimiter with escape handling; its own header says "the
boundary is exactly this scan rather than 'the byte is `[`'". D33 §9.1 makes
those four mandatory migration controls. *Repair:* the deviating cell is
implemented by the surviving pair-opens scan at one call site; strike it from
§9's deletion list.

### C2/F3 — MAJOR — §5.6's longjmp carve-out now points the wrong way

§16.3 needs the NON-producing case returned (the disabled `\d`'s VERDICT must
reach the endpoint caller to become 150), but §5.6 — not listed as superseded
— still allows `EXT_TERMINATED` to leave by `ctx_fail`. D33 §5 named this the
piece to attack. Two implementers diverge: build the
diagnostic-outlives-the-handler mechanism across the 23 `ctx_fail` sites, or
keep the longjmp and silently lose every endpoint override for disabled
features. *Repair:* add §5.6 to §17.1's superseded list — every terminal
answer is returned under WANT_VERDICT; `ctx_fail` survives only under
WANT_RESULT.

### C2/F4 — MAJOR — the count-scan must ACT on `(?x)`/`(?n)`, which §13.3
assigns to the gated producer

`(?n)` and `(?x)` are not extents — they change what counts. New cell
measured: `\2(?x)#(a)\n(b)` err 115 vs `\2#(a)\n(b)` COMPILES. With
`modifiers` disabled the count-scan must apply `(?x)` comment semantics —
"acting", which §13.3 gates. Either count-affecting semantics are always live
by name, or `backrefs` cannot land alone. *Repair:* split the axis in three —
recognise / affect-the-count / produce-a-node — middle one always live,
members listed: `(?x)`, `(?n)`, `(?|`, `\Q…\E`.

### C2/F5 — MAJOR — disposition is per-ROW; the verb doorway has ONE row for
fifty names spanning both dispositions

`RK_VERB` is one `RegRow`; the fifty `VerbName`s carry no status/feature at
all, and are not uniform: `(*COMMIT)` is NEVER, `(*pla:…)`/`(*atomic:…)` are
the lookaround/atomic constructs in verb spelling. K14's own repro set is not
separated by K14's fix. Second gap: for an `RD_NEVER` row whose feature IS
enabled, the §15 gate never fires — nothing stops `(*COMMIT)` at WANT_RESULT.
*Repair:* `disposition` on `VerbName` too, row value as default; one sentence
in §15: RD_NEVER demotes `want` independently of the enabled set.

### C2/F6 — MAJOR — `RS_NOT_OFFERED(option)` is a roadmap fact on the
PCRE2-facts axis — the exact category error K14 names, inside K14's fix

"Real under a PCRE2 option pcrec does not implement" — the operative clause is
roadmap. K14 said both of §7.2's options were category errors; D34 ruling 1
creates the disposition axis because of that finding and keeps the offending
value on axis 1. §7's framing was refuted (the mode-dependent set reaches base
grammar), and §17.2 ships §7's vocabulary anyway. *Repair:* split it — the
PCRE2 fact is "real under option X" (status), pcrec's refusal to offer X is
disposition — or mark it deferred to the bound-mode document and drop it from
§17.2 until then.

### C2/F7 — MAJOR — status × disposition has no legality rules, and `RD_*`
already means something else in shipped code

Illegal/vacuous cells unstated (RS_BASE × either; RS_REJECTED × RD_PLANNED;
RS_REJECTED × RD_NEVER indistinguishable from RS_MODULE × RD_NEVER). `RD_*`
is the shipped diagnostic-disposition enum (`internal.h:263`), cited by
§5.3/§5.5 as the message source; the verb row would be `RD_MODULE` (promises
a module) and roadmap-NEVER (must not promise) with nothing arbitrating, and
§5.5's vocabulary has no entry for "real, refused, names no module".
*Repair:* disjoint names (`ROADMAP_*`), state the illegal cells, add the
missing diagnostic disposition.

### C2/F8 — MAJOR — "either direction, but one source" is a dodge, and one
direction destroys the control that found K14

Generating the compliance prose from the column makes the two homes agree by
construction — retiring the only instrument that has caught this defect class
(a control sharing a source with what it controls). The repo already ruled:
"the inventory is generated and the analysis is not"
(`compliance_section.py:1-16`); the OUT-OF-SCOPE verdicts are analysis.
*Repair:* choose the checked direction: prose stays the human oracle;
`--names` grows a pass asserting prose-OUT-OF-SCOPE ⇔ RD_NEVER.

### C2/F9 — MAJOR — the check-3 successor names two incompatible oracles and
stores a fact whose home is another row

"Through the real dispatch" is self-referential (drift detection only — K13
was the CODE being wrong); "against libpcre2" cannot observe
`claimed-by-me`/`claimed-by-row-R` at all. `claimed-by-row-R` is the K10
two-homes shape — the fact belongs to row R's recogniser. And the vocabulary
is not total: `\Q`/`\E` have measured class behaviour, no port — §17.3
invariants 3 and 4 contradict on three rows. *Repair:* two-valued,
libpcre2-observable column ("what does `[<syntax>]` do — compile-as-what or
error N"), drop claimed-by-row-R, exempt lexical rows in invariant 4's text.

### C2/F10 — MAJOR — LEXICAL rows need recognisers; Part II puts recognition
inside ports and gives lexical rows no ports

§12's always-live rule and §14.1's arbitration are stated over PORTS; §17.3
invariant 3 says lexical rows have none. Reading 1: degenerate recogniser-only
port (invariant 3 false as written; §15 gate undefined for them). Reading 2:
the lexer pre-empts the doorway (dead `sel`/`rank`, check-1 not total, missed
gate falls to `ext.c:84`'s generic wording and breaks byte identity).
*Repair:* reading 1 — "lexical rows have a recogniser and no producer port";
give §15's gate a lexical-row case.

### C2/F11 — MAJOR — §17.1's divergence list omits the K13 fix

Literal-fallback ports are base scalars, never gated: `[\8]`, `[\12]`, `[\k]`,
`[\g]`, `[0-\k]` go from refusal to compiling with every name disabled — the
byte-identity configuration. A fifth divergence, the largest, omitted — the
same omission shape R13 caught for K10 one draft earlier. *Repair:* add it,
and note it cannot be gated off during the refactor.

### C2/F12 — MAJOR — K12 is closed on the high side only; the low side needs
a distinction the VERDICT does not carry

K12's repro list includes `[\d-x]`, `[\w-z]`, `[\d-\w]`. With `classes`
disabled the endpoint caller gets two VERDICTs from the same row it must treat
oppositely: `[[:alpha:]-z]` ("requires module") → must become 150;
`[[:foo:]-z]` (130) → must stay 130. Deciding needs "would PCRE2 accept this
construct here", which §15/§16.3 never put in the returned value. *Repair:*
VERDICT payload = (diagnostic, shape, would-PCRE2-accept); add the low-side
disabled worked example.

### C2/F13 — MAJOR — D33 §4/§6 are reversed with no amendment banner

(The report's other half — "D34 does not exist" — was true of the tree the
critic read; D34 was appended later the same session. Banner half stands:)
D33's revisit clause has fired, yet D33 carries no amendment block, unlike D32
which points at D33. A decision-log-only reader still gets

*Repair:* amendment banner at D33's head naming §4 and §6 as falsified, with

### C2/F14 — MINOR/MAJOR — `want`×`may` legality unstated

Gate idempotence (floor?), VERDICT without MAY_DIAGNOSE cannot deliver its
answer, RESULT's assumed full `may`. *Repair:* gate floors at VERDICT;
WANT_VERDICT requires MAY_DIAGNOSE; WANT_RESULT requires all three.

### C2/F15 — MINOR — WANT_CLAIM's only named consumer was deleted by §16

C1's proposal annotated CLAIM with "(the endpoint rule)"; §16 replaced that
consumer with full evaluation; §15 names no replacement — the unexercised-
structure defect §4.3 argues against. *Repair:* state arbitration is a

### C2/F16 — MINOR — "leave the outcome vocabulary entirely" is false on the
only path that exists today

Disabled lexical rows return `EXT_TERMINATED` — a member of the vocabulary
they left, and their only path until quoting/comments land. *Repair:* "leave
the PRODUCING outcomes; their only outcome is EXT_TERMINATED while disabled."

### C2/F17 — MINOR — "disposition" now means three things, and the collision
lands in §17.3's hand-off to an author denied this document

Shipped `RegDiag`, the roadmap column, and §14.4's class-position column.
*Repair:* rename §14.4's ("class-position expectation"); spell invariant 4
without the word.

### C2 negative results (seams that HOLD)

## C3 — controls, sabotage-liveness, the K10 shape (17 findings: 6 CRITICAL,
9 MAJOR, 2 MINOR)

C3's headline: "Part II's control surface is worse than Part I's §8, not
better — because it is smaller. §17.3 hands the D27 author nine one-line
invariants in place of ten checks, and the shrinkage happened by dropping the
population, scope and oracle from each. Six of the nine cannot fail in the
direction the design's own gaps produce."

### C3/F1 — CRITICAL — invariant 2's scope excludes the only failure
direction the design can produce

"Equals the real parse's count on every pattern that COMPILES" — but an
UNDERCOUNT makes the pattern not compile (err 115), so it exits the scope;
only overcounts are in scope. Undercount is exactly what §12.2's incomplete
extent list produces. The scope predicate is the field under validation
(K10/check-4 shape). Second defect: both sides of the equality come from ONE
extent implementation ("the lexer in count mode"), so an extent bug is shared
and invisible — K4's byte-identity limit re-committed. *Repair:* compare
against LIBPCRE2's capture count on generated patterns, compiling or not;
print the population.

### C3/F2 — CRITICAL — §12.2's "exactly" list is incomplete by five
families, two of them RD_NEVER; "backrefs can land alone" is FALSE

Measured: the count-scan also needs named-group capture (`\1(?<n>a)`
COMPILES), lookaround/atomic bodies (`\1(?=(a))` COMPILES vs `\1(?=a)` 115),
conditional bodies (`\1(?(DEFINE)(a))` COMPILES), verb name args hiding `(`
(`\1(*MARK:(x)` 115 / `\1(*MARK:(x)(a)` COMPILES), callout string args
(`\1(?C{(})` 115). So the always-live machinery must classify EVERY
`(`-initiated form as capturing or not, including delimiter scans for two
RD_NEVER families. "Captures / does not capture" is a new hand-written
per-row fact with no column, no home, no check — and invariant 2 (F1) cannot
see it wrong. *Repair:* a per-row `captures` column, checked against
libpcre2's capture count per row syntax.

### C3/F3 — CRITICAL — §15's FIX-2 promise fails in the shipping
configuration

"Parse the body with the real parser" — the real parser has GATED producers.
Measured: `(a)(?(1)\d|y|z)` is PCRE2 err 127 (permanent), but pcrec's parser
hits the gated `classes` row first and answers "requires module 'classes'" —
an over-promise for a permanently-invalid pattern, in the all-disabled
configuration the refactor ships in. (Negative result attached: body-first
error precedence is right — `(a)(?(1)x|y|[z)` 106, `…|a**)` 109 — the defect
is confined to gated producers inside the parse.) *Repair:* count branches
with the always-live count-scan instead — which also removes
MAY_ALLOCATE|MAY_RECURSE from the only case that motivated them.

### C3/F4 — CRITICAL — §13.3 and §14.3 each reintroduce what the other fixes

Three lexical rows, three class-position behaviours, all NULL ports:
`[\Qa-z\E]` — \Q ACTIVE in class; `[a\E]` — \E active (no-op); `[a(?#x)]+`
matches "(#x)" — (?# is NOT a comment in a class, the bytes are literal
members. So NULL means three things again (R13 C5/F4 restored), and the
tokenizer's comment mode must know whether it is inside a class. *Repair:*
lexical rows carry explicit class ports (\Q/\E active, (?# literal-fallback);
LEXICAL describes only the AST-side locus.

### C3/F5 — CRITICAL — the two-byte short-circuit is refuted by Part I's own
§6 table

`[0-[:]`, `[0-[:a]`, `[0-[:digit]`, `[0-[.a]`, `[0-[=a]` all COMPILE; the
two-byte test answers 150 for all five. The deviating cell's real condition
is "a class-bracket pair opens AND CLOSES" — an unbounded scan, i.e.
`pcrec_ext_class_pair_opens` (21,396/0), which §16.3 proposes deleting.
(Same finding as C2/F2, reached independently.) *Repair:* the surviving
predicate implements the cell; strike the deletion.

### C3/F6 — CRITICAL — quantifiability is a third, unmodelled axis; §13.2's
totality claim is false

At atom position constructs partition into THREE quantifier classes:
transparent (`(?#c)`, `\Qz\E`, `\E`), repeatable (`(?=x)`, `(?:x)`, `(?>x)`,
`(?R)`…), and NON-repeatable — err 109: `\b \B \A \Z \z \G \K`, the modifier
family `(?i)`, `(?C1)`, `(*FAIL)`, `(*MARK:z)`. No §4.4 outcome encodes "not
a quantifier target"; a non-repeatable construct returning EXT_NODE lets
`try_quant` compile `a\b*` / `a(?i)*` / `a(*FAIL)*`, which PCRE2 rejects —
R13's `\Qab\E*` mechanism on 22+ rows, declared closed by removing three.
Not derivable from any existing column ((?=x) is an assertion and repeatable;
\b is an assertion and not). *Repair:* per-row `quantifiable` fact, checked
by sweeping `a<syntax>*` over all 100 rows against libpcre2.

### C3/F7 — MAJOR — §12's "structural property" is the same absence-guard
§5.4 was corrected for

"The code that could break it does not exist" names no mechanism; the
one-line sabotage (`if (!enabled) return NOT_MINE` in a recogniser) is
undetectable — invariant 7's population is empty at landing (F10). Third
instance of the shape the document diagnoses. *Repair:* mechanical —
recognisers/extent scans in translation units that do not link the
enabled-set symbol, checked by `nm`, sabotage = an added reference.

### C3/F8 — MAJOR — §14.4's check compares pcrec against pcrec

"Through the real dispatch" is drift detection; K13 survives it (the author
who believes the wrong module writes the column, the dispatch agrees). Only
§17.3.4's "matches libpcre2" wording has an external side. *Repair:* delete
"through the real dispatch" as the primary; column vs libpcre2 probe is the
check, dispatch comparison secondary.

### C3/F9 — MAJOR — the disposition check's real population is 44, not 100

41 esc + 3 class-bracket rows can reach the class doorway; 55 group + 1 verb
cannot (`(` is a literal in a class — `[a(?#x)]+` matches "(#x)"). For 56
rows the column value is §4.4's "invented value nothing reads" defect. Within
the 44, one syntax string samples one point (`[\cX]` never exercises
`[0-\c]` → 108 / `[\c-z]` → COMPILES, where `\c` eats the delimiter).
*Repair:* print real/printed populations per bucket with a ratcheting floor;

### C3/F10 — MAJOR — invariants 7 and 9 have empty populations at landing

Zero of 16 features can be enabled at landing; C4/F4's prescribed remedy
(whole-set variation, per-name coverage count, ratcheting floor) did not
travel into §17.3. Invariant 7's scope ("whose validity PCRE2 decides") is a

### C3/F11 — MAJOR — invariant 3 is a tag checked against fields written
beside it

Both sides hand-written on one row; a row that SHOULD be lexical but is not
tagged is out of scope (check-4 shape). Negative result attached: the LEXICAL

*Repair:* the criterion becomes the invariant — "a row is LEXICAL iff

### C3/F12 — MAJOR — §16.3's sweep is sized, not designed; its alphabet
excludes what §16.2 exempts; and the curated 33 already miss asymmetries

No owner, no stop rule, no per-cell floor ("tens of thousands, four cells" is

### C3/F13 — MAJOR — §14.2's digit rule is itself refuted by the bucket it

At 0 groups: `\81 \90 \80 \99 \89 \801` are all ERR 115, not octal. A digit

method failure §14.2 exists to correct; invariant 5 passes on an
implementation that reads `\81` as octal.

### C3/F14 — MAJOR — §17.3 is addressed to a reader forbidden to resolve its

Every invariant terminates in a citation into the document the D27 author is

### C3/F15 — MAJOR — §17.1's divergence list is a hand-maintained manifest

Nothing distinguishes "planned divergence" from "regression found during the
refactor", and the list has been wrong twice in one document's life (K10

### C3/F16 — MINOR — §13.2 claims impossibility where it measured cost

The EXT_NODE reading miscompiles (measured); the sixth-outcome reading was
rejected on an unmeasured cost over an uncounted consumer set. "Cannot" is
not what was established — and it licenses §17.3 to hand over no obligation

### C3/F17 — MINOR — §15's want table mislabels its caller

WANT_CLAIM annotated "(the endpoint rule)" — stale after §16 made the

## C1 — adversarial measurement (9 findings: 4 CRITICAL, 4 MAJOR, 1 bounded
NEGATIVE; ~5,400 probes)

C1's headline: "Part II's two central factual claims are both false. §16.2's
'exactly ONE deviating cell' is refuted by a cell printed in §16.1's own
table, and §14.2's atom-side digit rule predicts a compile where libpcre2
raises 115. Both were measured on buckets that could not falsify them — the
same failure R13 recorded twice."

### C1/F1 — CRITICAL — §16.2's "exactly ONE deviating cell" is false; the
second cell is in §16.1's own table

`[:<:]`/`[:>:]` are word-boundary assertions legal ONLY as a class's entire
content: `[[:<:]]` compiles (63/256 single-byte subjects match — the word
chars); `[[:<:]x]`, `[x[:<:]]`, `[[:<:]-z]`, `[[:<:]-\d]` are all 130;
`[0-[:<:]]` is 150 (high side: pair-open wins). "Evaluated anywhere in the
class" `[:<:]` is NOT an error, so §16.2 predicts a range/150; libpcre2 says
130 — and `[[:<:]-z] 130` sits in §16.1's low-side column, read as confirming
the rule. Generated differential: 71 items × 71 = 5,041 (low, high) pairs
with verdict AND shape taken from libpcre2 (predictor shares no source with
the predicted): **71 disagreements, all with `[:<:]` low. Every other
doorway×side×shape combination agrees.** The repo already ships the correct
answer (`pcre2_compliance.md:241`, R9/C3-4) — the design contradicted a fact
the project had measured, written down and shipped (the two-homes-one-wrong
shape). Also: `at_content_start` alone is insufficient — `[[:<:]x]` IS at
content start and is 130; the predicate needs content-start AND class-end.

### C1/F2 — CRITICAL — the digit rule predicts the wrong verdict for every
multi-digit escape beginning 8 or 9

At 0 groups `\81 \90 \88 \99 \80 \819 \8123` are ALL err 115 — and 8 is not
an octal digit, so the prescribed fallback is not even well defined. The
missing clause: **a decimal number beginning 8 or 9 is always a
backreference, regardless of count.** Count matters within that: `(a)×9 \98`
115, `(a)×98 \98` OK. Plus two behaviours mis-modelled as "fall back by
count": up to three octal digits are re-read (`\19` = `\x01` + literal "9";
`\0123` = `\x0a` + "3"), and overflow is its own error (`\400 \500 \777` err
151 in 8-bit non-UTF; `\3777` = `\377` + "7"). (Same core finding as C3/F13,
independently, with the octal re-read and 151 added.)

### C1/F3 — CRITICAL — §12.1's extent list is missing at least four member
families; "no duplicate branch scanner" is false

(Same territory as C3/F2, measured wider.) The count-scan must recognise the
ENTIRE `(?` sub-grammar — `\1(?<n>a)` OK vs `\1(?<=a)` 115 needs the
`(?<n>` / `(?<=` split (features `named-groups`, `lookaround`); verb/callout
bodies hide `(` (`\2(*MARK:()(a)` and `\2(?C"(")(a)` err 115), both RD_NEVER
families. **`(?|` is not an extent but a per-branch maximum needing a
nesting-aware top-level `|` scan**: `\2(?|(a)|(b)|(c))` 115, with `|` hidden
by an escape, a class, a quote, a comment, and unbounded group nesting (all
measured) — exactly the scan R13 used to kill TERMINAL at `(?(` ("that scan
IS the parser"), reappearing inside the count-scan §12.2 promises is not a
second parser. And `(?n)` is a SCOPED mode: `((?n))(a)\1` OK, `(?n)(a)\1`
115, `(?-n)` reverses it.

### C1/F4 — CRITICAL — the "two-byte lexical test" would regress the
K4/FIX-2 boundary

(Same finding as C2/F2 and C3/F5, third independent derivation, widest

    leading '0'                octal always
    single digit 1..9          backref always; valid iff TOTAL >= d
    multi-digit leading 1..7   backref iff RUNNING >= n, else OCTAL
