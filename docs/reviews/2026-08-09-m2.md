## Critic: engine semantics (report received)

### CRITICAL
- **R2-S1** (CONFIRMED — independently reproduced by main session; PCRE2 AND
  python `re` both disagree with pcrec, so not an oracle quirk) Nullable-FIRST
  alternative under a quantifier over-consumes: `(?:|a)*` on "a" → pcrec
  [0,1), PCRE2/python [0,0). PCRE rule: when a repeat iteration matches
  zero-width, the loop terminates — the empty-iteration exit outranks a
  lower-priority consuming branch. pcrec instead lets the consuming branch
  survive. Verified variants: `(?:|a)+`, `(?:()|a)*`, `a(?:|b)*` on "ab"
  ([0,2) vs [0,1)), startpos>0, and — critically — BOTH engines
  (`^(?:|a)*` on ENG_ATTEMPT and bare `(?:|a)*` on ENG_UNANCH).
  Reversed order `(?:a|)*` is CORRECT, isolating the trigger to
  "preferred alternative is nullable". Independent of minimization (critic
  rebuilt with the pass stubbed). Triage: FIX-NOW → FIXED (loop-entry closure).
- **R2-S1a** (DOC, CONFIRMED) docs/known_issues.md K1's scoping claim
  "Confined to the ENG_ATTEMPT engine; assertion-free patterns are
  unaffected" is FALSE — written by the main session, disproved here.
  Triage: FIX-NOW (DOC) → FIXED; K1 entry rewritten and closed.

### Main-session analysis (root cause shared with K1)
Both K1 and R2-S1 are the same defect: `src/ir/dfa.c` `clo_visit` cuts the
ε-path that re-enters an already-`seen` loop-entry SPLIT, so the
empty-iteration path never reaches the loop EXIT/ACCEPT and therefore never
takes its rightful (higher) priority over lower-priority consuming branches.
Candidate single fix: mark star-loop entry states in the NFA and, on ε
re-entry during closure, follow the loop's EXIT edge once (an empty iteration
ends the loop, per PCRE) instead of cutting. That places ACCEPT at exactly
the right priority position and should resolve K1 and R2-S1 together.

### Main-session finding while validating the fix (NEW, not from a critic)
- **R2-M1** (CONFIRMED, pre-existing — current repo binary reproduces it):
  lazy bounded repeats pick the wrong span when the body contains an
  alternation. `(?:ab|a){0,2}?b` on "abab" → pcrec [0,2), PCRE2 AND python
  [0,4). Greedy forms agree; only lazy diverges. **Cause:** `{m,n}` was
  compiled as CHAINED optionals (`X?X?`), which accepts the same language as
  PCRE's NESTED form (`(X(X)?)?`) but orders backtrack preference
  differently — a later copy's alternation choice wrongly outranks an earlier
  copy's. This falsifies the "language-equivalent ... for span-only matching"
  claim in src/ir/nfa.c AND the R1 semantics critic's "probed and held" result
  on that exact claim (it targeted the encoding but its subjects never hit the
  lazy+alternation shape). Triage: FIX-NOW → FIXED (nested {m,n} encoding).

### Fix status: APPLIED (2026-08-09, after all reporting critics finished)
A single closure change (mark star-loop entries; on ε re-entry follow the loop
EXIT once — PCRE's empty-iteration rule) fixes **R2-S1 and K1 together**.
A second change (nested `{m,n}` encoding, built innermost-first and
iteratively so depth cannot overflow the stack) fixes **R2-M1**. Verified in
the scratchpad build: suite 463/463 green, K1's tests/known_fail cases now
PASS, a 4,050-pair nullable/loop differential went 1 → 0 divergences, and the
committed fuzzer is clean on seeds 1/2/3/5/7. Now applied to the repo and
re-verified there: 479 harness + 41 CLI cases, 471/471 oracle, seeds
1/2/3/5/7/11 clean, bench budgets pass.

### Probed-and-held (~5,865 oracle pairs + 19,530 min-vs-no-min comparisons)
Reverse-scan start-finding (912 pairs incl. length-differing alternations
sharing an end — the D7 correctness claim survives, with an analytic argument
as well as empirics); skip-state self-loops fwd+rev (1,564 pairs incl.
20 KB single-class runs, accepting-and-self-looping states, newline

## Critic: benchmark methodology (report received) — the published claims were overstated

### CRITICAL
- **R2-B1** (CONFIRMED) The "faster on 7 of 8 comparable cases, loses only (f)"
  claim MISCLASSIFIES case (g). Its published ratio is 0.944 — a LOSS — and
  across 7 same-box measurements the ratio ranged 0.820–1.113 (mean 0.984,
  sign flips in 4/7). Corrected claim: wins clearly on 6 (a,b,c,d,e,h), loses
  clearly on 1 (f), statistical tie on 1 (g). Triage: FIX-NOW (DOC) → corrected
  in journal + results snapshot.
- **R2-B2** (CONFIRMED) The headline "61 ns vs 76 ns" latency pair is a single
  low-precision sample: across 7 runs the ratio ranged 1.013–1.982 and pcrec's
  own ns/call swung 40.8–61.1 on identical code/hardware. DIRECTION is robust
  (pcrec wins all 7 runs, and across an 8 B–500 KB sweep); the precise numbers
  are not. Triage: FIX-NOW (DOC) → reframed as "~1.0–2.0x lower per-call
  latency".
- **R2-B3** (CONFIRMED) No CPU pinning or governor control despite `taskset`
  and `chrt` being installed; box runs `schedutil` with turbo enabled, and
  per-measurement wall times (15–160 ms) sit inside a frequency-ramp window.
  pcrec is also always measured FIRST in every case. Triage: PLAN → M2.9.

### MAJOR
- **R2-B4** (CONFIRMED) Budget margins are 9x–300,000x loose; none would catch
  a 2x regression. Case (c)'s "throughput" budget is meaningless (its subject
  plants a match near the start, so it measures early exit). The linearity
  check is computed from 46 µs/337 µs absolute times, below the script's own
  anti-blowup floor — currently a timer artifact. Triage: PLAN → M2.9.
- **R2-B5** (CONFIRMED NEGATIVE — reported honestly) The "we cheat by not
  filling an ovector" hypothesis is FALSE: ovecsize 1 vs 16 changed PCRE2

- **R2-B6** (CONFIRMED) Case-insensitivity is a real undisclosed feature gap:
  pcrec's only option (class expansion) loses 10.9x to PCRE2-JIT's native

- **R2-B7** (CONFIRMED) Missing capture support is never surfaced near the

- **R2-B8** (CONFIRMED) The "13x email class" highlight drops its own

### MINOR

## Critic: architecture / M3 readiness (report received)

### FATAL-FLAW
- **R2-A1** (MEASURED) K1's documented scope is false — the same bug is live in
  ENG_UNANCH. Five assertion-free repros vs the PCRE2 oracle. Independently
  matches the semantics critic's R2-S1. Triage: FIX-NOW → FIXED (see below).
- **R2-A2** (MEASURED, and this is R1's A-2 "declared fixed but isn't")
  Any `$` without full `^` anchoring routes the whole pattern to ENG_ATTEMPT,
  which has ZERO scan-avoidance — no prefilter, no skip states — and restarts
  per start position. Measured: `[0-9]+ .*ERROR.*` 1072.6 MB/s vs the same
  pattern with a trailing `$` 74.8 MB/s (14.3x slower, same verdict); and
  `a*b$` over all-'a' reproduces textbook O(n²) (main session confirmed
  independently: 0.199 s / 0.738 s / 2.886 s at 20/40/80 KB, while `a*b` and
  `^a*b$` stay flat). `$`-without-`^` is a large, ordinary slice of real
  patterns. plan.md M2.0 and the R1 review both state A-2 is closed without
  this qualification. Triage: FIX-NOW (DOC, honesty) + PLAN → M2.7.

### MAJOR-RISK
- **R2-A3** (ARGUED, code-traced) D7's "same shape M3 streaming needs" holds
  only for END-finding. The reverse pass rescans backward through raw bytes a
  stream may no longer hold, unbounded for `.*` shapes. M3.0 is a zero-content
  placeholder and does not name start-finding as an open problem. Triage:
  PLAN → M3.0 expanded with an explicit design gate.
- **R2-A4** (MEASURED) The trie-deferral rationale is right at the DFA level
  (240-alternative flat vs hand-factored: identical 2268-entry table) and WRONG
  at the level it was deferred for: compile time is superlinear (200→25.6 ms,
  1000→239 ms) and **at 2000 alternatives pcrec HARD-FAILS** ("NFA exceeds
  20000 states"). 2000–3600-term keyword lists are ordinary. Hand-factored trie
  of the full 3600-word space compiles in 5 ms. Triage: PLAN → M2.8 (NFA-level
  trie), and DOC the current ceiling.
- **R2-A5** (MEASURED) Case (f) diagnosed: NOT cache footprint (a 144-byte
  table gets identical throughput to the 4.6 KB one) and NOT branch
  misprediction (periodic vs random data identical). It's the serial
  load-to-load dependency chain with no skip-eligible state. ALSO code-verified
  that D7's promised "computed-goto vs table arbitration for small DFAs" was
  never built — `emit_unanchored` is unconditionally table-driven, yet M2.3 is
  marked completed. Triage: PLAN → M2.10 + DOC correction to D7.
- **R2-A6** Design-debt gaps: nothing owns which machine becomes M4's capture
  prefilter now that D7 forked the engines; nothing owns the engine
  unification D7 itself promises; DD-4 (\G) doesn't note that
  `nfa_wrap_unanchored` bakes in the self-loop with no toggle. Triage: PLAN.

### GAP
- **R2-A7** APPROACH §6 promises `--emit-ir`/`--emit-dot`; neither exists.
  Triage: DOC (mark aspirational) + PLAN.

## Reflection

1. **The panel found a bug the previous panel had explicitly cleared.** R1's
   semantics critic targeted the `{m,n}` chained-optionals claim and reported
   it probed-and-held; R2-M1 shows it was wrong for lazy repeats with an
   alternation body. A "probed and held" result is evidence about the probes,
   not a proof — and comfortable claims deserve re-attack each round, which is
   the argument for repeating the gate rather than trusting the last one.
2. **My own scoping claim was the most expensive error.** K1's "confined to
   ENG_ATTEMPT; assertion-free patterns are unaffected" was written by me from
   a plausible-sounding root-cause story I never tested against the other
   engine. Two independent critics falsified it. Scope claims about a bug are
   themselves claims requiring evidence.
3. **"Fixed" needs a population, not a checkmark.** R1's A-2 was marked closed;
   it is closed for assertion-free patterns and untouched for `$`-bearing ones,
   which is a large ordinary slice. Milestone completion should record WHICH
   inputs a fix covers.
4. **Publishing numbers invites the most useful scrutiny.** The benchmark
   critic corrected a headline I wrote (7-of-8 → 6 wins, 1 tie, 1 loss),
   showed the latency pair carried false precision, and retired its own leading
   fairness hypothesis when the data disagreed. It also audited its own
   contamination unprompted. That is the standard the project should hold.
5. **The unreported lens is a real gap, not a formality.** The process critic
   never delivered, and the two defects I stumbled into (fuzzer
   non-determinism, false-divergence bucketing) were squarely in its territory
   — including one where the tool's own documented guarantee was false.
   Tooling that produces the project's headline claims needs the same
   adversarial treatment as the compiler. Carried to R3.
