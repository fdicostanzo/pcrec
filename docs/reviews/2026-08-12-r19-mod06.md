   sweep-template lesson's fourth recurrence — K15 was the length axis,

   representative cells (`\p{!}`, `\p{9}`, and the has_eq pair below).
   LINKED PAIR recorded in K16: the differential must not gain
   malformed-body generation until the fix.
2. **Tier 3, NOTED, no action (D26):** empty-name bodies (`\p{ }`,

## Reflection

The milestone's central bet — two-phase briefs and measure-first — held:
zero tier-1 divergences, and the one substantive engine finding is a
category/offset cell in a space where everything refuses. But the panel
earned its cost twice over on the pattern-level findings: the S27 lesson
and the sweep-template lesson EACH recurred inside the very milestone
that cited them, which says the lessons transfer by checklist, not by
osmosis — the closing checklist should now literally include "sweep the
axis your generator cannot produce" and "grep for message-only pins in
the surface you touched". Mech's first UNDETECTED rows (S33/S34) were
the framework working as designed, and both root causes (a misread of
the landed design's message taxonomy; a control sharing a source with
its subject) were shapes this project has named before.
