## Checks critic — "what would let the MOD-0.3 checks pass while the production is wrong?"

**F1 (CONFIRMED, measured, FIXED same-session — the panel's sharpest
finding).** Ten of the fourteen POSIX names had NO non-libpcre2 pin, and
the coverage that LOOKED like it touched `lower` was structurally blind:
the only lower-adjacent corpus blocks were `flags i` blocks, and the -i
fold makes `[[:lower:]]` and `[[:upper:]]` produce the IDENTICAL set, so a
`lower`/`upper` bitmap swap in mod_classes.c's name map changes nothing
they can see. The critic supplied the exact sabotage; MEASURED: with the
swap live, PC-4 fails with 1,151 disagreements — and the corpus, the
designated no-libpcre2 net, stayed 43/43. On a stranger's clone (PC-4
skips loudly), `make test` was fully green while `[[:lower:]]` matched
uppercase. FIXED: ten discriminating non-caseless blocks (one per unpinned
name; `lower`/`upper` chosen to kill exactly this swap), validated in the
failing direction — the swap now fails the corpus alone at the two new
blocks — and pristine 53/53 with the python oracle green. The general
lesson joins the catalogue: **a caseless pin is not a pin of the two sets
it folds together.**

**Refuted-by-own-investigation (recorded so it is not re-run):** the
literal-fallback scalar tie in check_class_ports looked self-referential,
but tests/base/class_escape_fallbacks.rxt pins all four rows against 41
libpcre2-measured cells — covered.

**PROBED-AND-HELD (checks lens):** step 4 of the endpoint rule is one

## Engine critic — "find a reachable input where pcrec disagrees with PCRE2 or its own contract"

**F1 (CONFIRMED, measured, FIXED same-session — a live tier-2 divergence
on the milestone's own producer).** `\N{2,3}` was refused as the
unsupported `\N{name}` construct where PCRE2 parses the brace as a
QUANTIFIER first and compiles it as bare `\N` repeated. The `\N{` row's
tail recogniser fired on the literal `{` unconditionally; the row predates
MOD-0.3 (2aca7dd6), but this milestone made bare `\N` produce, which is
what turned one-refusal-among-several into a reachable divergence. The
full boundary was MEASURED before the fix (tests/probes/probe_nbrace.c,
22 cells — richer than the critic's own set, and one of the critic's
cells was corrected by it: `\N{,3}` COMPILES under 10.46, `{,n}`
being `{0,n}` since PCRE2 10.43, consistent with pcrec's own try_quant
which the stale fuzz.py exclusion note misdescribes): quantifier-shaped
bodies including `{2, 3}`/`{ 2}`/`{,3}` fall back to quantified `\N`;
err-104/105 bodies prove the quantifier parser claimed the brace; `{}`,
`{,}`, `{2,3,4}`, `{2x}`, unterminated `{2` stay the name construct.
THE FIX is the recogniser machinery doing what it was built for:
`pcrec_brace_quant_shape` — ONE pure scan in scans.c with TWO load-bearing
callers (try_quant's entry pre-test and the `\N{` row's custom
recogniser, the table's first non-default recogniser), so the two grammars
cannot drift without the whole base-tier corpus noticing. Every measured
cell verified against the fixed binary, `^\N{2,3}$` matched end-to-end
through emitted C, the esc-'N' liveness floor moved 10→9 with the exact
probe text predicted before the run ("{0041}" lost its second answer;
the triple-answer floor untouched), corpus blocks and three reject pins
landed (counts 265→268), registry/reject/spec suites green.

**PROBED-AND-HELD (engine lens, ~400 patterns beyond PC-4's 273, all
differential-matched cell-for-cell against live 10.46):** ranges adjacent
to produced sets (accept and refuse directions); sets doubled and at both
ends; double negation ([^\D], [^[:^alpha:]]); caseless × negation ×
members (7/7 — including one self-correction where the critic misread a
codegen table by hand and the differential said otherwise); \N in

class-open positions; empty-ish classes; the startpos axis (16/16 — PC-4
only drives 0); the gate seam (--features all ≡ classes on classes rows;
multi-module lists clean); 342 combinatorial juxtapositions in both
caseless states.

## Reflection

- The milestone's sharpest findings both came from the panel, both were
  measured within the hour, and both fixes reused machinery the project
  already had: the corpus gained pins where a fold made two sets
  indistinguishable, and the table's first custom recogniser landed
  exactly where D32's design said recognisers were for. A design that
  pays for its generality does so on the first hard case — \N has now
  been that case for rank, for prefix pairs, and for recognisers.
- A caseless pin is not a pin of the two sets it folds together — the
  catalogue's newest sentence, and the second time this project has found
  case-closure hiding a defect from every downstream observer (OS-1's
  fold-the-complement lesson was the first).
- The engine critic self-reported an overreach it had already reverted.
  The brief discipline (scope mandate restated in every spawn) is doing
  its job; keep it.
- Two of three critics' reports contained one wrong cell each ({,3}, and
  a misread codegen table), both corrected by measurement — one by the
  main session's probe, one by the critic's own differential before
  reporting. Verify a critic's cells the way you verify your own: run
  them.
