BOUNDARY LEGEND. A subject's CHARACTER BOUNDARIES are printed with each
block. Under UTF-8 a position p is a boundary iff p == n or s[p] is not
a continuation byte (s[p] & 0xC0) != 0x80 — the same local predicate
`next_pos` and the [K49] advance already implement.

§2  BADUTFOFFSET — is the refusal UNIFORM, or pattern-dependent?
==========================================================================

Subject CE B1 CE B2 (alpha beta): boundaries 0, 2, 4; offsets 1 and 3
are mid-character. If `PCRE2_UTF` answers the same way for every
pattern at 1 and 3, the refusal is a property of the OFFSET, which is
what an O(1) entry guard can reproduce. If it is pattern-dependent, an
entry guard is the wrong shape and the charter's design needs revising.
