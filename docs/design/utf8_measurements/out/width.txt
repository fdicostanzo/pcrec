1. IS A LOOKBEHIND'S LENGTH MEASURED IN CHARACTERS OR IN BYTES?
==========================================================================
  The discriminating cell is a SINGLE branch that is fixed at one
  CHARACTER and variable at one-or-two BYTES. `[a\x{3b1}]` is
  exactly that: 'a' is one byte, U+03B1 is two.
