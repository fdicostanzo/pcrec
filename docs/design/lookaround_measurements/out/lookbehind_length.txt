B2 -- THE PREFERENCE ORDER when more than one back-step could succeed
==============================================================================
The design's capture semantics rest entirely on this. TWO LEVELS, and
they do NOT follow the same rule.

--- level 1: TOP-LEVEL BRANCHES are tried in WRITTEN ORDER ---
pattern                        subject  | libpcre2                       | verdict
(?<=(a)|(aa))c                 'aac'    | (2,3) g:(1,2) _                | branch 1 (shorter, written first) WINS
(?<=(aa)|(a))c                 'aac'    | (2,3) g:(0,2) _                | branch 1 (longer, written first) WINS
(?<=(a)|(aa)|(aaa))c           'aaac'   | (3,4) g:(2,3) _ _              | branch 1 wins over both longer ones
(?<=(aaa)|(aa)|(a))c           'aaac'   | (3,4) g:(0,3) _ _              | branch 1 wins again

--- level 2: WITHIN one branch the STEP-BACK LENGTH is tried LONGEST
    FIRST, and the alternation's own written order does NOT decide it ---
pattern                        subject  | libpcre2                       | verdict
(?<=(a|aa|aaa))c               'aaac'   | (3,4) g:(0,3)                  | longest (3) wins though written LAST
(?<=(aaa|aa|a))c               'aaac'   | (3,4) g:(0,3)                  | longest (3) wins, written first
(?<=(a|aa))c                   'aac'    | (2,3) g:(0,2)                  | longest (2) wins though written last
(?<=(x|aa|a))c                 'aac'    | (2,3) g:(0,2)                  | longest VIABLE (2) wins; 3 is not viable
(?<=(a|ba))c                   'bac'    | (2,3) g:(0,2)                  | longest (2) wins though written last
(?<=(a|ba))c                   'xac'    | (2,3) g:(1,2)                  | only length 1 is viable there
(?<=(a{1,3}))c                 'aaac'   | (3,4) g:(0,3)                  | a bounded quantifier: longest first
(?<=(a|aa)(b|bb))c             'abbc'   | (3,4) g:(0,1) (1,3)            | composite: the SUM is what varies

--- the LOOKAHEAD's own preference, for comparison: ordinary
    leftmost-first alternation, since there is no length to choose ---
(?=(a|ab))ab                   'ab'     | (0,2) g:(0,1)                 
(?=(ab|a))ab                   'ab'     | (0,2) g:(0,2)                 
(?=(a|aa))aa                   'aa'     | (0,2) g:(0,1)                 
(?=(aa|a))aa                   'aa'     | (0,2) g:(0,2)

B3 -- THE max_varlookbehind CAP: a property of the COMPILE CONTEXT
==============================================================================
Bisected, not quoted. `(?<=a{1,N})x` needs a back-step of N.
DEFAULT CAP (bisected): largest accepted variable back-step = 255
  `(?<=a{1,255})x` -> ok
  `(?<=a{1,256})x` -> err 200 (branch too long in variable-length lookbehind assertion)

# the SAME pattern under an EXPLICIT cap, proving the cap is contextual
pattern                | cap          | libpcre2
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=0        | err 200 (branch too long in variable-length lookbehind assertion)
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=1        | err 200 (branch too long in variable-length lookbehind assertion)
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=2        | err 200 (branch too long in variable-length lookbehind assertion)
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=3        | err 200 (branch too long in variable-length lookbehind assertion)
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=4        | ok
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=5        | ok
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=16       | ok
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=255      | ok
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=256      | ok
(?<=a{1,4})x           | cap=65535    | ok

# a FIXED-length lookbehind is NOT subject to the cap at all:
(?<=a{10})x-as-literal | cap=1        | ok
(?<=a{10})x-as-literal | cap=2        | ok
# and the FIXED bound that does exist -- bisect a plain literal body:
  largest accepted FIXED back-step = 32759; 32760 -> err 120 (regular expression is too large)
