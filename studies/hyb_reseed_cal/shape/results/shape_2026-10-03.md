# Shape probes, 2026-10-03 (lane xcalldes)

SCRATCH TIER, DIRECTIONAL ONLY. Apple M1, gcc-16 `-O2`, compiler at main
c231ffc1 (abi 55), `--features all`, `-e byte` unless noted. Load average 2-5
throughout (other lanes running). Each line: `rr.sh`, 6 launches of each
binary round-robin, each launch the median of 7 passes; the cell is the
median of the 6 launch medians, in ns summed over the subject list (short
search: per call, summed over the 42 subjects; find-all: per pass, summed
over the listed subjects). `a=[..]`/`d=[..]` are the launch min..max.
"deny" is always the `-fno-hyb-reseed` artifact (the `fixed` row, byte for
byte today's pre-reseed retry). Answers (a hash over every call's result)
were identical on every line.

Subjects: `subj/` = syntax@0.1's 42 short subjects, `thr/` = its t-64k and
t-256k, both regenerated by `regen_bench_subjects.py` and sha256-verified
against the bench manifests (45/45). `cal/` = `../subjects.py`.
